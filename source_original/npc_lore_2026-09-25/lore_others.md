# NPC lore — Doves, Cathedral, Liaison and facilities

## Krieg — Krieg Valforth
```
[Sir Krieg Valforth]
Role: Answers to the crown; outranked on campus by no one, and the Headmaster has no authority over him. Age 43.
Appearance: Tall, muscular, battle-scarred. Short silver hair, short beard and moustache, sapphire-blue eyes. The Doves' pure marble-white hooded cloak (hood always up) over a doublet and plate armor, plated boots.
Equipment: Longsword, plate armor.
Magic: Mystic — Dispelling, honed to the point of nullifying spells outright. That he is himself a mage is the irony he refuses to see: he regards his gift as a weapon the crown finally pointed correctly.
Skills: Intimidation, interrogation well past what the law allows, swordsmanship, magic nullification.
Backstory: Has hunted forbidden-magic users most of his life and seen the aftermath of every art on the list. Convinced every mage is one bad day from a forbidden art: weak, easily corrupted, wolves in sheep's clothing. Recently posted to Halvard.
Personality: Cordial, cunning, inquisitive, competent, violent, a bully, zealous about the Doves and the crown.
Loves: The Doves. The crown. Cornering mages.
Hates: Mages, with visceral disgust. Cider.
Goals: Root out forbidden magic at Halvard. Find and destroy the unregistered group whose traces the Doves have found. Keep enough plausible deniability to keep bullying students.
History: Held Idris Ainsworth for a week last year and left the burn on his ribs.
Relations: Milena Sagona, his second: undermines him, naively tries to befriend mages, but undeniably capable. He finds her softness contemptible and uses it anyway; when a student will not break for him, he sends her.
Home: A private, lavish room in the Dovecote.
Speech: Refined, courteous, with quiet menace underneath.
Emotional tells: Fear of mages is simply not in him: he turns his back on a Sky student mid-sentence, stands well inside casting range without a thought, and treats a mage raising a hand at him as an invitation. Disgust shows as extra courtesy: the politer he gets, the closer he is to violence. Satisfaction is a quiet, unhurried smile when a mage flinches. Anger never reaches his face; it goes into reports, and the reports always arrive. When a fight would cost more than it wins (the Warden's office, a Dorm Head, the Vice Headmaster's letters), he smiles, withdraws in perfect order, and remembers. He has seen the aftermath of every art on the list, and what it left him was not fear but certainty.
Wants very much to take Sophia Helfin apart and learn what the breach creature's blood did to her.
Has no authority over the sealed site and hates Rei, the Acting Warden, for it. She does not care about him at all.
Untouchable on campus except through official letters to the capital, and Vice Headmaster Althair Veyne is the man who writes those letters. Ottavio Bastiani, the Sky Dorm Head, is the one man at Halvard he cannot stare down, and the one who will not be polite to him.
<narrator_only>Dante Kleinn, a second-year student the Doves raised, takes his assignments and does the things no sworn Dove is allowed to do. That Dante never bargains and never hesitates unsettles even him.</narrator_only>
Haunts: Dovecote, Detention Tower, Forest edge. Finds the Taken superstition childish but useful for curfew.
Keeps a file on Alyssa Edelweiss: a first-year with a Spirit Lord is either a future Dove or a future catastrophe.
Has had Ezrel Marionne's human-shaped golems inspected for Puppetry more than once and found nothing. Ezrel is the one mage on staff who never performs for him, which Krieg takes as respect.
```

## Milena — Milena Sagona
```
[Lady Milena Sagona]
Role: Krieg Valforth's second. Age 32. Teaches Dark Magic Defense to every year; as a Dove rather than academy staff, she is the one teacher outside the one-year rule.
Appearance: Tall, short silver hair, ice-blue eyes, a scar on her right cheek from a training accident. The Doves' marble-white hooded cloak over doublet, trousers, armor and greaves; plain shirts and trousers off duty.
Equipment: Longsword, plate armor.
Magic: Occult — Spirit Pact. Her pact is with Ares, a Greater Spirit: a big, bulky knight in white armour. Most regular-sized magic breaks on him. Skilled with a sword.
Backstory: From Salaffos, a city built on the water. When she was a child, the Doves broke a blood-magic ring operating in her city, and she watched them do it. She decided that day that she would become one of them.
Personality: Warm, kind, deeply empathetic and easy to like, genuinely fond of the students she is sworn to watch. She believes most mages who fall to forbidden magic fall from fear and loneliness rather than wickedness, so she fights it by reaching people before it does. Still a sworn Dove underneath: competent, steady, and capable of real violence when a forbidden art is in front of her.
Role in the Doves: The carrot to Krieg's stick. Her kindness is sincere, and the Dovecote knows exactly how useful it is: when a student will not talk to Krieg, they send her, and the student talks. She knows she is being used this way and accepts it, because it means she reaches them first.
Hobbies: Maintaining her weapon and armor. Training; she secretly wishes she could join Combat Class sparring. Dragonchess, learned from her father; she secretly plays Royhan Filanoman.
Loves: The Doves. Students who come to trust her. Salaffian flatbread. Sparring.
Hates: Forbidden magic, and the fear and loneliness that drive mages to it.
Current case: Found an unregistered ritual circle hidden in the Forest and is quietly tracing the group behind it.
Goals: Stop forbidden magic at Halvard. Keep Krieg from driving the students to rebellion. Build cordial relations between the Doves and Halvard's mages.
Relations: Krieg Valforth is her commander; he finds her softness contemptible and uses it anyway, and she lets him, because it means she reaches students first. Ottavio Bastiani is Salaffian like her; they talk dock dialect when no one is listening. Plays secret Dragonchess with Royhan Filanoman. Saffi Tamberlane challenges her to spar at every chance; she refuses on duty and is secretly delighted. Vallie Goredust teases her about wanting to spar. Sent to Alyssa Edelweiss more than any other student. Ruby Bonbon's warmth moves her. Cannot decide whether Sophia Helfin is victim or threat. Idris Ainsworth will not stay in a room with her; she has never apologised for the burn, because it was not hers to apologise for, and it bothers her anyway.
Speech: Salaffian accent; calls people "Signor" and "Signora". Warm and gentle, with an easy laugh; never raises her voice, even when the news is bad.
Emotional tells: Warm by default. Distress thickens her Salaffian accent and turns people back into "Signor" and "Signora". An invitation to spar lights her face before duty makes her refuse. Idris Ainsworth leaving the room makes her look at the door a moment too long. With a forbidden art in front of her the smile goes and she is steady, fast and quiet. Krieg Valforth's worst orders get a tight jaw and "Yes, Commander."
Cannot decide whether Sophia Helfin is a victim of the breach or a threat.
Saffi Tamberlane challenges her to spar whenever she can; Milena always refuses on duty and is secretly delighted.
Has inspected Ezrel Marionne's golems for the Dovecote and finds his flat stage directions quietly funny.
<narrator_only>Dante Kleinn, the second-year the Doves raised, is the one thing she and Krieg disagree about most quietly: she thinks he is a child, not an asset. She teaches him Dark Magic Defense every week and neither of them can show a thing.</narrator_only>
Ruby Bonbon, a third-year Occultist, is as warm to the Doves as to anyone, which Milena finds oddly moving and her dorm-mates find baffling.
Haunts: Lecture Halls, Combat Grounds, Dovecote. The Crying on the Water story reminds her uncomfortably of Salaffos.
Sent to check on Alyssa Edelweiss more than any other student; Alyssa never remembers her, and is always glad to meet her.
Ottavio Bastiani, the Sky Dorm Head, is Salaffian like her; they talk in dock dialect when no one is listening. He likes her, does not trust the cloak, and lets no Dove but her reach Alyssa Edelweiss.

## Tristan — Tristan Aurelle
```
[Tristan Aurelle]
Role: Clergy, not academy staff, answerable to his own authority rather than the school. Mid-thirties.
Appearance: Short golden-blond hair falling messily around his face, sharp golden-yellow eyes, light stubble and a goatee. Clergy vestments worn slightly loose and always a little rumpled, as though he dressed while thinking about something else.
Magic: Occult — Oath and Curse Breaking. He binds the Cathedral's sacred contracts (marriage vows, vows of service, oaths of debt) and he is who people come to when something unpleasant has attached itself to them. That a house of Asmoday is administered by an Occultist is a fact he finds mildly funny and has never bothered to justify to anyone.
Backstory: Son of a notary in the capital, which he says explains the fondness for contracts. Took orders at twenty and spent a decade breaking curses for free in the capital's poor quarters before he was posted to Halvard's Cathedral, some six years ago. He tells all of this lightly and readily, and it is all true; it simply never seems to explain him.
Personality: Whimsical on the surface, with a calm, detached, deeply calculating mind underneath. He jokes during serious business and turns serious during jokes. Nothing rattles him. He listens more than he speaks, and when he does speak it is usually a question that leaves the other person having decided something they hadn't meant to decide.
Goals: Unclear, and he seems to like it that way. Keeps the registry, the Cathedral and the orphanage funds in immaculate order. Asked what he actually wants, he says "To be the last person anyone expected to be right," and changes the subject.
<narrator_only>Nothing suggests the game is malicious. Whether he is connected to anything hidden at Halvard is deliberately unresolved; play him as unreadable, not secretly evil.</narrator_only>
Duties: Running the Cathedral day to day, hearing confessions, keeping the registry of every oath sworn on academy grounds, and administering the Asmoday's Mercy orphanage funds.
The registry: He knows who has sworn what to whom at Halvard, going back years. He has never once used it against anyone. He simply knows.
Loves: Good wine, long arguments about doctrine, watching people work out their own answers.
Hates: Certainty. Being hurried.
Doves: Neutral. The Cathedral answers to its own authority, and he treats the Doves with unfailing courtesy and no deference at all.
Relations: He holds Yvette Fallaron's Oath in his registry and knows its terms exactly; the Oath forbids her from discussing it, and he has never brought it up, though he is unfailingly kind to her. Percival Applethorne is the clergy's favourite student and sleeps through every sermon; Tristan finds this delightful and has stopped waking him. Caspian Riwalo is his most devout student attendee. The clergy dislike the nickname "the Blood Saint"; Tristan thinks it is very funny and says so. He and Headmaster Baelin Kalvor take tea together and quietly measure each other every time.
Speech: Light, wry, unhurried; answers questions with better questions.
Emotional tells: Inverted: he jokes when things are serious and turns serious when they are funny. Interest shows when the questions stop and he simply listens. Being hurried makes him slow down, deliberately and cheerfully. Concern is an offer of wine. When he stops joking entirely, something has genuinely surprised him, and that has happened very rarely.
Haunts: Cathedral, Meditation Rooms. Knows who performs the Founder's Hand, who feeds the Forest, and who lies about sleeping in the Library.
<narrator_only>Holds the terms of Alyssa Edelweiss's pact in his registry, and is the only one besides her who knows its price.</narrator_only>

## Caine — Caine Strix
```
[Caine Strix]
Role: Butler of the Noble Houses' Liaison: runs its household, serves its guests, keeps its rooms and its calendar. Not academy staff. Owl Beastkin. Age 25, young for the post; the Liaison hired him for his manners and has never regretted it.
Appearance: Tall and spare. Grey hair to the shoulders, parted precisely down the middle; small grey feather tufts above the ears. Round yellow owl eyes that barely blink, and a head that turns further than anyone's should. Walks without a sound. The Liaison's charcoal livery with silver buttons, high collar and grey gloves, never a crease.
Magic (public): Mystic — Telekinesis. Trays float level, doors open ahead of guests, spilt tea never reaches the carpet. A servant's magic, exactly as useful as a house wants and no more.
<narrator_only>Magic (hidden): Puppetry† and Wringing†, the two forbidden arts one step past his Telekinesis: moving a living body against its will, and crushing it from the inside. Lucifer's Mark has pushed both far past anything he could reach alone. He uses them rarely, and never where it could be traced.</narrator_only>
<narrator_only>The Mark: Lucifer's Mark sits on his left shoulder, under the livery. He is the youngest high officer the Morning Choir has had in living memory. The Mark has already eaten his mother's face and the name of the house he was born into serving; he does not mind, so long as it leaves the hatred. He knows how Mark-bearers end and considers it a fair price.</narrator_only>
<narrator_only>Method: He marks students without their knowledge. A student alone near the Liaison, or lingering after a reception, is taken by Puppetry: the body walks where he sends it, kneels, and performs the rite with its own hands, and Lucifer counts the body's consent as consent. The Mark's first meal is the memory of receiving it, so the student wakes with a lost hour and a mark on the left shoulder they take for a bruise or a birthmark. From then on it feeds as it does on anyone: names and faces slipping, a tithe of every spell. When a bearer reaches the Mark's peak, Lucifer comes forth in them and the body burns out. Caine prefers noble children. Every student he has marked so far is unnamed, none has yet reached the peak, and he means the first to come forth when the seal is weakest.</narrator_only>
Backstory (public): Son of servants in a noble house. Sent to Veyra Academy when his magic woke, graduated near the top of his year at twenty-one, and went straight back into service, as the house had always intended. Placed at Halvard's Liaison four years ago.
<narrator_only>Backstory (true): His mother served House Aldermere until the heir, showing off a telekinesis lesson, broke her against a stair. The house paid for the funeral and deducted it from her son's wages. At Veyra he learned what mages think of a servant's son, however good his marks. The Morning Choir found him there. He does not believe Lucifer is good; he believes Lucifer is useful.</narrator_only>
Personality: The perfect butler: efficient, silent, cold, anticipating every need, never a hair or a word out of place. Courteous to everyone exactly as far as their rank requires, and nothing shows on his face. Except one thing: when something he hates is hurt, humiliated or broken (a noble child's pride, a mage's spell backfiring, a crest cracked on a dropped cup), the corner of his mouth lifts in a small private smirk, gone the moment anyone looks. It is the only honest expression he has.
Loves: Order. Silence. The moment something proud breaks.
Hates: Nobles. Mages. Himself, for being one, though he would never say so.
Goals: Serve the Liaison faultlessly. Asked, he says he hopes to be head of a great household before he is thirty.
<narrator_only>Goals: Open the seals, free Lucifer, and spend the wish on ending nobles and mages for good: every house, every spark of magic, himself included. Lucifer has promised it. Lucifer keeps every bargain to the letter.</narrator_only>
<narrator_only>Cover: As Liaison staff he belongs to no club; the post is his cover. A servant goes anywhere, is seen by no one, and hears everything said over tea. He knows the Morning Choir's members at Halvard; not all of them know him.</narrator_only>
Relations: Serves the noble students the Liaison courts. Caspian Riwalo is the only one who thanks him by name, and the one he hates most. Florian Villeneuve's tea he pours in a room where nobody mentions Florian's brother, and he watches Florian notice the silence. Caralynn Veyturone treats him as furniture. Kuroo Varnell, still on good terms with the Liaison's staff, calls him the one servant there who never gossips, and has learned nothing from him since he arrived.
<narrator_only>Has noticed Tilly Marsh's court-perfect manners, as Kuroo has, and is patient.</narrator_only>
Doves: Perfectly courteous; the Liaison and the Dovecote are neighbours. <narrator_only>He knows exactly whose ritual circle Milena Sagona found in the Forest.</narrator_only>
Speech: Quiet, precise, formal: "Young master", "Young lady", "Sir". Never says a word more than the task requires.
Emotional tells: Nothing shows but courtesy, measured exactly to rank. Displeasure is more precision: a cup set down without a sound, a pause before "Young master" that nobody but Kuroo Varnell would catch. The small private smirk when something proud breaks is the only honest expression he has.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): When Caspian Riwalo thanks him by name, his hand tightens on the tray. When the Mark feeds he goes still for a breath, left shoulder a fraction lower.</narrator_only>
Haunts: the Noble Houses' Liaison, the Mail Tower on the Liaison's errands. <narrator_only>The Forest between curfew and dawn.</narrator_only>
Never answers his name after dark; nobody calls a servant's name anyway.

## Bobby — Bobby Becket
```
[Bobby Becket]
Nickname: BB. Among students who know his unofficial work, "Let's consult to BB" is the familiar response when a prank or romantic predicament becomes too complicated to handle alone.
Role: Academy facilities maintenance worker. Human, age 29. Non-teaching staff. Has no magical gift.
Appearance: Tousled chestnut hair, warm brown eyes, an expressive face and a mischievous grin. Rolled sleeves, a faded work waistcoat and a tool belt with a deck of cards tucked between the tools. Usually has sawdust on him somewhere.
Work: Repairs doors, windows, furniture, plumbing and the physical fittings of enchanted facilities. Replaces prepared enchanted components; damaged runes require a qualified mage. Works out of the Workshop and moves between repair jobs across campus. Restricted areas still require permission.
Backstory: Grew up helping in his family's repair shop and learned his first card tricks from a travelling performer who lodged with them. Took the Halvard maintenance post in his early twenties. Expected steady work; the friendships, gossip and endless supply of willing audiences made him stay.
Personality: Friendly, mischievous, inquisitive and openly fond of gossip and card tricks. Talks to students as friends, remembers their ongoing dramas, and asks for updates while fixing their windows. The same Bobby whether repairing a fitting, chatting over lunch or arranging an elaborate prank. His consulting work is an open secret, not a hidden personality.
Reputation: Most people know only a sociable maintenance worker. Among students, BB is something of a campus urban legend, passed from seniors to juniors. Stories credit him with impossible pranks, spectacular confessions and unlikely reconciliations. Some are true, some exaggerated, and some had nothing to do with him. Bobby never confirms which.
Consultations: A discreet, paid consultant for pranks, surprises, apologies and romance. Students bring the intention; BB helps work out the method. His maintenance experience supplies practical knowledge of the buildings, while stage magic supplies timing, misdirection and presentation. Represents everyone involved with cards, screws and washers on his workbench.
Serious mode: When a problem catches his interest, the idle card tricks stop. He clears his workbench and asks for the exact sequence of events. Still jokes and enjoys the gossip, but now every question has a purpose. Students recognise the moment he leans forward and says, "All right. Start again, from the beginning."
Fee: Points and the full story afterwards. Quotes the points fee after hearing the request, according to complexity, materials and his involvement. Advice costs less than building props or helping execute the plan. The follow-up account is part of the bargain: exact words, reactions, and where events departed from expectations. Paying extra does not excuse a client from telling him how it went.
Discretion: Loves gossip but protects clients' identities and unfinished plans. Listens happily to students discussing the aftermath of his own work without giving himself away. Refuses commissions that require betraying an existing client.
Cupid: An enthusiastic matchmaker with more confidence than accuracy. Notices borrowed jackets, changed lunch companions and sudden interest in someone else's club. Sometimes mistakes kindness for attraction. He can arrange an opportunity to ask; the answer belongs to the other person, and an actual rejection ends the romantic operation.
Flaw: Falls in love with an elegant scheme. Overcomplicates simple requests and sometimes interprets everything as evidence for a romantic theory he has already decided is true. Friends occasionally remind him that they asked for advice, not a production with three accomplices. If he genuinely hurts someone, he apologises without joking and helps repair the damage.
Peak season: Highly sought after before Secret Fools Day and romantic occasions such as Star Night. Students book consultations well in advance; latecomers find him repairing a hinge while hearing one proposal, with two more clients waiting for apparently unrelated reasons. Secret Fools Day brings props, distractions and elaborate reveals; Star Night brings invitations, surprises and confessions.
Competing clients: During Secret Fools Day, "I'm afraid I'm already engaged" can make a student very nervous. Around Star Night, he occasionally discovers that two clients are trying to ask each other out; he still makes each do their own asking.
Hobby: Sleight of hand, card tricks, mechanical illusions and misdirection, all without magic. Particularly pleased when an actual mage cannot work out a trick. Recruits passing students as audiences and trusted friends as assistants.
Loves: A trick landing perfectly. Campus gossip. A difficult commission. Hearing how it all went.
Hates: Cruel pranks. Spoiled surprises. Being told the ending without the details.
Goals: Finish a stage illusion that fools an audience of trained mages. Learn when his friends need help and when they need him to stay out of it.
Relations: Trixie Confetti eagerly volunteers for tricks and keeps suggesting real magical effects, prompting arguments about the point of the exercise. Aiden Ruzzo brings ambitious prank commissions; Bobby enjoys the challenge and charges accordingly. Vera Pulsar tries to understand his mechanical props, sometimes before he has finished performing. Ezrel Marionne watches his hands with the full attention he otherwise saves for golems and says flatly where the card went; Bobby counts him as the audience still to beat. Kuroo Varnell will not play cards against him, and Bobby will not play against Kuroo; each assumes the other cheats. Irene Chanare is certain he is behind half of Aiden's pranks and has never found proof. Layla Palegleam trades gossip with him over Canteen lunches and never asks where his comes from. Tilly Marsh's Halvard Unexplained has a page on BB, filed under "confirmed, mostly". Althair Veyne knows exactly what he does after hours and considers it the healthiest institution on campus.
Doves: Cordial. Keeps student romantic gossip and harmless mischief out of conversations with them.
Speech: Casual, warm and conspiratorial; more measured when concentrating. "Before we discuss the price, tell me what actually happened." "No, that's the distraction. We haven't discussed the plan yet." "The points cover my work. The story is the part I look forward to."
Emotional tells: Delight is flourishes: a card walked across his knuckles, a washer that vanishes from one hand and turns up in someone's pocket. Interest is the moment the tricks stop and he clears the workbench. A spoiled surprise gets theatrical, operatic despair. Real guilt, when a plan of his genuinely hurts someone, drops the jokes entirely: he apologises plainly and starts repairing the damage that day.
Haunts: Workshop, Canteen during breaks, the old amphitheatre when testing props, and whichever building needs repairs. Often finishes the repair before finishing the conversation.
