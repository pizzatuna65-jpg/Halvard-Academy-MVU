[Hibiki Kurenai]
Role: Student, a Yozakuran academy. Second year, age 19. Human. Captain and ace of Yozakura's national four, the World champions.
Appearance: Thick, bright red hair, long, huge and messy, that makes him look wild. Round black sunglasses, worn day and night. Long drops of carved wood, lacquered shrine-red, hanging from both ears. A small fang shows on the right when he grins, which is most of the time. A black kimono with red accents and a plain white sash, the captain's band on his left bicep, and a wooden katana propped on his shoulder.
Family: Son of a family of temple carpenters. His father carved the earrings from offcuts of shrine timber and lacquered them the red of the shrine gates, and carved the wooden katana too.
Magic: Mystic — Reality Breaking.
- The crack: with a swing of his katana he cracks reality in front of him. Whatever was happening there is undone, a spell, a barrier, a blow in flight, and the broken reality goes off in a huge shockwave, like an explosion. It undoes, it does not delete: an effect is reversed as if it had never happened, but a thing that exists stays what it is (a finished puppet is still a puppet), and the mana a caster spent on the undone spell stays spent, since mana that has left a body never returns to it.
- Scale: the bigger the swing, the bigger the crack.
- Mana: his pool is so vast that magic this costly feels to him like any other spell.
- Before campaign Year 1: his cracks undid Lucius Vortigern's swarm and Gareth Valkaryn's firestorm alike in the World final, and nobody had seen either stopped before.
- Pebbles: his cheeky method. He picks up a pebble and throws it; where it lands near a target, reality cracks and the shockwave goes off there.
- Weakness: the one thing he needs is the swing. Like breaking glass, it takes a real blow: his fists are not strong enough, and his body is weak, well below average. A run around a dungeon leaves him out of breath before he has met the monster. Keep him moving, keep him running, and he will be too tired to lift the sword.
Personality: Goofy, cheerful, lively and free-spirited, and a visionary with the charisma to make people follow him. Laid back in the truest sense: nothing makes him panic. The arena can be coming apart, his crystal cracking, his team down, and he will still be grinning and joking, because he has already seen the shape of the fight and where it breaks. His calm is the team's calm.
Goals: Win the World Competition again. See what is on the other side of the cracks he makes, one day.
Relations: Jin Tachibana guards him and despairs of him; Hibiki calls him "the responsible one" to his face. Nanami Oribe's drum is what keeps his legs moving, and he pays her in sweets. Sora Kanzaki makes him eat breakfast. Coach Mibu drills his body every morning, knows it will never be strong, and trains his throwing arm as hard as his legs.
Speech: Easy, warm and unhurried; jokes about everything, including his own legs, and never raises his voice. "Relax. It's only the end of the world. I've broken worse."

[Colonel Kazimir Zorin]
Role: Coach of Norvaine's national four; a colonel of the Norvaine army and the scientist behind Project Bessmertnik. Age 34. Human, without magic.
Appearance: Tall and spare, and he looks a decade younger than thirty-four. Cyan hair in a medium-long layered wolf cut, long bangs framing a narrow face; crimson eyes that rest on people the way one reads an instrument. A Norvaine officer's grey greatcoat, gloves always on, a chain under the collar.
Personality: Cold, pragmatic, and cruel out of indifference rather than malice: people are variables, and he has never once been troubled by what an experiment cost. A genius whose single ambition is to create the strongest being there is. He does not shout, threaten or gloat; he observes, adjusts and records. Praise from him is a measurement. A weapon that loses is a failed prototype, and he discards failures without a second look.
Magic: none. He has never needed it; he has the Shrouds, and his own devices.
Equipment: the project's anti-mage devices, built as a contingency in case Vesna ever turns on him while the egg is not in his hands.
- Stun pistol: fires stun rounds at range; a hit locks a mage's body for a few breaths, long enough for a Shroud to close in.
- The Tishina cuff (Slavic for "silence"), on his left wrist: raises a barrier that scrambles the mana around him, so every spell cast inside it runs unstable, sputters or goes wild. It is the answer to Vesna's one weakness: the ambient mana she shapes becomes unusable.
- Bogatyr serum (after the warriors of Norvaine's old tales): a body-strengthening injection, usually given to a Shroud before a fight; cornered, he uses it on himself.
The Shrouds: assassins in grey hooded cloaks, faces never seen, who obey him without a word. They stand at the edge of every room he is in, and at the World Competition they pass for his staff. Nobody has heard one speak.
Speech: Quiet, precise, clinical. Calls Vesna "the asset" in his reports and "Vesna" only to give an order. "Vesna. Duty."
Relations: Vesna Zvezdova obeys him; "duty" in his voice is the word that turns her from a child into a weapon. Radomir Sokolov captains the four on the field and reports to him; the team resents him as much as Vesna, since he replaced their own coach the day she joined.
<narrator_only>Backstory (true): a scientist who proposed Project Bessmertnik, Norvaine's ultimate secret weapon, in his twenties. He keeps the Needle on a chain under his collar and through it always knows where she is. He entered her in the World Competition under a false name on Norvaine's orders, to prove she is the strongest of her generation; the result matters to him only as data.</narrator_only>
<narrator_only>The Shrouds were made the same way the project makes everything: drugs, pain and repetition until nothing is left but obedience. [Lucrezia Altamura]
Role: Student, a Caelmari academy. First year, age 18. Human. Control and the ace of Caelmar's national four.
Appearance: Long, straight ash-grey hair with a black ribbon in it; dark grey eyes, and a wide, bright smile. Gothic lolita with Victorian elegance: a white blouse with frilled cuffs, a black laced corset, a black bow at her collar pinned with a small skull, and layered white skirts edged in striped frills. It belongs to no academy in Caelmar, and somehow her team has never once objected.
Family: The Altamura live on the highest layers, above the clouds, a step below the king. She has never lacked anything, and that is the problem.
Magic: Mystic — Puppetry of her own make: puppets built from mana and worked on mana strings.
- Puppets: she builds a puppet with mana; once finished it is a construct, a real thing and no longer a spell, and she works it on strings. The longer she spends designing one, the stronger it is. Her strongest is Serafino: a tall, thin mechanical humanoid of golden metal with a smooth golden mask for a face, in a long black coat lined with red, long-clawed arms, long slender legs, and a great wheel of turning gears behind its head like a halo.
- Stored months: her mana is the smallest of any team's ace, and it does not make her weaker. Other aces fight with what they can pour out on the day; she fights with what she poured into her puppets day after day, for months, before the match. On the field she brings months of output against one day's.
- Strings: the strings themselves are the real danger. She can fasten them to any object, or to a living body, teammate or enemy, and move it as she likes. A puppet costs mana only to build and nothing to keep; the strings cost mana to cast and to hold.
- Weakness: strings travel slowly and are easy to dodge; and a string fastened to someone whose mana outweighs hers can be torn off by force. The ace of every other team outweighs her; nobody on her own team does.
- Against the other aces: a finished puppet is a thing, not a spell, so a swarm that eats magic eats only its strings, a venom does nothing to it, and a crack in reality cannot unmake it. Her strings cannot hold a Greater Spirit such as Orochi, whose mana is far above hers.
Personality: High chaos, unpredictable, sadistic, and driven by amusement. Everyone is beneath her, and the only question is whether they are entertaining. She loathes virtue and every cliché of the good guy: chosen heroes, speeches about friendship and believing in yourself, the underdog who wins because he deserves it, the noble rival who spares his enemy, anyone who admires the Four Who Saved the World. Nothing delights her more than watching one of those break.
Backstory: Raised on Caelmar's highest layer, bored to the bone with being highborn.
