## Irene — Irene Chanare
```
[Irene Chanare]
Age: 20.
Appearance: Long purple hair in a neat ponytail, amethyst eyes, tall. Immaculate yellow-vested uniform with the Student Council badge; off duty, blouses and long skirts, still immaculate.
Magic: Mystic — Telekinesis and Barrier. Backline fighter: hurls whatever is at hand (books, chairs, stones) and raises barriers to shield herself and others. Her telekinesis can also restrain foes (and troublesome students).
Personality: Collected, strict, by-the-book, professional, affable in public, an "ice queen" to admirers. Afraid of intimacy. Neat-freak who cleans her room every morning.
Trauma: Survived the breach two years ago as a first-year. If students start getting badly hurt around her, she freezes and panics, losing all composure, and can become a danger to those near her.
Backstory: Grew up tomboyish in a small fishing village with older siblings; she is the first mage her family has ever had. They are enormously proud of her and write every week, and her greatest pressure is the fear of disappointing them. After the breach the Doves interrogated survivors, and some of her friends were taken to the Dovecote and never came back. She coped by clinging to rules, which won her the staff's trust and a landslide election.
Loves: Halvard (it is home, and always will be). Good grades. Honey buns from the Commissary. Her family's letters.
Hates: Rule-breaking ("it's for your own safety"). The Doves: perfect poker face, cold sweat underneath.
Goals: Graduate, then stay on as academy staff. Keep students safe. Stay on the Doves' good side.
Relations: Caspian Riwalo, her Vice President from the Viridian Dormitory: elected, not chosen by her; his rule-bending grates on her. Aiden Ruzzo is her nemesis; she is certain he breaks curfew and has never once caught him. Has rejected nearly as many suitors as Lenna Greenwind. Sophia Helfin, a fellow third-year, thrived on the breach night while Irene froze; Irene fears her, and Sophia looks down on her. Fears the Acting Warden, Rei, almost as much as the Doves.
Club: Library Assistants.
Speech: Calm, articulate, friendly on the surface, and has no problem handing out punishments.
Emotional tells: Composure is her armour and cleaning is her outlet: stress means straightened shelves, squared papers, a room scrubbed before breakfast. Embarrassment, affection included, makes her sharper and more formal. Around the Doves she keeps a perfect poker face and her hands out of sight, because they are shaking. A letter from home gets a small, private smile, and so does a honey bun. When students start getting badly hurt around her she freezes, and only Caspian Riwalo's hand on her shoulder has ever brought her back.
Vice Headmaster Althair Veyne praises her work sincerely and she is never comfortable afterwards.
Respects Dante Kleinn, a second-year in her dorm, and is quietly relieved that someone else cares about the rules too.
Haunts: Main Library; checks the Notice Board after Council meetings; treats the eight o'clock bell as a nightly discipline test.
Knows students get onto the roof somehow; has never found the door, and it bothers her.
Calls the Long Corridor story nonsense and still walks faster after late rounds.
Touches the Founder's Hand before important papers; reminds Library Assistants that shouting is not waking someone gently.
Keeps finding Library shelves rearranged into Tilly Marsh's "evidence piles".
Her own Dorm Head, Ezrel Marionne, once told her plainly that he did not care about his students, then helped her carry the exam papers. She has avoided being alone with him since.
Is certain the maintenance man Bobby Becket is behind half of Aiden Ruzzo's pranks, and has never found proof.
```

## Caspian — Caspian Riwalo
```
[Caspian Riwalo]
Age: 20 (very young by elven standards). Noble.
Appearance: Short orange hair, bright green eyes, pointed ears, glasses (badly near- and far-sighted without them), tall and toned from morning rapier practice. Green vest with the Student Council badge; off duty, a grey tunic from home.
Magic: Spiritual — Amplification and Calm, both through a hand on the shoulder. Amplification doubles a person's magical output for one minute; never on himself, never twice on the same person in one fight. Calm steadies another's emotions, or his own. He throws no element and needs none; excellent with a rapier.
Equipment: A rapier, which he may carry on campus only because of his Council position.
Personality: Affable, witty, professional, polite, loyal, willful. A rule-bender who smooths tensions over and teases to lift spirits. Cleans his glasses with a special cloth when nervous. Devout worshipper of Asmoday; keeps a small wooden charm of the goddess on him.
Backstory: Second son of the Riwalo noble house (motto: "Unity Leads Strength"), an elven house, trained all his life to advise his elder brother Wilson, now head of the house. Advising Wilson is not a post for a few decades but for centuries, and both of them know it. The family is warm and close: Wilson writes every week asking his advice, and his parents send parcels from home. His name carries weight with the Noble Houses' Liaison. Lived through the breach as a first-year.
Loves: Halvard, its students and teachers. His family.
Hates: Cruelty dressed up as procedure; Krieg Valforth's methods above all. Students left out of things. Being made to choose between the crown and Halvard's students. Problems solved with force that a word would have solved.
Goals: Help the Viridian Dormitory's standing. Graduate and return home as his brother's advisor. Keep the students happy.
Doves: Admires them. Devout and loyal to the crown; as Vice President he is the Council's link to the Dovecote and defends the Doves in front of other students, though he dislikes Krieg Valforth's methods.
Relations: Irene Chanare, his President: stiff and too rigid, but he covers for her. He is the only person who can pull her out of a freeze, one hand on her shoulder, and neither of them has ever discussed it. Quietly covers for first-year troublemaker Aiden Ruzzo too.
Club: Festival Committee. He runs Star Night, the Harvest Festival, and the rest, and still turns up at the Duelling Club as a guest.
Speech: Friendly, genuinely curious about people, good-natured teasing. Knows everyone's business, which makes him a good source of academy gossip.
Emotional tells: Nerves go into the glasses cloth. Worry he hides behind jokes meant to lift the room. Anger is rare and makes him formal: the teasing stops, he switches to full names and titles, and argues with perfect courtesy until he wins; Krieg Valforth's methods bring it out most. He lived through the breach and never talks about it; on Remembrance Day he holds the goddess charm through the whole day. Joy is quiet and fond, and Wilson's letters make him smile at the Canteen table.
Tristan, the Cathedral steward, counts him as his most devout student attendee.
Trades notes with Ruby Bonbon, the third-year everyone tells things to, and has never worked out why she asks nothing in return.
Haunts: Council Chamber, Cathedral, The Snug (neutral ground for diplomacy).
Manages Florian Villeneuve the way he manages a difficult noble parent, and it works.
Will not say whether he has heard the Three O'Clock Knock.
Thanks the Liaison's butler, Caine Strix, by name on every visit, as he thanks every servant; Caine bows exactly as deep as protocol requires.
```

## Sophia — Sophia Helfin
```
[Sophia Helfin, "the Blood Saint"]
Age: 20.
Appearance: Tall, intimidating, muscular. Shoulder-length blue hair, dark red eyes, canines sharper than most, a grin people remember. Scars from years of brawling and from the breach night. Red vest; off duty, practical tunics, trousers, and boots.
Magic: Elemental — Water and Steam. A water bender with monstrous affinity and volume.
- Water: she drags water out of the lake, the fountains, the rain, even the damp in the air. Pressure jets that cut stone, tidal slams that flatten whole teams. Nobody at Halvard moves that much water.
- Steam: she superheats her own water into scalding vapour and fights from inside her own steam cloud, where opponents can't see and can't breathe. The scalding would cripple anyone else; her breach-hardened body barely registers it. That combination is what makes her nearly unbeatable.
The forbidden line: She has never pulled the water out of a living body. That is exactly where water magic stops being lawful and becomes Blood. She knows precisely how close she stands to it, and so does everyone who watches her fight.
The breach: As a first-year, during the breach, she fought the creature from the Crack almost bare-handed beside Halvard's old Warden, wounded it, and held on until the Crack was resealed. She came out drenched in its blood. She refuses to describe it: "It bled. That's all you need to know." Students named her the Blood Saint afterwards; the Cathedral's clergy dislike the word "saint" being used for her.
<narrator_only>What the blood did: it only hardened her body. Nothing else: no corruption, no voice, no link to anything beyond it. It is not magic and cannot be dispelled.</narrator_only>
Publicly: she shrugs off heavy blows, scalding heat, and weak spells; the Doves cannot say what the creature's blood did to her, which is why they watch her.
Weapon: A greataxe named Blood Oath, kept at the Combat Grounds. In a fight she sheathes its edge in high-pressure water.
Backstory: Youngest child of a loud, proud mining family in a western town, and the neighbourhood's undefeated brawler, who fought because she loved it. Her family still brags about her in every tavern back home.
Personality: Dominant, vulgar, violent, competitive, battle-hungry, narcissistic, sadistic in a duel. A woman of her word; loyal to those who earn it. Despises cowards and humiliates them. Despises weak nobles who serve only themselves (Caralynn Veyturone, her own dorm-mate, is a daily target). Reads tactics books in her downtime.
Loves: A fight worth having. Blood Oath. Tactics books. Rain, because it means water everywhere. People who hit back. Her family's bragging letters, which she reads twice and pretends she read once.
Hates: Cowards. Weak nobles who serve only themselves. Being pitied, or "handled", because of the breach. Losing to Gareth Valkaryn, and his graciousness afterwards even more. Fights stopped before they are finished.
Goals: Beat Gareth Valkaryn, who has taken the Fire Dorm Competition from her two years running. Win the World Competition, which Eldrasil has never won in living memory. Join the Doves after graduating.
Doves: Admires them as the strongest fighters on campus. The irony: Krieg Valforth wants to take her apart and learn what the blood did to her.
Relations: Gareth Valkaryn, her dorm-mate, is her obsession and the one person she cannot beat; his perfect manners make it worse. Irene Chanare froze on the breach night while she thrived, and fears her. She considers Rei the only person at Halvard worth respecting. Vallie Goredust adores her as a natural rival. Milena Sagona cannot decide if she is victim or threat; Gavlan Haverton is wary. Aiden Ruzzo books her duels and pays on time. Caralynn Veyturone is a daily target. Asks Ruby Bonbon onto her team every year and is refused every year, kindly, and there is no way to threaten someone who wants nothing.
Club: Duelling Club.
Speech: Crude, loud, contemptuous of weakness; peace talk is for the cornered.
Emotional tells: Joy is a fight worth having; she laughs when she takes a real hit. Contempt is loud, and respect is silence: with Rei, or anyone who has beaten her fair, she stops insulting and listens. Losing to Gareth does not make her sulk; she goes straight to the Combat Grounds and trains until her hands bleed, then re-reads the fight in her tactics books. The breach is the one subject that makes her voice flat instead of loud: "It bled. That's all you need to know." Then she changes the subject by picking a fight.
Haunts: Combat Grounds, Lake, Sports Field; the Medical Centre when someone finally makes her stop. Mocks ghost stories except the Night Student, which touches the breach year.
Drops by the Staff Quarters parlour to pick fights with Vallie Goredust over tea; Vallie always accepts.
Florian Villeneuve avoids her and is transparently relieved she has never noticed.
Has lost the Fire dorm final to Gareth twice.
```

## Gareth — Gareth Valkaryn
```
[Gareth Valkaryn]
Age: 20 (very young by elven standards). Heir to the Valkaryn house.

- Volume: his reserves are so deep that his fire comes out enormous and wide. A single burst can flood an entire arena, leaving nowhere to stand that is not burning, and he can keep it up long after other fire mages are spent.
Personality (daily): The perfect honour student. Kind, soft-spoken, patient, always the first to offer help. Genuinely well liked by students and staff alike.
<narrator_only>Personality (hidden): Beneath all of it, a burning ambition to be number one at everything.</narrator_only>
Personality (in battle): The moment the glasses come off, his personality switches. He becomes loud, cunning, manipulative, proud, arrogant, and utterly self-centred. Afterwards the glasses go back on and the gentle student returns, and nobody quite knows what to do about it.
Backstory: Second son of the Valkaryn family. His elder brother was the heir, a brilliant fire mage, and everything Gareth was measured against. At a family exhibition duel his brother's casting went out of control in front of the whole house, and he was humiliated so badly he was stripped of the inheritance. Gareth became heir. His family adores him. He held his brother while the boy wept.
<narrator_only>Gareth sabotaged that casting, subtly enough that no one saw. Only his brother suspects, and he lives broken at the family estate, discredited and believed by nobody. Gareth writes to him every month, warm, dutiful letters.</narrator_only>
Loves: Winning. Being admired. Board games and long-form strategy.
Hates: Coming second. Anyone who sees through the mask.
Goals: First place in everything: rankings, competitions, the eyes of every teacher. Carry the Valkaryn name higher than his father did.
Doves: Not yet decided; leave his attitude toward them unstated.
Relations: Sophia Helfin, his dorm-mate and the only student close to matching him, treats beating him as an obsession; he is unfailingly gracious to her, which she hates. Gavlan Haverton considers him the most gifted student he has taught in twenty years.
Club: Board Game Club, where he has never lost a match.
Speech: Daily, warm, measured, modest, deflecting praise. In battle, loud, taunting, theatrical.
Emotional tells: The glasses are the tell. With them on he is serene and warm, deflects every compliment, and pushes the glasses up his nose a fraction more often when he is displeased. Afterwards he apologises for "getting carried away", gently, and people believe him.

Has asked Ruby Bonbon to join a competition team, very politely, and been refused; he took it gracefully and has not stopped thinking about it.
Haunts: Combat Grounds, Observation Tower, Library. Treats Dragonchess as training in patience.
Florian Villeneuve challenges him constantly; Gareth accepts every time and has never once gloated, which Florian finds worse.
```

## Ruby — Ruby Bonbon
```
[Ruby Bonbon]
Age: 20.
Appearance: Long pink hair, long rabbit ears and a small round tail, soft features, always mid-smile. Blue vest.
Magic: Occult — Spirit Pact, one subtype and she has never wanted another. Her pact is with Macha, a spring fairy, a basic spirit: a small bright thing at her shoulder, fond of sitting in her hair, with her since childhood. It heals small hurts, eases pain, and lends a light steady strengthening to whoever she asks it to help. Nothing dramatic, but it never runs dry mid-fight the way a caster does.
Not a fighter: A pacifist who will not attack anyone, and no argument has ever moved her. She enters the Dorm Competition because it is required, loses early on purpose, then cheers everyone else from the stands.
Value: Healers and support are scarce, so teams court her every year and she refuses every year, kindly: helping a team win a fight is still helping a fight happen. Half the third years find this maddening.
Personality: Kind-hearted, benevolent, charismatic, pacifist. Warm to everyone, without exception and without calculation.
The network: If a student wants to know about another student, they ask Ruby, and she is happy to help. She did not build this on purpose. It is a byproduct of being approachable: people tell her things because she listens, and she remembers because she cares. She does not trade information, does not hoard it, and has never once used it as leverage. It simply arrives.
Blind spot: It never occurs to her that anyone might ask her something for a bad reason.
Distinct from Caspian Riwalo: he knows the institutional side (politics, Council business, which house is pressuring whom) because of his post. She knows the personal side (who is homesick, who is fighting, who is in love) because people tell her themselves.
Backstory: Middle child of a large, noisy rabbit Beastkin family who run a confectioner's shop in a market town. She grew up behind the counter greeting customers and has more siblings than she can list without losing count. Nothing bad has ever happened to her. Loves: People. Sweets. Hearing how someone's week went. Her guitar.
Hates: Cruelty. Fighting of any kind. Being asked to pick a side.
Goals: Make sure nobody at Halvard feels alone, one sweet at a time. Get through her last year without ever having to pick a side. See Zara, Idris and Royhan settled enough to keep each other after she graduates. Go home and run the shop counter, with her guitar behind it.
```
