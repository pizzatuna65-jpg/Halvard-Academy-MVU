## 1. Nation — Arslan Sultanate (owner: kerajaan uang, "Wall Street" sebagai satu kerajaan, lokasi World Bank)
Keys: `Arslan Sultanate`, `Arslan`, `World Bank`, `the Sultan` (keyword `Arslan` / `Arslan Sultanate` pindah dari mitos
The Devoured Kingdom ke sini; mitos itu tetap memakai `Primal Desert`, `Azathoth`, `Lord of All`, `Devoured Kingdom`).
```
[Arslan Sultanate]
What: a sultanate in the Primal Desert, where the Devoured Kingdom once stood, and the money capital of the world. The desert grows nothing, so the whole country trades: bankers, brokers and moneylenders.
Known for: the World Bank, in its capital, which sets what every nation's coin is worth and lends to every crown, Eldrasil's included.
People: rich, exact, and very polite to anyone who owes them.
Status: the sand still turns up stones of the devoured kingdom. Nobody digs deeper than they have to.
```
Kaitan: mitos 1 (lokasinya Primal Desert). Canon: "the Bank" termasuk kekuatan luar di tanah akademi (WORLD INDEX, Banking
House, uid 92) → usulan mention: Banking House adalah cabang World Bank (lihat bagian Mention).

## 2. Nation — (nama?) kerajaan utara (owner: makmur, militer terkuat, raja ber-pact dengan Yog-Sothoth)
Keys: `<nama>`, `Yog-Sothoth`, `Lord of the Cosmos`, `stars became eyes`, `Star Night saint`
```
[<Name>]
What: the richest kingdom of the north, with the strongest army on the continent. Its king holds a pact with Yog-Sothoth, Lord of the Cosmos, a Spirit Lord.
Star Night: at midnight, over the royal castle, every star becomes an eye and every comet a tentacle.
Price: at that midnight Yog-Sothoth takes the one it has chosen as its Saint. They are erased from the world's memory, every proof that they existed vanishes, and their soul goes with them.
Status: the kingdom knows a Saint was taken and never remembers who. Nobody knows what the king gained, or why Yog-Sothoth wants them.
```
Catatan kanon:
- **Logika penghapusan** (tafsiran Claude): kalau Saint benar-benar terhapus dari ingatan dunia, tidak ada yang bisa tahu ada yang
  diambil. Jadi yang diingat hanya **aturannya** (harga pact itu diketahui), bukan **orangnya**: setiap pagi sesudah Star Night
  kerajaan tahu satu Saint sudah diambil, dan tidak ada yang bisa mengingat siapa. Ganti kalau maksud owner lain.
- Pact dengan Spirit Lord sah menurut canon; mitos 1 ("a lawful pact can still cost everything") jadi cermin kerajaan ini.
- Star Night di canon: langit sangat cerah dan hujan meteor di tengah malam. Di atas kastil kerajaan ini, meteor/komet itu yang
  menjadi tentakel. Di Halvard tidak terjadi apa-apa.
- Kata **"saint"**: di canon, clergy Cathedral tidak suka kata "saint" dipakai untuk Sophia Helfin ("the Blood Saint"). Jadi kata
  itu bermuatan agama; bisa jadi hook (Cathedral membenci gelar Saint milik Yog-Sothoth). Keyword tidak memakai "Saint" saja
  supaya entry Sophia tidak ikut terpanggil.
