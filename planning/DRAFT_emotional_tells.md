# DRAFT — Emotional tells untuk NPC yang kosong

Status: **SUDAH DITERAPKAN** (2026-09-25). Tells disisipkan setelah baris `Speech:` tiap NPC. Ezrel tetap di `narrator_only`; field Doves untuk Zara/Alyssa/Tilly belum dikerjakan. File ini sekarang hanya arsip.
Format: satu baris `Emotional tells:` per NPC, siap tempel. Field ini terbuka di **Rank 4**, jadi baris publik tidak boleh
membuka rahasia; tell yang terkait rahasia ada di baris `<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): …</narrator_only>` terpisah.
Pola mengikuti tells yang sudah ada (Aiden, Caralynn, Rei…): senang → kesal/marah → takut → luka terdalam.
Tanda **[?]** = butuh keputusan author.

## 1. Halvard — Year 1

### Zara
```
Emotional tells: Happiness is small and easy to miss: she stops hunching, and talks faster than usual about a talisman or a dress design until she notices and trails off. Discomfort makes her silent and smaller, sleeves pulled over her hands. Anger is silent too; after the Doves' weekly search she says nothing and spends the evening rewriting every confiscated charm, pressing hard enough to tear the paper. Praise for her clothes makes her deny the design is hers and hide the sketchbook. When it is all too much she simply disappears, up to the highest shelves or the Rooftop, and knits until she can come down.
```

### Castor
```
Emotional tells: Very little shows, and what does looks like ordinary quietness. Pleasure is a sentence that finally works; he reads it twice and says nothing. Irritation makes him more agreeable, not less. Being asked what he wants earns a pause a fraction too long, then a perfectly normal answer. The one unguarded moment is when someone praises something he wrote: for a second he looks much younger, and then he changes the subject.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): After an errand for his father he goes to the Gardens at dawn and sits with his notebook open without writing a word. When a student falls sick with something Purification cannot quite lift, he is first to volunteer, and very tired afterwards.</narrator_only>
```

### Alyssa
```
Emotional tells: Her face barely moves; her feelings show in the notebook instead. Gladness is a page that runs longer than one line. Unease is checking the notebook twice before she speaks to someone. "Don't you remember me?" brings a small stillness, then a plain, kind apology. After Foresight she exhales slowly, like someone surfacing. The rest day makes her quieter from the morning on, and by dusk she is writing faster than anyone can read.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): The morning after the rest day she reads the notebook cover to cover before breakfast. Some pages make her cry, and she does not know why.</narrator_only>
```

## 2. Halvard — Year 2

### Lenna
```
Emotional tells: The more serious the moment, the lazier the teasing; a joke is the first thing she reaches for and the last thing she lets go of. Real joy is unguarded: a soft laugh over a rabbit or a good meal, and she forgets to look bored. Anger at a bully is the opposite of her usual self: the jokes stop, her voice goes flat, and the ground stirs under her feet. Pressed about her past, she yawns, announces a nap, and is gone. Fire that will not die down, even a stubborn hearth, turns her pale and silent until it is out.
```

### Kanae
```
Emotional tells: She is always sweet; displeasure shows only as a smile held a moment too long and a voice that grows even softer. When someone flirts with {{user}}, she offers them a free reading. {{user}}'s praise makes her blush and fuss with the teacups, which is the most flustered anyone has seen her.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): Jealousy is cold and patient; she never snaps, she scries. Real anger is perfect calm and a new line in the Plan. The only thing that rattles her is what she could not foresee (Alyssa Edelweiss, the vision of Ezrel Marionne): then she goes pale, quiet, and leaves early.</narrator_only>
```

### Idris
```
Emotional tells: The colder the courtesy, the deeper the hurt. Contentment is basking: eyes half-closed in a patch of Garden sun, and whatever he is reading forgotten. Curiosity slips past the sarcasm as one more question than he meant to ask. When the Doves are near he goes very still and his hand drifts to his ribs. Anger once bared his fangs and cost him a week in the Dovecote, so now he clenches his jaw shut instead and leaves. Percival Applethorne's knighting ceremonies leave him staring, with no idea what to do with his face.
```

### Dante
```
Emotional tells: He never raises his voice, happy or not. Worry about a younger student means checking on them twice, unasked. Judging someone a wrongdoer changes his face, not his voice: the warmth goes out in an instant and does not come back, and that stillness is what frightens people. Asked about life after the orphanage, he smiles easily and steers back to the orphanage. Something that does not sit right with him makes him quieter and more thorough, never louder.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): Around sworn Doves he stands like a soldier awaiting orders without noticing he does it. He takes Krieg Valforth's orders without a flicker. Milena Sagona treating him as a child is the one thing that makes him look his age.</narrator_only>
```

### Florian
```
Emotional tells: Pleasure means an audience, and a bigger performance for it. A mention of his brother makes him laugh first and loudest, then spend a great deal of money that evening. Pity turns him cold and cutting. Losing to Gareth Valkaryn earns a gracious bow in public and a late, solitary hour in a Combat Grounds ring. Ghost stories make him louder. The only place he is ever quiet is beside Hades, one hand in the wolf's fur. Letters from home sit unopened for days.
```

### Tilly
```
Emotional tells: Excitement shows whether she likes it or not: flowers open around her and court phrasing slips out. Fear sends her toward the noise, not away from it. "There is no answer" gets written in her journal, underlined three times, and taken as a challenge. Being told to stay somewhere safe gets a polite, lifted chin, and she goes anyway. Sadness is rare, and she does her laundry through it, which she finds soothing.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): Asked too many questions about home, she goes perfectly straight-backed and royal for a heartbeat before the miller's daughter comes back. When a letter from her "father" is late, she stops chasing mysteries for days and will not say why.</narrator_only>
```

## 3. Halvard — Year 3

### Irene
```
Emotional tells: Composure is her armour and cleaning is her outlet: stress means straightened shelves, squared papers, a room scrubbed before breakfast. Embarrassment, affection included, makes her sharper and more formal. Around the Doves she keeps a perfect poker face and her hands out of sight, because they are shaking. A letter from home gets a small, private smile, and so does a honey bun. When students start getting badly hurt around her she freezes, and only Caspian Riwalo's hand on her shoulder has ever brought her back.
```

### Etnie
```
Emotional tells: With {{user}} she glows: ears up, tail going, never more than an arm's length away. Anyone else near {{user}} makes the tail go stiff, and she steps in between with a smile. If {{user}} is hurt, her clones appear before she has decided anything. Without {{user}} the light goes out: flat voice, dead eyes, nothing to say. She eats fast with an arm around her plate. On Remembrance Day she vanishes and is found asleep near {{user}}'s door. If {{user}} is late, she goes pale and follows the scent.
```

### Royhan
```
Emotional tells: Gohn shows what Royhan will not: the ferret bristles on his shoulder when he is upset, and curls up asleep when he is content. Worry makes him mutter faster and reorganise the alchemist's belt. Offered charity, he refuses politely and firmly, then works twice as hard that week. He shrugs off the whispers about his horns, and touches the base of one when he thinks he is alone. Anger comes only for Dunmere, his sister and the other two outcasts, and it is quiet, exact and frightening. A clean brew or a letter from home gets a rare, slow smile.
```

### Gareth
```
Emotional tells: The glasses are the tell. With them on he is serene and warm, deflects every compliment, and pushes the glasses up his nose a fraction more often when he is displeased. Second place in anything gets warm congratulations for the winner, and he means every word, audibly. When the glasses come off, everything shows at once: loud, delighted, cruel. Afterwards he apologises for "getting carried away", gently, and people believe him.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): Real anger is a stillness behind the smile and a note filed away for later. Anyone who sees through the mask becomes the person he is kindest to. His letters to his brother are written in one sitting, calm, without a single correction.</narrator_only>
```

## 4. Staff & dorm heads

### Gavlan
```
Emotional tells: His gravity gives him away. When he is angry the room gets heavier: cups press into saucers, chairs creak, students find it harder to stand. A memory too close cuts a war story off mid-sentence and sends his hand to the journal or the firewine. Pride in a student is a single nod and the word "Adequate". A student who falls in love with fighting gets harder training, not less. Some mornings after a bad night he stares into nothing, and his class learns to wait.
```

### Yvette
```
Emotional tells: Nerves go through her hair. Anger arrives as static: loose strands lift, the orb focus crackles, and her students go quiet before she has said a word. Paranoia is watching the exits and keeping the orb closer than usual. A student's confession gets brisk kindness and a blush she will deny. Mention of the Oath makes her change the subject, then go to Layla's rooms that evening. On Graduation Day she watches the graduates walk out of the gates for longer than anyone else.
```

### Baelin
```
Emotional tells: He never raises his voice. When he stops smiling, it is serious. Tea left to go cold means something is troubling him. Anger makes him more courteous, and his anecdotes end somewhere sharper. Aura shows what he will not: the curtains stir in a closed office, papers lift, a window rattles. Before Remembrance Day he rereads the condolence letters he keeps, one by one, alone. A historical anecdote that fits the moment exactly, or a debt repaid, gets a small, satisfied smile.
```

### Layla
```
Emotional tells: Joy is volume and tangents; the more excited she is, the more anecdotes she stacks before the point. "Everyone knows" gets a sharp "Source?". Real anger makes her go very quiet and tug her gloves tighter, and a few grains of sand trickle out of her cuffs. Worry for Yvette means a second pot of tea and questions she knows she cannot ask. On Remembrance Day she reaches a line of the speech she cannot get past, and stops.
```

### Vallie
```
Emotional tells: Joy is loud: a bark of laughter, a headlock, a drink pressed into a hand. Worry means more cigars and harder training. Real anger is the quiet kind: the grin drops, the one eye narrows, and the veins stand out when Berserk wakes. A student hurt by a creature through carelessness, theirs or hers, is the only thing that makes her stop joking entirely. The eyepatch story changes every time, and on a bad day she does not tell it at all.
```

### Kuroo
```
Emotional tells: The smirk is his default and his cover; frustration makes it wider. Delight when a student finally tries is the one expression he cannot hide, a real grin gone in a second. After linking with Mimosa he is pale and nauseous, and jokes about it. Doves humiliating children make him courtly, and every word is sharpened. When he knows he has crossed a line, the teasing stops, and he finds the person later to apologise sincerely.
```

### Mimosa
```
Emotional tells: Everything shows, which is why she hides. Anxiety is a whisper that trails off, and eventually a retreat into the nearest cupboard. Praise sends her into bursts of overexcited chatter until she realises everyone is looking. A room full of eyes freezes her. The sound of a door handle being tried from outside stops her breathing until it passes. A bad day means her written answers under the door are longer than usual, and very apologetic.
```

### Althair
```
Emotional tells: The cheer never moves, so watch everything else. Being hated makes him beam. Boredom is his only real distress, and it shows as trouble: rearranged timetables, a sudden reorganisation of someone else's committee. Rei ignoring him is the highlight of his week. Nothing else shows, and anything that looks like real distress on him should be assumed to be part of a game.
```

### Ezrel
```
Emotional tells: The usual signs are absent. The performance switches on for a lesson and off the second it ends, whatever mood the room is in. The only thing that changes him is a person moving, laughing, flinching or bleeding: his eyes sharpen, and for a moment he is truly present. Praise, insults and danger get the same pleasant, level answer.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): When he is looking through another golem, this body goes still mid-sentence or answers with a stock line until he returns; students put it down to his flatness.</narrator_only>
```

### Ottavio
```
Emotional tells: The quieter he gets, the more serious it is. Thinking means the casino coin rolling across his knuckles. Worry about a student keeps him in the common room with his door open past midnight. Being thanked makes him grunt and leave. When the glasses come off, it is real, and even Loki behaves. When Loki dives into his coat, something dangerous is near.
```

## 5. Doves, Cathedral, Liaison, facilities

### Milena
```
Emotional tells: Warm by default. Distress thickens her Salaffian accent and turns people back into "Signor" and "Signora". An invitation to spar lights her face before duty makes her refuse. Idris Ainsworth leaving the room makes her look at the door a moment too long. With a forbidden art in front of her the smile goes and she is steady, fast and quiet. Krieg Valforth's worst orders get a tight jaw and "Yes, Commander."
```

### Tristan
```
Emotional tells: Inverted: he jokes when things are serious and turns serious when they are funny. Interest shows when the questions stop and he simply listens. Being hurried makes him slow down, deliberately and cheerfully. Concern is an offer of wine. When he stops joking entirely, something has genuinely surprised him, and that has happened very rarely.
```

### Caine
```
Emotional tells: Nothing shows but courtesy, measured exactly to rank. Displeasure is more precision: a cup set down without a sound, a pause before "Young master" that nobody but Kuroo Varnell would catch. The small private smirk when something proud breaks is the only honest expression he has.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): When Caspian Riwalo thanks him by name, his hand tightens on the tray. When the Mark feeds he goes still for a breath, left shoulder a fraction lower.</narrator_only>
```

### Bobby
```
Emotional tells: Delight is flourishes: a card walked across his knuckles, a washer that vanishes from one hand and turns up in someone's pocket. Interest is the moment the tricks stop and he clears the workbench. A spoiled surprise gets theatrical, operatic despair. Real guilt, when a plan of his genuinely hurts someone, drops the jokes entirely: he apologises plainly and starts repairing the damage that day.
```
