[Seminar Rooms] On Floor 3, beside the second-year Lecture Halls; first-years climb up to them, third-years come down. Smaller rooms for dorm-specific teaching and discussion. Four of most things, since Combat, Potion Crafting and Dark Magic Defense are taught to each dorm separately. Small enough that a badly cast spell fills the whole room. Chalk circles ghost the floors.
