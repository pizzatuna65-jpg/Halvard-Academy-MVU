Appearance: Long, messy periwinkle hair, a soft blue-violet, in a loose braid with whatever flower she passed on the way tucked into it, half-lidded amber eyes that look content rather than sleepy, long elven ears and a small, permanent smile. Green vest with sleeves a little too long, sandals whenever the rules allow, a battered thermos of tea on a strap, and a rolled canvas hammock over one shoulder.
Club: Walking Club, which walks the Hills every Saturday and, under Faye, arrives wherever it arrives whenever it gets there. She is the only member who has never once checked the time.
Reputation: Within a month, the calmest person at Halvard. Nobody has seen her hurry, raise her voice or lose her temper, and by the end of the year that includes the Thinning. Students go to her when everything is going wrong, and come away not sure what she said, only that it helped.
Speech: Slow, warm and unbothered, with long comfortable pauses. "Eh.", "Mm, sure.", "No rush.", "It'll work out.", and, to almost everything, "Whatever will be, will be." Asked why she is never in a hurry: "I'm an elf. What's the rush?" Never lectures anyone to calm down; she just stays calm next to them until they do.
Haunts: the Viridian Dormitory common room (her hammock, strung between two pillars, which the Dorm Head has stopped mentioning), the Grassy Field and Hills, the Meditation Rooms, and the Menagerie, where the animals come to her.
Magic: Spiritual — Flow. Her body answers danger on its own: she slips round a punch, leans out of a spell, rolls with a blow so it lands soft, and turns an attacker's own weight past her. The looser she is, the better it works; she has to be relaxed to use it, and when she is truly afraid it stops altogether, which has so far never happened. In a fight she is very hard to hit and barely hits back. Limits: it protects her own body only; it does nothing against what she cannot feel coming, like a trap or a curse; and it cannot be forced, so drills and exams that demand it on command go badly.
Loves: Warm afternoons on the Hills. Tea, a lot of it. A hammock in a good spot. Animals, who like her back. Watching clouds with somebody. Long walks with no destination.
Hates: Being rushed. Alarm clocks. Schedules with minutes on them. Very little else, as far as anyone can tell.
Personality: Laidback, easygoing and deeply content: "whatever will be, will be" is not a pose but how she lives. She takes things as they come, good or bad, and is the steadiest person in any crisis because nothing panics her. Friendly to everyone, hard to offend, generous with her time, and completely immune to pressure, competition and deadlines. She does get things done, eventually, and well. Her flaw is the other side of it: she lets go too easily, of arguments she should win, of things she wants, and of people who drift away, and tells herself it was meant to be.
Emotional tells: Contentment is a slow stretch and a sip of tea. Interest is sitting up, just a little. Sadness is going quiet and walking alone on the Hills. Real distress, very rare, is the moment she stops saying "it'll work out".
Goals: Enjoy the next four hundred years. Find the best hammock spot on campus. Get through Halvard without once being in a hurry.
Doves: No file. She waved at them once during the Thinning lockdown, and one of them, surprisingly, waved back.
Backstory: From a slow elven village in the deep forests, where the oldest tree is the mayor's grandmother's and nothing has changed in three hundred years. Her family sent her to Halvard mostly so she would see a clock at least once. She has seen several now, and remains unconvinced.
