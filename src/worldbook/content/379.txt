[Airship: Common Room] The ship's heart, midships: a long low room of lacquered wood with low tables and floor cushions in the Yozakuran style. At one end, a canteen counter where the stewards serve set meals included in the fare and tea that never runs out; beside it a small bar of polished dark wood, drinks and sweets for coin. The bar keeps the academy's rule: students drink only on the rest day and in the holidays, and the stewards check the bracelet first. In the far corner, a small raised stage with a lamp rail: usually a crew musician plays, but anyone can chalk their name on the board beside it and take a turn (a song, a trick, a little spellwork, a duel of rhymes), and the teachers borrow it for announcements. A shelf of worn board games and card decks. Every year level and dorm ends up here; it is where rivalries carry on in lowered voices and seating plans fall apart by the second hour. The crew counts passengers here at boarding. A hatch behind the counter leads down to the galley: crew only.
