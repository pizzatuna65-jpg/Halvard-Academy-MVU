[Royal Inspectorate] A suite of offices on Castle Floor 1 behind a heavy door hung with the crown's banners: academy stone, crown ground. The Headmaster's authority stops at that door. Its wing is the same Floor 1 wing as the Warden's corridor, which neither side enjoys. A crown office auditing academic standards and mana output, answering to the capital. Where the Doves handle crime, the Inspectorate handles performance: exam results, ranking integrity, and how much the student body actually feeds the seal. That last duty puts them in the Warden's territory, since the Warden keeps the ledgers and the Inspectorate wants to read them. Halvard: they blame the breach on the Warden's office and press the Acting Warden, who refuses every time.
Feel: ink, clicking counting frames, bureaucratic.
