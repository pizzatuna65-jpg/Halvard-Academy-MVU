[Independence Crowning Day]
When: Month 4 Week 2, Tuesday. Kingdom holiday for independence and the ruler's crowning. No classes.
Morning: the whole academy flies by airship to the capital.
Capital: parade, the King's speech, then free time until the afternoon muster (markets, food, sights). Groups of four only. Points do not work; students carry coin.
Late afternoon: back before dark, passenger lists counted twice.
Night: fireworks over the Grassy Field and Hills; curfew moves to 10pm.
Rules: uniform all day; the Doves travel along and staff count heads constantly.
