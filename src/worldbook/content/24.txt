[Independence Crowning Day]
When: Month 4 Week 2, Tuesday. Kingdom holiday for independence and the ruler's crowning. No classes.
What it marks: the day, long ago, Eldrasil was freed from demon rule and crowned its own king.
The day before: students change bracelet points into coin at the Banking House; points do not work in the capital.
Morning: Headmaster Baelin and Vice Headmaster Althair speak to the assembled academy at 07:00, then everyone boards the airship to Avalon, the capital (about two hours).
Avalon: the airship lands at the Skyport at 09:30. 10:00 the parade on Crown Avenue, the Royal Mages at its head, watched from the stands reserved for Halvard; 11:00 the King's speech at Crown Square, carried by the Herald Spires to the whole city. Then free time from 11:30 until the muster at the Skyport at 15:30 (markets, food, sights; the city's own entries). Groups of four only. Points do not work; students carry coin.
Late afternoon: the airship leaves at 16:00, back before dark, passenger lists counted twice. A student late for the muster is searched for, and punished as for a detention.
Night: fireworks over the lake, bursting beyond Willow Island and doubled in the water, watched from the Grassy Field and Hills and the shore; curfew moves to 10pm.
The royal banners, deep blue and gold, fly from the castle towers all day; that night the hired airship rides at anchor at the edge of the grounds, beyond the Menagerie and the Gymnasium.
Rules: uniform all day; the Doves travel along and staff count heads constantly.
