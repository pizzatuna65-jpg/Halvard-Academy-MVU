[The Morning Choir]
Underground cult serving Lucifer inside the academies. Unknown to students and staff at large.
Goal: open the seals. Believes Lucifer, not Asmoday, made magic; the sites are prisons and the seals steal his light. The Archmage was a coward.
Cover: not a registered club, so no signature, room, or roster to seize. Every student and staff member also joins an ordinary club. Meets in the Forest between curfew and dawn.
Members: staff stay decades and plan in generations; students are present three years, unsupervised and expendable, and take the risks. Members also sit among the outside powers' staff on academy land; their post is their cover, and they join no club. Recruits Occult students and bottom-band students, telling both that the academy is a mana farm and they are the fuel. That is the cult's framing, not the truth: the seals only collect residual mana already left behind by casting, as History Class teaches.
Lucifer's Mark: borne by high officers, greatly strengthens their magic. Costs: memories, eaten a name or a face at a time and later whole years; a tithe of everything they cast; and finally possession, which is temporary and destroys the body. Cultists consider being chosen the highest honour. The Mark can also be forced: a body made by Puppetry to perform the rite with its own hands takes it, and Lucifer counts the body's consent as consent. Such bearers are vessels, not officers, and the Mark's first meal is the memory of receiving it.
Opportunities: the Warding Rite (every dorm casts at once, so one altered working goes unnoticed); The Thinning (seal weakest, academy locked down); the mana ledgers (only the Warden can read them).
Danger: patience. Undoing a seal outlasts any student's three years, so whoever starts never finishes, and current members may not know what earlier ones did.

<narrator_only>The unregistered group the Doves are tracing IS this cult; they do not know it yet.</narrator_only>
