[GRAND VAST FOREST INDEX]
What: Eldrasil's great rainforest, half a day from Halvard by airship, so vast that crossing it takes weeks and nobody has walked all of it. Hunting-company country. Halvard comes once a year, for the Creature Studies Expedition (Month 2 Week 3), and uses only its southern rim: from Base Camp to the farthest point of that country is about half a day on foot.
Landmark: the Old Heart, a single giant tree at the rim's northern edge, rising far above the canopy and seen from anywhere in the southern forest; everyone steers by it. Beyond it the deep forest runs on, unmapped.
Climate: warm and wet all year. Mornings start in mist, the air is heavy and humid, and most afternoons a downpour comes through the canopy; nothing ever fully dries. Buttress-rooted giants, palms, tree ferns, hanging vines and moss on everything.
Places: Base Camp (the meadow at the southern edge: tents, the command tent, the Huntsmen's Lodge), The Wildwood (the ordinary rainforest west, north and east of Base Camp), Blightbriar (poisoned thorns and spore fungus), The Gloaming (the oldest, darkest stand, where noon looks like dusk), Silverstair Falls (the Silverrun river falling in a dozen tiers), Jade Terraces (hundreds of cold stepped pools above the falls), Grand Cave Network (a cliff mouth onto caves nobody has mapped), The Old Heart (the single giant tree at the northern edge). Each has its own entry when it comes up.
