[Seal Chamber] The sealed site itself, the reason the academy exists. Only the Warden enters, and the way in is watched. Contents differ by academy: a monster at Myrdath, a tomb at Veyra, an elixir at Ashvale, a crack at Halvard. Most students never see the door. Halvard: the corridor toward it runs colder, the lanterns burn unnaturally steady, and students who wander close feel pressure on their ears and turn back before the door.
Feel: cold, still, oppressive.
