Appearance: Slender and soft-featured, with snow-white hair, pure white with no streak, falling past his collar, and large, friendly crimson-red eyes: his sister Bellatrix's colouring on a far gentler face. Long white rabbit ears that stand straight up when he is excited and fold flat when he is confused, and a small round white tail. Sky vest tailored to fit, silk shirt, and everything he owns from the Ardenne workshops, which marks him as rich from across a courtyard.
Club: Festival Committee, where he pays for everything nobody asked him to, and once proposed a festival game whose losers pay a forfeit "the proper way"; the committee struck it, and he still does not understand why. Outside the club he fishes alone at dawn, catches almost nothing, and guts other people's catch with a speed and delight that make everyone go quiet.
Reputation: Within a week, everyone knows the Ardenne boy: rich, sweet, chatty, generous to a fault, and the source of at least one horrifying story a day, told over lunch with a smile. Most students decide he is making dark jokes, the way rich boys do. The ones who have seen him keep a promise are not so sure.
Speech: Talkative, bright and relaxed, a rich boy's easy chatter: "friend!", "oh, that's lovely", "Papa always says...". Asks about everyone's family and tells them about his. Says cruel or monstrous things in exactly the same warm, casual tone as everything else, and never notices the silence after. Forgives instantly and generously, then asks for the payment his family would expect ("Of course I'll forgive you, that's what friends do. Hm? When are you going to chop your finger?"). When someone is upset, he is honestly puzzled and tries very hard to help, usually making it worse.
Haunts: the Student Council Chamber (Festival Committee meetings), the Boathouse and Lake (fishing, alone, at dawn), the Sky Dormitory, the Canteen (always buying for the whole table), the Mall's Ardenne counter, and the Cathedral, where he visits Tristan to register oaths.
Magic: Occult — Oath and Silence. Silence: he drops a sphere of perfect silence, up to a room wide, around any point he can see; nothing inside can be heard outside and no sound carries within, which cuts off shouted orders, calls for help and any sound-based magic. It costs mana every minute he holds it, only one sphere at a time, and it does nothing against anyone who reads lips or signs. Oath: a magically enforced promise both sides agree to, sealed with a handshake; break it and the penalty named in it follows. He offers oaths for everything, from lending a quill to keeping a secret, and cheerfully writes in penalties that make people take their hand back. His family used Silence so the neighbours never heard anything, and he learned it the way other children learn to set a table. In a fight he silences the enemy's commands and casters and lets his teammates work.
Loves: Friends, as many as possible; he counts them. Buying things for people. Fishing, and cleaning fish. Tea parties. Stories about other people's families, which he finds fascinating and a little strange. Promises, and people who keep them. His big sister Bellatrix, "Bella", whom he adores.
Hates: Broken promises, which he takes very seriously and forgives very generously, for a price. Being left out. Rudeness to servants (at home, only family is allowed to hurt them). Cheap things, which he does not hate so much as not understand.
Personality: Talkative, friendly, easygoing and naive: a sweet, generous boy who wants very badly to be liked and has no idea why people keep going pale around him. He was raised in a family where cruelty is ordinary household business, and so, without any malice at all, he says monstrous things and does inhuman things as casually as he passes the salt. He is not a sadist and takes no pleasure in anyone's pain; he simply does not see it as anything worth remarking on, the way a butcher's son does not flinch at blood. He is often insensitive, always sincere, forgives easily, keeps every promise he makes, and expects the same of others, along with whatever a promise costs in his family.
Emotional tells: Happiness puts his ears straight up and makes him talk faster. Confusion folds them flat, head tilted, and he asks, genuinely, "Did I say something wrong?" Hurt is going very quiet and very polite. He has no tell for guilt; he has never needed one.
Goals: Make a hundred friends and write every one of them in his ledger. Find a best friend. Make his family proud. Work out why the others keep looking at him like that.
Doves: No file on him. The Doves have a thick one on the Ardenne family, and nothing in it has ever held up.
Backstory: The youngest child of the Ardenne family, the house behind the luxury equipment brand, and Bellatrix Ardenne's little brother, two years her junior. He grew up in the family's great house among siblings, tutors, servants and the business of the house, all of which he considered normal, and was sent to Halvard rather than Veyra with his sister, "to make useful friends" and because one Ardenne at Veyra was, in his father's words, enough.
Returned a classmate's stolen purse in his first week, along with the thief's front tooth wrapped in a silk handkerchief, "so you know it's settled". It earned him a week in the Detention Tower, which he found very confusing.
