[The Hundred Alleys] A knot of lanes so narrow two people cannot pass, packed with a hundred tiny stalls that seat six at most: noodle broth simmered since dawn, skewers over charcoal, sake by the cup, smoke and laughter spilling into the snow. Every stall owner knows every regular.
