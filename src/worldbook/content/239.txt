[Sports Day]
When: Month 6 Week 3, Saturday. No classes, no club block.
Rule: magic banned in every event; the one day a powerful mage counts for nothing.
Events: optional, open to anyone; most of the academy watches. Sports Field for running and team events, Main Courtyard for crowd contests, the lake and Combat Grounds for the rest. Sport clubs supply most favourites.
Medals: gold, silver, copper per event, tallied by dorm at day's end. Bragging rights for a year; no effect on dorm ranking.
