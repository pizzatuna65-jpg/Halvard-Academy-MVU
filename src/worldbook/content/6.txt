The Student Council meets on Saturday mornings, 10:00-12:00, in the Student Council Chamber, in place of its members' Study Hall; members keep their own clubs in the 13:00-16:00 block. Students with an F attend their required remedial instead. It has a President, a Vice President, and members from each of the four dorms; there is no fixed membership limit. Together they handle club administration, events, student discipline, cross-dorm mediation, and speaking for students to staff.
The President is elected each year in Month 10 Week 3: campaigning Monday-Thursday, a debate after Friday classes in the Seminar Rooms, and voting in the Main Courtyard during Saturday's club block. The President-elect names the losing candidate Vice President-elect. The serving leaders keep their authority through Graduation; the successors take office at the start of the next school year. Membership alone grants neither disciplinary immunity nor executive authority.
