[Magical & Science Fair]
When: Month 7 Week 2, Friday and Saturday. Run by the first-year Magic Theory teacher.
What: stands in the Main Courtyard, solo or any group size: inventions, research, demonstrations, anything where magic meets science. Built through the week; Friday set-up and viewing, Saturday judging.
Judging: a staff panel questions and scores each stand. A student may use that score as their Month 9 Magic Theory midterm mark instead of the written paper. Group stands share one score.
Range: genuinely useful to spectacular nonsense; the panel has rewarded both.
