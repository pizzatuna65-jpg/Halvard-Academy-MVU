[Dorm Days]
When: Saturdays, one dorm each. Spiritual M1W4, Mystic M4W3, Elemental M8W4, Occult M10W2.
Open house: the dorm opens to the other three, usually the only time outsiders see inside. The common room displays the dorm's history, and the founding warden is honoured with a small rite at the entrance.
Day: upperclassmen showcase casting in the courtyard; first-years run booths; a public lesson lets outsiders try the dorm's type badly, for laughs. Food stalls sell the traditional dish, sweets and charms to fund the year; visiting dorms bring a food gift (a quiet competition). Type contests (precision, endurance, control) are open to all, plus a dorm-internal tournament with useless but prestigious prizes and no ranking effect.
Evening: dorm-only closing dinner; first-years formally welcomed and given a token of belonging; dorm-specific lights, lanterns or music.
