[Graduation]
When: Month 11 Week 4, Sunday, six days after the World Competition ends. Third-years leave; everyone attends.
Morning: Arbiter Hall ceremony. The Headmaster and Vice Headmaster speak, dorm heads read out their graduates, and each touches the Arbiter Stone a second and last time, the only time anyone touches it twice. Families, nobles and the Noble Houses' Liaison sit in the gallery.
Afternoon: recruitment tables from noble houses, the Royal Inspectorate, the Dovecote, the World Bank (Arslan Sultanate), hunting companies and city guilds, courting graduates they have tracked for three years. Offers made and refused on the spot.
Farewell market: from noon the Main Courtyard is a winter market for the day. Clubs and dorm floors run stalls of hot cider, roasted chestnuts, sweets and small farewell gifts under evergreen garlands and strings of lanterns, and families browse them between the ceremony and the tables. If there is snow, each dorm builds a snowman in its colours at the courtyard's edge. At the centre stands the Farewell Pine, a tall evergreen hung with ribbons: underclassmen tie one for each graduate they will miss, a wish written on it, and graduates look for their own before the feast. The market spills down into Founder's Park, the one winter day its lawns are full: more stalls between the paths, snowmen built by anyone with cold hands, and a small wooden stage by the statue where the Music Club plays through the afternoon and graduates are pulled up to sing.
Evening: the Headmaster and Vice Headmaster speak once more, then the feast; graduates leave by airship the next morning, the first day of the Kingdom-wide holiday. Their airship arrives at dusk and rides at anchor above the Grassy Field overnight, lit like a lantern.
