[Graduation]
When: Month 11 Week 4, Sunday, the day after the World Competition ends (the national four fly home overnight). Third-years leave; everyone attends.
Morning: Arbiter Hall ceremony. The Headmaster speaks, dorm heads read out their graduates, and each touches the Arbiter Stone a second and last time, the only time anyone touches it twice. Families, nobles and the Noble Houses' Liaison sit in the gallery.
Afternoon: recruitment tables from noble houses, the Royal Inspectorate, the Dovecote, the Bank, hunting companies and city guilds, courting graduates they have tracked for three years. Offers made and refused on the spot.
Evening: feast; graduates leave by airship the next morning, the first day of the Kingdom-wide holiday.
