[Forest] Wide old woodland at the farthest edge of the grounds, past the far slope of the Hills (a quarter of an hour from the field, twenty-five minutes along the lake shore). Creature Studies fieldwork, foraging, walking, and where students go when they want to not be found. Students leave a bite before eating there (Eating in the Forest superstition). Creature Studies teaches there and patrols watch its edge. A deer path, half an hour's walk, leads deep in to the Forest Clearing and its Elder Oak, the one bright place inside it.
Feel: old growth, muffled, watched.
