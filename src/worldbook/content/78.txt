[Boathouse and Lake] Vast Open water for rowing, swimming and the Fishing Club. The boathouse stores the craft and doubles as a quiet place to sit. Beside the boathouse stands the Fishing Club's Fishing House, with its own dock. Sound carries strangely across the water: a far-bank conversation arrives too clearly. Home of the Crying on the Water story; the boathouse refuses bookings after dark. Willow Island sits in the middle of the lake, reached only by boat.
Feel: wet timber, cold water.
