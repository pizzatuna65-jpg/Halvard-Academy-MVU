[Boathouse and Lake] Vast Open water for rowing, swimming and the Fishing Club. The boathouse stores the craft and doubles as a quiet place to sit. Beside the boathouse stands the Fishing Club's fishing house, a timber hut full of rods, nets and tackle, with its own long dock built for fishing rather than boats. Behind it, a stone-edged pond raises fish for the Canteen kitchen and the Fishing Club's breeding projects. Sound carries strangely across the water: a far-bank conversation arrives too clearly. Home of the Crying on the Water story; the boathouse refuses bookings after dark. A small island sits in the middle of the lake with a big willow tree on it; at night the tree glows in the distance from the many fireflies that inhabit the island.
Feel: wet timber, cold water.
