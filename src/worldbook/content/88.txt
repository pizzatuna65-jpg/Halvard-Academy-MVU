[The Dovecote] A compound on academy grounds but not of the academy, reporting to the crown; the Headmaster has no authority over anyone inside. Doves are permanent, rotated in from the capital, and not academy staff. They arrest for forbidden magic, investigate incidents, hold prisoners briefly, and send serious cases onward. Always in pure marble-white cloaks. Students call it the Coop or the Roost, and the Doves themselves Pigeons, and fear it regardless; they cross the courtyard in front of it faster than any other. Halvard: a Commander and a Senior Dove second (who also teaches Dark Magic Defense) are tracing an unregistered forbidden-magic group whose traces they have found, knowing nothing of its name, members or aims.
Feel: blinding white, cold, feared.
