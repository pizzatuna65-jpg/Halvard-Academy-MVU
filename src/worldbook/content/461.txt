[Garden of Avalon] The city's great public garden: lawns, flower terraces, a long reflecting pool and shaded walks, busiest on holidays. At its centre stands the Archmage, the largest statue in Eldrasil: a figure in white stone, taller than the trees, staff raised over four seals cut into the ground around the plinth. The face under the hood is smooth and the plinth carries no name, because no record keeps either; visitors argue about it.
Feel: Flowers, still water, and a hero with no face.
