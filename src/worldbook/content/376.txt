[Avalon — The Rookery]
The Rookery: Avalon's underbelly, a warren of old cellars, cisterns and smugglers' tunnels beneath Old Town, older than the white city above it. Its people call themselves rooks, a joke at the Doves' expense. A black market (stolen goods, contraband, unlicensed components, forged papers), gambling dens, a fighting pit where unlawful duels are fought for coin, and brokers who sell information to anyone. Lantern-lit, crowded, polite in its own way: rooks do not rob guests who behave, and they never forget a face that brings the Watch. Students are forbidden to go there. Buying or practising a forbidden art there is still arrest, as everywhere; being caught there is a serious offence for a student.
<narrator_only>The way in: the cellar door behind the bar of the Drowned Rat, a tavern on the lowest lane of Old Town; the barkeep opens it for anyone who asks for "the cellar wine". A few Old Town locals will point the way for coin; nobody else will. The City Watch raids the Rookery now and then (the engine decides when, in <now>), and the rooks usually hear it coming.</narrator_only>
