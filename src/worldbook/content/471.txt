[The Challenge Crossing] The great five-way crossing on Victors' Avenue, the main street from the castle hill, lined with snow-laden sakura and a stone lantern for every Alpha. When the bell rings, the crowd crosses in every direction at once; on the clerks' platform in the middle, anyone may challenge anyone to a fair contest, from duels to eating matches, and a Tournament Office clerk records the result. A Yozakuran never refuses.
Feel: A bell, a thousand people moving at once, and someone always shouting a challenge.
