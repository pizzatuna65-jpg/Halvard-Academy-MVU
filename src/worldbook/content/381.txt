[Airship: Cargo Hold] The bottom of the ship, down a steep ladder from the aft end of the Cabin Deck: a long dim hold of crates in cargo nets, racks of luggage and gear strapped to the ribs of the hull. Cold, and the loudest place aboard: the engines' hum comes up through the floor. Passengers may come down for their luggage. A Yozakuran quartermaster keeps the hold and grumbles at everyone who does, but sends no one away. The teachers rarely come down, which makes it the most private place on the ship and the reason couples, plotters and the seasick end up here. What it carries changes with the route: tents, cookfires and camp gear on the way to the forest, the same gear and muddier on the way home; the academy's banners and spare uniforms on the way to Avalon; on the way back from Sunreach, more luggage than left Halvard, and the crates of what the teachers confiscated. A door at the bow end of the hold leads to the engine room: crew only.
