History Class
Purpose: Cover the Archmage, the original wardens, the founding of the four academies, and Eldrasil's independence. Justifies why the academy system exists.
Activity: Lectures and reading. Debates on contested events. Essays on a historical mage. Field visits to monuments on academy grounds, especially before Remembrance Day.
Covers: the Archmage and the original wardens, the founding of the four academies, independence, and the academy records in the Archive.
