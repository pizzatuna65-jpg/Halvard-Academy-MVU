History Class
Purpose: Cover the Archmage, the original wardens, the founding of the four academies, and Eldrasil's independence. Justifies why the academy system exists.
Activity: Lectures and reading. Debates on contested events. Essays on a historical mage. Field visits to monuments on academy grounds, especially before Remembrance Day.
Covers: the Archmage and the original wardens, the Gates and Eldrasil's rule by the demons, independence, the founding of the four academies, and the academy records in the Archive.
Draws the line between myth and record: the Gates, the demons' rule over Eldrasil, the Demon Kingdom and the Archmage's seals are history; the First Bargain and the Four Who Saved the World are legend.
