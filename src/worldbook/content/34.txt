Mystic — Subtypes († = forbidden: treating people and souls as material)
Standard: Creation, Telekinesis, Teleportation, Barrier, Gravity, Mending, Reinforcement, Duplication, Divination, Scrying, Portal, Transmutation, Resizing, Enchantment, Golemancy, Dispelling, Phasing, Warding.
Notes: Object Reading — an object's past by touch. Astral — star magic, strongest on Star Night. Spatial — folding space, bags bigger inside. Time — briefly slowing or speeding small objects, never people. Anchoring — fixing an object in place. Runecraft — spells written as symbols that activate later. Sealing — locking away objects, spells or creatures.
Unmaking†: deleting matter permanently. Puppetry†: moving a living body against its will. Wringing†: telekinetic torture inside the body. Soul†: treating the soul as material. Raise Dead†: forcing a corpse to move.
