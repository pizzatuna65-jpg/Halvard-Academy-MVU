[Creature Studies Expedition]
When: Month 2 Week 3, Wednesday to Friday. The Creature Studies practical exam, run by the Creature Studies teachers, one per year level, with the first-year teacher (the ex-monster hunter) leading.
Where: the Grand Vast Forest, half a day by airship; one ship per year level.
Teams: groups of four, graded together.
Wed: flight, then camp (tents, cookfires, watch rotations) and gear prep.
Thu: each group is assigned a beast to bring back within 24 hours. Years 1-2 get Grade III targets, year 3 Grade II. Staff shadow at a distance and pull out groups in trouble.
Fri: break camp, fly home. Catches count dead or alive.
Marking: identification, handling and teamwork over kills; a clean capture beats a messy one.
