[AVALON INDEX — the capital. Each place has its own entry when it comes up.]
Avalon is the capital of Eldrasil and its largest city, some two hours from Halvard by airship. A round city of white stone on a gentle hill, inside one great white wall with four gates; the Great Gate in the south faces the Skyport. Past the wall there are no more walls: the city is laid out in rings by its streets alone, each ring a little higher than the one outside it, rising to Eldrasil Castle on its hill, which can be seen from every street.
Rings, from the castle out: the Noble Ring, the houses of the noble families on the castle's terraces; the Crown Ring, where the kingdom is run, around Crown Square; and the Lower City, the widest ring by far, where most of Avalon lives, works, shops and plays. Great avenues run from the gates to Crown Square, and the Ring Boulevard circles the Lower City just inside the wall; between them lie homes, workshops and the quarters visitors come for.
The White Line: a band of white marble set into every street where the Noble Ring begins. The Royal Guard stands on it. Nobody crosses without a royal summons or a noble's household badge, and the guards do not argue; the Doves are never far away. Students never cross it, except the team that wins the Kingdom Competition, received by the King at the castle.
The city was rebuilt in white after the War of Independence. Old Town alone keeps the grey stone of the years before, left as it was on purpose. Royal banners, deep blue and gold, fly from the castle towers; on Crowning Day they hang along every avenue.
The Herald Spires: seven tall white spires across the city, the city-sized version of the academy's announcement pillars. Each carries the same broadcast at once, live and never recorded: royal proclamations, the noon news, a bell and a few bars of music on every hour, and on Crowning Day the King's own voice. At night they glow faintly. The Great Spire in the north is the one the public may climb.
The city map (parts, then their places): Eldrasil Castle: Eldrasil Castle (the royal seat on the hill), Noble Ring (the noble houses on the castle terraces). Crown Square: Crown Square (the great square, the King's speech), Grand Cathedral (the kingdom's cathedral), The Chancery (the crown's offices and courts), High Dovecote (the Doves' tower), Royal Bank (the kingdom's bank). Great Gate: Skyport (airship masts outside the Great Gate), Gilded Arcade (glass-roofed shopping arcades). Lantern Quarter: Lantern Market (the biggest market, under lanterns), Spellwright Row (the magic trades). Old Town: Old Town (the grey old quarter), Skywheel Fair (the fairground and its giant wheel), The Great Spire (the tallest Herald Spire, open to climb). Garden of Avalon: Garden of Avalon (the great garden and the Archmage statue), Grand Theatre (the domed opera house). Arena Quarter: Royal Arena (the Kingdom Competition's stadium), Whitestone Hotel (where the four academies' teams stay).

Getting around: on foot, or by hire carriage from the stands at the Skyport, Crown Square and every quarter: half the time, for coin. Bracelet points do not work in Avalon; students carry coin, changed at the Banking House before they leave.
