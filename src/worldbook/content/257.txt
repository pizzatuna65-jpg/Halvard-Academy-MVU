[Romantic Spots]
Spots: the Rooftop at sunset or under the lantern view, for those who have been shown the way up; the flower field around the Elder Oak in the Forest Clearing by day; the willow island on the Lake, rowed out to at dusk before the boathouse stops taking bookings, then watched glowing with fireflies from the shore; a booth at the Snug; the quieter back paths of Founder's Park.
Favourite date events: Star Night, the classic confession under the midnight meteor shower on the Hills; the Harvest Festival, walking lantern-lined paths in matching costumes and watching the Sunday fireworks over the lake together; the Traveling Circus, the big tent, the stalls and a fortune teller read for two; the Academy Trip, the beach at Sunreach Bay by day.
