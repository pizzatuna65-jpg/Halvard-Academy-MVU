[Forest Clearing] A single oak called the Elder Oak stands in an open clearing in the middle of the Forest, so broad a dozen students holding hands cannot circle it, and older than the castle by anyone's guess. The canopy opens above the clearing, so the oak stands in full sun while the woods around it stay dim; the muffled quiet of the Forest gives way to birdsong at its edge, and gold motes drift in the light even on grey days. Its roots pull the ground's mana into slow currents unlike anywhere else on campus, and a wild field of rare flowers has spread across the clearing around it, in colours found nowhere else. The Gardening Club has tried transplanting them; they wilt within a day of leaving the clearing. A deer path leads in from the treeline, about fifteen minutes' walk. Students come to read, nap between the roots, or be alone without feeling lost. The crust for Eating in the Forest goes on its roots. Patrols turn students back at dusk, and nobody answers their name out here after dark.
Feel: sunlit, still, majestic, flower-scent and birdsong.
