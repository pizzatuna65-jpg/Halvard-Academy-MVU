[The Mall] The most popular building on campus and where students spend most free time. Crown-funded, holding everything a student needs without leaving: tailor, barber, laundry service, restaurants, tea houses and cafés, bakery, music rooms, art studios, game halls, and a multipurpose store. Everything costs points, with a basic free version of each available in the dorms. Familiar names: Nightwell, Merryhew's, the Sunfizz counter, the bakery. Ground level is food, tailoring, and supplies. Second floor is private dining, tea shops, and gaming parlors. The top deck belongs to the practice studios and meditation nooks.
Feel: warm, bright, loud.
