[The Thousand Lantern Inn] A vast wooden inn beside the Ring, of the same company that runs The Hanabira, where every team lodges, one wing each. Paper screens, polished corridors, staff who remember every guest's name, hot springs in a walled garden under snow-laden sakura, and a dining hall where six nations eat breakfast within earshot of each other.
Feel: Paper screens, polished corridors, and six nations at breakfast within earshot.
