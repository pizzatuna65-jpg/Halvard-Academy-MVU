[Yozakura team]
Team: Hibiki Kurenai (Attack, Y2, Human, M, wild red hair, laid-back, Captain, the team's ace); Jin Tachibana (Defense, Y1, wolf B, M, grey, stoic); Nanami Oribe (Support, Y3, cat B, F, black, dry); Sora Kanzaki (Healing, Y2, deer B, M, light brown, fussy).
Kit: black kimonos with red accents; the captain's band on Hibiki's left bicep; every member carries a katana, Hibiki's a wooden one.
Coach: Tetsuya Mibu, a white-haired badger Beastkin swordmaster who lost the Alpha final twice and laughs about it; coaches with a bamboo stick in one hand and a teapot in the other.
Plays: head-on. Yozakura meets every challenge where it is offered and never refuses a fair fight, which wins it most matches and lets a patient team bait it. Everything is built to get Hibiki within reach of a swing without wearing him out first.
Before campaign Year 1: champions, through a field that included Eldrasil's Lucius Vortigern and Gareth Valkaryn; Hibiki, then a first-year, struck the deciding blow, grinning the whole time.
Nanami Oribe: third year, age 20, cat Beastkin. Short black bob, gold eyes, black ears and a white-tipped tail, a small war drum slung on her back. Spiritual: her drumbeat carries strength and speed to her team, and its off-beat throws the enemy's timing. The team's unflappable, dry-humoured older sister; her drum is what keeps Hibiki's legs moving.
Sora Kanzaki: second year, age 19, deer Beastkin. Slight and soft-spoken, light brown hair, small antlers and a short white tail, sleeves tied back with a cord. Spiritual (Healing): heals by touch, very fast, and sets a bone mid-fight without blinking. Gentle, fussy, devoted to tea; scolds Hibiki for skipping breakfast before every match.
Jin Tachibana: first year, age 18, wolf Beastkin. Broad-shouldered and very still, short grey hair, grey ears and a thick tail held low, a long katana he almost never draws. Spiritual (Endurance and Will): when he plants his feet, spells break on him like waves on a rock, but while he holds he cannot move. Silent, proud and stubbornly courteous; he stands between Hibiki and anything fast, and thinks his captain is far too calm for his own good. From campaign Year 3 he captains Yozakura's four.
