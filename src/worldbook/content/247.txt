SUNREACH BAY INDEX — the city of the Academy Trip. Each place has its own entry when it comes up.
One city around a turquoise bay. By day a seaside paradise; from 18:00 to 06:00 the same streets are lit by rune-light: glowing coloured glass signs, painted banners under rune lamps, lanterns. No electricity anywhere.
Skydock: Skydock (airship masts, customs, the cliff road up to the Lighthouse).
Fisherman's Wharf: Fish Market (the seafood heart, grills by day, lantern stalls by night), Sea Dock (fishing fleet and the tour boats to the open sea).
Coral Strand: The Hanabira (Halvard's Yozakuran hotel, rooftop hot spring), Coral Beach (white sand, volleyball, fire dancers at night).
Sunpier: Sunpier (amusement pier, the Gull Run coaster), Tidehouse Aquarium (glass tunnel, the old turtle, violet at night).
Sunreach Crossing: Sunreach Crossing (six-street crossroads, glowing banners, game halls), Velvet Lanes (tiny stacked bars, singing rooms, the night market).
Casino Row: The Golden Tide (the great casino and its fountain lake), Plaza Dourada (pastel colonial square, custard tarts).
Cliffside Terraces: The Terraces (the islanders' stacked hillside town on stilts), Skyrail (red cable cars from the Crossing to the summit), Old Fort (ruin on the summit, stars at night).
Turquoise Lagoon: The Lagoon (warm turquoise water, rafts, a bar on stilts), Reef Gardens (glass-bottom boats, pearl divers), Shell Cove (hidden pink-sand cove).
West Cliffs: The Lighthouse (Norvaine keeper, up the cliff road from the Skydock), Sunset Cliffs (sunset, cliff diving), Glowtide Grotto (sea cave, glowing cavern and pool).
