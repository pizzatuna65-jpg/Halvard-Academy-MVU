[Academy Trip]
When: Month 11 Week 3, Monday to Wednesday. All three years; the third-years' last event as students.
Where: Sunreach Bay, half a day by airship. Whole beachfront hotels booked, four to a room; the rest is free time.
Rules: teachers in the same hotels set a curfew; leave the hotel only in groups of four. The day side is allowed; the night side is out of bounds, which some students ignore every year.
Money: points do not work abroad; this is what a year of saving is for, and Sunreach is expensive.
Mood: students return tired, sunburnt and poorer than planned.
