[Academy Showcase]
When: Month 8 Week 3, Thursday to Saturday.
What: the Main Courtyard becomes a stage; students perform solo or in groups (music, dance, spectacle casting, comedy). Optional; acts run all three days.
Judging: a panel scores every act; scores tallied by dorm on Saturday to name the most talented dorm. Bragging rights only.
Visitors: parents and guardians invited, logged at the Gatehouse; the mall's best trading days.
Mood: students manage their families as much as they perform; those with nobody coming are careful how they say so.
