[Entrance Event]
When: Month 1 Week 1 Monday
- Sorting: new students place a hand on the Arbiter Stone to decide their dorm.
- Tour: each new student is grouped with 3 other newcomers and 1 senior to tour the whole campus all day.
- Club promotion: stands across the academy; registration closes at 5pm. Held on sorting day on purpose, so clubs recruit before dorm loyalty settles.
- Seniors show new students their rooms inside the dorms.
- Entrance Feast, 7pm, Ring Dining Hall head master and vice head master speech before dining.
