[Glowtide Grotto] A sea cave in the cliff foot. A boatman rows visitors in through a low arch at the water line and along a winding tunnel where the water glows blue wherever an oar touches it, until the tunnel opens into a great cavern. There the boat ties up on a broad shore of smooth rock that can be walked on all the way round. In the middle lies a still round pool whose water glitters like scattered stars, and high above, the whole ceiling is covered in glowing plants, hanging moss and vines that shed a soft blue-green light over everything. Voices echo; nobody talks loudly for long. Boatmen charge a few coins and wait as long as you like.
Feel: blue-green glow, echo, cool stone, glittering water.
