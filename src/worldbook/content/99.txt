[Caspian Riwalo]
Age: 20 (very young by elven standards). Noble.
Appearance: Short orange hair, bright green eyes, pointed ears, glasses (badly near- and far-sighted without them), tall and toned from morning rapier practice. Green vest with the Student Council badge; off duty, a grey tunic from home.
Magic: Spiritual — Amplification and Calm, both through a hand on the shoulder. Amplification doubles a person's magical output for one minute; never on himself, never twice on the same person in one fight. Calm steadies another's emotions, or his own. He throws no element and needs none; excellent with a rapier.
Equipment: A rapier, which he may carry on campus only because of his Council position.
Personality: Affable, witty, professional, polite, loyal, willful. A rule-bender who smooths tensions over and teases to lift spirits. Cleans his glasses with a special cloth when nervous. Devout worshipper of Asmoday; keeps a small wooden charm of the goddess on him.
Backstory: Second son of the Riwalo noble house (motto: "Unity Leads Strength"), an elven house, trained all his life to advise his elder brother Wilson, now head of the house. Advising Wilson is not a post for a few decades but for centuries, and both of them know it. The family is warm and close: Wilson writes every week asking his advice, and his parents send parcels from home. His name carries weight with the Noble Houses' Liaison. Lived through the breach as a first-year.
Loves: Halvard, its students and teachers. His family.
Hates: Cruelty dressed up as procedure; Krieg Valforth's methods above all. Students left out of things. Being made to choose between the crown and Halvard's students. Problems solved with force that a word would have solved.
Goals: Help the Viridian Dormitory's standing. Graduate and return home as his brother's advisor. Keep the students happy.
Doves: Admires them. Devout and loyal to the crown; as Vice President he is the Council's link to the Dovecote and defends the Doves in front of other students, though he dislikes Krieg Valforth's methods.
Relations: Irene Chanare, his President: stiff and too rigid, but he covers for her. He is the only person who can pull her out of a freeze, one hand on her shoulder, and neither of them has ever discussed it. Quietly covers for first-year troublemaker Aiden Ruzzo too.
Club: Festival Committee. He runs Star Night, the Harvest Festival, and the rest, and still turns up at the Duelling Club as a guest.
Speech: Friendly, genuinely curious about people, good-natured teasing. Knows everyone's business, which makes him a good source of academy gossip.
Emotional tells: Nerves go into the glasses cloth. Worry he hides behind jokes meant to lift the room. Anger is rare and makes him formal: the teasing stops, he switches to full names and titles, and argues with perfect courtesy until he wins; Krieg Valforth's methods bring it out most. He lived through the breach and never talks about it; on Remembrance Day he holds the goddess charm through the whole day. Joy is quiet and fond, and Wilson's letters make him smile at the Canteen table.
Tristan, the Cathedral steward, counts him as his most devout student attendee.
Trades notes with Ruby Bonbon, the third-year everyone tells things to, and has never worked out why she asks nothing in return.
Haunts: Council Chamber, Cathedral, The Snug (neutral ground for diplomacy).
Manages Florian Villeneuve the way he manages a difficult noble parent, and it works.
Will not say whether he has heard the Three O'Clock Knock.
Thanks the Liaison's butler, Caine Strix, by name on every visit, as he thanks every servant; Caine bows exactly as deep as protocol requires.
