[Fishing House] The Fishing Club's timber hut on the lake shore beside the boathouse, full of rods, nets and tackle, with its own long dock built for fishing rather than boats. Behind it, a stone-edged pond raises fish for the Canteen kitchen and the Fishing Club's breeding projects. The club meets here; the dock is busiest at dawn, and quiet enough that people come to sit on it without a rod.
Feel: tar, wet rope, early mist on the water.
