[Campus Map — Halvard Academy]
Adjacent places (reached directly from each other):
- Perimeter: Gatehouse/Reception (with its visiting parlour) – Main Courtyard (up the main avenue), Commissary, Medical Centre, Mail Tower, Groundskeeper's Lodge.
- Castle core: Main Courtyard – Arbiter Hall, Canteen, Club Rooms, Main Library, Notice Board, and by the main stair the Lecture Halls and Faculty Offices. Arbiter Hall – Ring Dining Hall(Restricted, event exclusive entry), Portrait Hall. Portrait Hall – Bell Tower. Bell Tower – Rooftop (out of bounds). Club Rooms – Student Council Chamber, Announcement Room, old amphitheatre. Canteen – main kitchen (behind it). Lecture Halls – Seminar Rooms, Potion Halls, Workshop. Main Library – Study Rooms, Archive. Archive – Restricted Section. Faculty Offices – Staff Room, Headmaster's Office. Warden's Office (own corridor off Floor 1) – Seal Chamber. Royal Inspectorate (Floor 1 wing, near the Warden's corridor). Detention Tower (own spiral stair from Floor 1). Castle Floor 1 – Staff Quarters (a separate building, by covered walk).
- Castle levels (Floor 1 is ground level; floors are linked by the main stair, and each floor climbed adds about a minute):
  Undercroft: Workshop beneath the Lecture Halls; Archive beneath the Library, with the Restricted Section below it; the Seal Chamber deepest of all, reached only by the Warden's stair.
  Floor 1: Arbiter Hall (its vault rises through Floor 3), Ring Dining Hall, Canteen with the main kitchen behind it, Main Library reading rooms, Club Rooms corridor with the Student Council Chamber at its end and the Announcement Room and old amphitheatre off it, the Notice Board in the entrance hall at the foot of the main stair; the Warden's Office down its own corridor; the Royal Inspectorate's bannered door in the same wing; the foot of the Detention Tower stair.
  Floor 2: first-year Lecture Halls, Portrait Hall gallery along the Arbiter Hall's upper level, Study Rooms on the Library's galleries.
  Floor 3: second-year Lecture Halls with their own Study Rooms, Seminar Rooms, the Library's shadowed upper stacks.
  Floor 4: third-year Lecture Halls with their own Study Rooms, the four Potion Halls at the base of their towers.
  Floor 5: Faculty Offices, Staff Room; the Headmaster's Office in its turret, up a short stair.
  Rooftop: flat lead roofs, the Bell Tower belfry, the Potion Hall tower tops, the top of the Detention Tower.
  Lockers line the corridors on every Lecture Hall floor.
- Announcement Pillars (live broadcast from the Announcement Room) stand across the whole academy.
- Dormitories: Main Courtyard – Fire, Light, Sky and Viridian Dormitories, four separate buildings around it; each dormitory – its Common Room, Laundry, Bathhouse. Fire Dormitory – Sky Dormitory, Broken Statue. Sky Dormitory – The Mall, Cathedral, Broken Statue. Viridian Dormitory – Arbiter Hall, Staff Quarters, Gardens, Light Dormitory. Light Dormitory – Medical Centre, Founder's Park, Archery Range.
- Student life: Main Courtyard – The Mall, Medical Centre. The Mall – Medical Centre, Commissary (each its own building, three or four minutes' walk from it), Banking House, Cathedral. Medical Centre – Mail Tower, Commissary. Commissary – Banking House. Banking House – Meditation Rooms. Mail Tower – Groundskeeper's Lodge.
- Outer grounds: Main Courtyard – Founder's Park. Founder's Park – Light Dormitory, Medical Centre, Mail Tower, Groundskeeper's Lodge, Combat Grounds, Sports Field, Archery Range. Arbiter Hall – Staff Quarters. Staff Quarters – Gardens. Gardens – Menagerie, Archery Range. Archery Range – Menagerie, Gymnasium, Sports Field. Gymnasium – Menagerie, Sports Field, Swimming Pool (outdoor). Sports Field – Combat Grounds, Sparring Pavilion. Combat Grounds – Sparring Pavilion, Groundskeeper's Lodge. Spirit Path (the footpath through the trees east of the Gymnasium) – Gymnasium, Menagerie, Sports Field.
- Far edge: the long path out from Main Courtyard, past Arbiter Hall and the Staff Quarters, climbs to the Grassy Field/Hills. Hills – Observation Tower, Old Hut, Boathouse/Lake, Fishing House, Forest. Gardens – Boathouse/Lake. Boathouse/Lake – Fishing House (three minutes along the shore from the boathouse), Willow Island (by boat only), Forest. Old Hut – Forest. Forest – Forest Clearing.
- Set apart from the core: the Dovecote, Cathedral and Noble Houses' Liaison stand together on one side of the grounds, with the Meditation Rooms below them; between them and the Fire Dormitory, in the trees, stands the Broken Statue. The Dovecote, Cathedral and Liaison sit on academy land the academy does not control, as does the Royal Inspectorate's suite inside the castle (Floor 1). Dovecote – Cathedral, Noble Houses' Liaison, Broken Statue. Cathedral – Noble Houses' Liaison, Broken Statue, Meditation Rooms. Noble Houses' Liaison – Meditation Rooms.
Walking: <now> gives the minutes from where {{user}} stands ("Walks from here"); advance the clock by the walk. Adjacent places are reached directly, usually in four or five minutes on foot, and nobody passes through the Main Courtyard to reach a neighbour. The far grounds (the Lake, the Grassy Field and Hills, the Forest) are wide open country, much farther than the map makes them look, and far from each other too; nothing out there is next door to anything else except the Fishing House, three minutes along the shore from the boathouse. The long paths out are home of the Polite Walker story. A described speed spell can shorten the actual route according to its effect and mana cost; never impose the walking estimate on magical travel.
The Gatehouse is the only way in or out. A dormitory admits only its own students.
