[Campus Map — Halvard Academy]
Adjacent places (reached directly from each other):
- Perimeter: Gatehouse/Reception (with its visiting parlour) – Main Courtyard, Groundskeeper's Lodge.
- Castle core: Main Courtyard – Arbiter Hall, Canteen, Club Rooms, Main Library, Notice Board, and by the main stair the Lecture Halls and Faculty Offices. Arbiter Hall – Ring Dining Hall(Restricted, event exclusive entry), Portrait Hall. Portrait Hall – Bell Tower. Bell Tower – Rooftop (out of bounds). Club Rooms – Student Council Chamber, Announcement Room, old amphitheatre. Canteen – main kitchen (behind it). Lecture Halls – Seminar Rooms, Potion Halls, Workshop. Main Library – Study Rooms, Archive. Archive – Restricted Section. Faculty Offices – Staff Room, Headmaster's Office. Warden's Office (own corridor off Floor 1) – Seal Chamber. Royal Inspectorate (Floor 1 wing, near the Warden's corridor). Detention Tower (own spiral stair from Floor 1). Castle Floor 1 – Staff Quarters (a separate building, by covered walk).
- Castle levels (Floor 1 is ground level; floors are linked by the main stair, and each floor climbed adds about a minute):
  Undercroft: Workshop beneath the Lecture Halls; Archive beneath the Library, with the Restricted Section below it; the Seal Chamber deepest of all, reached only by the Warden's stair.
  Floor 1: Arbiter Hall (its vault rises through Floor 3), Ring Dining Hall, Canteen with the main kitchen behind it, Main Library reading rooms, Club Rooms corridor with the Student Council Chamber at its end and the Announcement Room and old amphitheatre off it, the Notice Board in the entrance hall at the foot of the main stair; the Warden's Office down its own corridor; the Royal Inspectorate's bannered door in the same wing; the foot of the Detention Tower stair.
  Floor 2: first-year Lecture Halls, Portrait Hall gallery along the Arbiter Hall's upper level, Study Rooms on the Library's galleries.
  Floor 3: second-year Lecture Halls, Seminar Rooms, the Library's shadowed upper stacks.
  Floor 4: third-year Lecture Halls, the four Potion Halls at the base of their towers.
  Floor 5: Faculty Offices, Staff Room; the Headmaster's Office in its turret, up a short stair.
  Rooftop: flat lead roofs, the Bell Tower belfry, the Potion Hall tower tops, the top of the Detention Tower.
  Lockers line the corridors on every Lecture Hall floor.
- Announcement Pillars (live broadcast from the Announcement Room) stand across the whole academy.
- Dormitories: Main Courtyard – Fire, Light, Sky and Viridian Dormitories, four separate buildings around it; each dormitory – its Common Room, Laundry, Bathhouse.
- Student life: Main Courtyard – The Mall, Mail Tower. The Mall – Medical Centre, Commissary (each its own building beside it), Banking House, Meditation Rooms/Spirit House.
- Outer grounds: Main Courtyard – Founder's Park, Combat Grounds, Sports Field, Gardens, Grassy Field/Hills. Founder's Park – Gardens, Sports Field. Sports Field – Gymnasium, Archery Range. Combat Grounds – Sparring Pavilion. Gymnasium – Swimming Pool (outdoor). Gardens – Menagerie. Hills – Observation Tower, Old Hut.
- Far edge: Gardens – Boathouse/Lake – Forest. Hills – Forest. Forest – Forest Clearing.
- Set apart from the core: the Dovecote, Cathedral and Noble Houses' Liaison stand together on one side of the grounds; between them and the Fire Dormitory, in the trees, stands the Broken Statue. All three sit on academy land the academy does not control, as does the Royal Inspectorate's suite inside the castle (Floor 1).
Club venues: Duelling – Combat Grounds (headquarters: Sparring Pavilion); Soccer – Sports Field; Running, Walking – Hills; Gardening – Gardens; Beast Handling – Menagerie; Fishing – Lake; Alchemy, Brewing – Potion Halls; Smithing – Workshop; Library Assistants – Main Library; Warding Study – Seminar Rooms; Festival Committee – Council Chamber; Groundskeeping – Groundskeeper's Lodge; Cooking – Canteen's dedicated kitchen section; Medical Centre Volunteers – Medical Centre; Newspaper – Announcement Room; Theatre – Club Rooms for meetings and rehearsals, old indoor amphitheatre for performances; Music, Card, Board Game, Divination, Tailoring, Drawing, Calligraphy, Tea, Specialized Magic – Club Rooms.
Walking times are measured FROM Main Courtyard, which is the navigation anchor, not a required stop: castle core and dormitories under 5 min; Mall and services 5; Founder's Park 5; Broken Statue 5; Combat Grounds, Sparring Pavilion and Sports Field 10; Gardens, Menagerie, Hills 15; Old Hut 20; Boathouse and Lake 20; Forest treeline 25; Forest Clearing 40.
Movement: adjacent places are reached directly, usually in four or five minutes. Characters do not pass through the Courtyard to reach a neighbour. Crossing between distant clusters does take real time; say so rather than skipping it.
The Gatehouse is the only way in or out. A dormitory admits only its own students.
