[Mana Grid] The academy runs on a grid drawn from the flow the seal already collects. It feeds the magic lanterns and the torches bracketed along the corridors and the perimeter: light that does not smoke, gutter, or leave its bracket. Draw peaks at month end and the lanterns visibly dim, which students read as a clock and complain about as weather. Nothing on the grid records or transmits anything; the Announcement Pillars are a separate sound working that only carries live voice. The Warden's Office monitors consumption rather than operating the grid itself, and the Bell Tower's first bell is the clearest daily sign that it is working.
