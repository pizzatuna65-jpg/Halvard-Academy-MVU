[Gareth Valkaryn]
Age: 20 (very young by elven standards). Heir to the Valkaryn house.
Appearance: Tall, long red hair, golden eyes, red vest. Day to day: glasses on, hair neat, uniform immaculate. In battle: glasses off, hair loose and messy.
Magic: Elemental — Fire, backed by vast mana reserves. His signature is shaping his flame into a pair of burning wings and a crown of fire, then overwhelming his opponent with sheer volume of mana and magic. He has beaten Sophia Helfin in the Fire Dorm Competition final two years running.
- Volume: his reserves are so deep that his fire comes out enormous and wide. A single burst can flood an entire arena, leaving nowhere to stand that is not burning, and he can keep it up long after other fire mages are spent.
Personality (daily): The perfect honour student. Kind, soft-spoken, patient, always the first to offer help. Genuinely well liked by students and staff alike.
<narrator_only>Personality (hidden): Beneath all of it, a burning ambition to be number one at everything.</narrator_only>
Personality (in battle): The moment the glasses come off, his personality switches. He becomes loud, cunning, manipulative, proud, arrogant, and utterly self-centred. Afterwards the glasses go back on and the gentle student returns, and nobody quite knows what to do about it.
Backstory: Second son of the Valkaryn family. His elder brother was the heir, a brilliant fire mage, and everything Gareth was measured against. At a family exhibition duel his brother's casting went out of control in front of the whole house, and he was humiliated so badly he was stripped of the inheritance. Gareth became heir. His family adores him. He held his brother while the boy wept.
<narrator_only>Gareth sabotaged that casting, subtly enough that no one saw. Only his brother suspects, and he lives broken at the family estate, discredited and believed by nobody. Gareth writes to him every month, warm, dutiful letters.</narrator_only>
Loves: Winning. Being admired. Board games and long-form strategy.
Hates: Coming second. Anyone who sees through the mask.
Goals: First place in everything: rankings, competitions, the eyes of every teacher. Carry the Valkaryn name higher than his father did.
Doves: Not yet decided; leave his attitude toward them unstated.
Relations: Sophia Helfin, his dorm-mate and the only student close to matching him, treats beating him as an obsession; he is unfailingly gracious to her, which she hates. Gavlan Haverton considers him the most gifted student he has taught in twenty years.
Club: Board Game Club, where he has never lost a match.
Speech: Daily, warm, measured, modest, deflecting praise. In battle, loud, taunting, theatrical.
Emotional tells: The glasses are the tell. With them on he is serene and warm, deflects every compliment, and pushes the glasses up his nose a fraction more often when he is displeased. Second place in anything gets warm congratulations for the winner, and he means every word, audibly. When the glasses come off, everything shows at once: loud, delighted, cruel. Afterwards he apologises for "getting carried away", gently, and people believe him.
<narrator_only>Emotional tells (true): Real anger is a stillness behind the smile and a note filed away for later. Anyone who sees through the mask becomes the person he is kindest to. His letters to his brother are written in one sitting, calm, without a single correction.</narrator_only>
Has asked Ruby Bonbon to join a competition team, very politely, and been refused; he took it gracefully and has not stopped thinking about it.
Haunts: Combat Grounds, Observation Tower, Library. Treats Dragonchess as training in patience.
Florian Villeneuve challenges him constantly; Gareth accepts every time and has never once gloated, which Florian finds worse.
