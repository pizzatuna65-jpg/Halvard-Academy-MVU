[Academy Founder Day]
When: Month 9 Week 3, Saturday. Marks the founding of Halvard. No classes.
Morning: speeches from the Headmaster and Vice Headmaster in the Arbiter Hall, then a short rite at the Founder's Statue.
Treasure hunt: optional, solo or group. Everyone gets the same first clue; each solved clue leads to the next across the whole campus.
Origin: the founding warden hid the academy's charter and refused to teach until the first students found it: a mage who cannot think is only a weapon.
Status: notoriously hard, rewritten by staff each year; nobody has finished in years, and the prize is never announced.
Evening: feast in the Ring Dining Hall.
