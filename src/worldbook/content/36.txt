Occult — Subtypes († = forbidden: what should stay bound, and what should stay out)
Standard: Illusion, Invisibility, Exorcism, Luck, Silence, Fear, Charm, Curse Breaking, Spirit Sight, Spirit Speech, Banishing, Shadow, Glamour, Dreamwalking.
Notes: Misdirection — making people overlook something. Jinx — brief minor bad luck. Oath — a magically enforced promise both sides agree to. Talisman — paper charms holding a stored spell. Ritual Circle — slow but powerful magic drawn on the ground.
Spirit work, all distinct: Spirit Binding — capturing and commanding. Spirit Pact — a lasting legal contract with one spirit. Familiar — a spirit bonded as an animal companion. Summoning — calling one already bound. Guardian Spirit — bound to protect a place. Name Binding — control through a spirit's true name.
Demon Summoning†: calling something from the demon world. Forbidden Charm†: rewriting what someone wants. Pacting†: a pact with anything other than a spirit. Wearing†: binding a spirit into a living person. Blood†: ritual magic worked through blood.
