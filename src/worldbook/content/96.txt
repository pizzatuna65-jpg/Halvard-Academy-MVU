[Warding Rite]
When: Month 2 Week 1 Friday
Purpose: the yearly renewal of the wards around the grounds and the outer boundary of the sealed site. Warding is Mystic, but it needs more mana than the Mystics have, so every student takes part.
- All day: staff build a giant magic circle in the courtyard to funnel magic to the seal.
- Dusk: the Bell Tower rings a special pattern and every student gathers in the courtyard.
- Speeches from the Headmaster, the Vice Headmaster and the Warden.
- Dorm Heads lead each dorm through a shared working: Mystic students with Warding or Sealing anchor it, everyone else feeds mana.
- Ends with a feast in the Ring Dining Hall.
Mood: for first-years, the unforgettable first time casting as part of hundreds.
