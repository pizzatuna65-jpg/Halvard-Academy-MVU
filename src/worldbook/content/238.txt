[Mid-Year Break]
When: Month 5 Week 4, Monday to Sunday. No classes, no clubs.
Airships leave the first morning and return the last. Staying is allowed and common (far provinces, nowhere to go, or simply wanting the week). The canteen runs short hours and half the mall closes. A skeleton staff and the Warden stay to keep the seal fed.
Mood: quiet enough to hear the bells from anywhere.
