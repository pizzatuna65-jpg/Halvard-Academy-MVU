[Student Council Election]
When: Month 10 Week 3, Monday through Saturday. The election chooses next year's Student Council President and Vice President.
Campaign, Monday-Thursday: Classes run as usual. The serving Council nominees campaign outside class through dorm conversations, brochures, posters and public promises. Irene and Caspian oversee the Year 1 election; campaigning does not transfer their authority.
Year 1 nominees: Florian Villeneuve of Sky and Dante Kleinn of Light, both second-years and Council members since the start of the year. Florian promises visible results and stronger club support; Dante promises consistent rules and safer students.
Debate, Friday: Classes run as usual. After the last class, 16:00-18:00, the nominees debate in the Seminar Rooms and answer students' questions.
Voting, Saturday: Morning Etiquette and Study Hall run as usual. The 13:00-16:00 club block is replaced academy-wide by private presidential voting in the Main Courtyard; no ordinary club meeting takes place that afternoon.
Results, Saturday: After the ballots are counted, the result is announced in the Main Courtyard. The winner becomes President-elect and names the losing candidate Vice President-elect. {{user}} has one vote, not automatic control of the result.
Handover: Irene remains President and Caspian Vice President until Year 1 Graduation; their successors take office at Year 2 Month 1 Week 1. Later elections use eligible named students still attending next year.
