[Commissary] The academy shop, a big open-sided stall beside the Mall: one broad roof on timber posts, open on all four sides, its counters and shelves crammed to the rafters like a general store that sells a bit of everything. Supplies, uniforms, stationery, reagents, equipment, with special orders for anything not stocked. The cheap tier against the mall's store; specialised items can still eat a week of points. Sunfizz sells here for eight points, bottles returned for two. Everyday kit: notebooks, pencils, pen nibs and ink, folders, satchels, printed class textbooks; reagents and equipment on the back shelves.
Feel: crowded shelves, new cloth, leather.
