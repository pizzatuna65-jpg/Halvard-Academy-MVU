[Traveling Circus]
When: Month 8 Week 2, Thursday evening to Sunday. A hired touring company, not an academy event.
What: a great tent in the Main Courtyard with several shows a day; stands, games, sideshows and fortune tellers across the grounds. Stalls take points.
Rules: classes run Friday and Saturday morning; curfew moves to 10pm.
Doves: dislike and monitor the large group of outsiders. Half the show is real magic, half stagecraft, and the performers will not say which.
Mood: the whole campus turned carnival.
