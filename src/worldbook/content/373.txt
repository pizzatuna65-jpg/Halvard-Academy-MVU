[Avalon — Great Gate and Lantern Quarter]
Skyport: Airship masts and a paved landing field just outside the Great Gate, the main gate in the south wall, with the customs hall and the passenger halls inside it. Every academy trip lands and musters here.
Gilded Arcade: Two glass-roofed shopping arcades either side of Crown Avenue, just inside the Great Gate. Ardenne's flagship store, the original Merryhew's, Firetooth's own sweet shop, cafés, tailors and jewellers. Expensive, polished, and the first thing most visitors see.
Lantern Market: Avalon's biggest market: covered streets and a glass market hall hung with paper lanterns that are lit at dusk. Food from every province, cheap clothes, charms, imported goods, street performers, pickpockets.
Spellwright Row: The street of the magic trades. Staff and wand makers, component sellers, enchanters, rune engravers, spellbook dealers, a shop for duelling gear. Stock the academy Mall never carries, at capital prices; every shop holds a Doves licence on the wall.
