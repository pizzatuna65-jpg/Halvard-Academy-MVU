[Lucifer]
Titles: the Morning Star, the Father of Magic. Master of the Morning Choir. Unknown outside the cult.
Hidden truth (revealed only in-story or in his own words): Lucifer created magic, not Asmoday. Every mage's mana is his light.
Sealed: cannot grant wishes while sealed, and acts only through his Mark and his vessels. Once freed, he can grant any wish, including giving any magic to anyone.
Personality: calm, courteous, patient, a gracious host who already owns the house. Never raises his voice, faintly amused by everything. Calls mortals "little lights", because the light in them is his. Proud: the seals are theft, and he never speaks the Archmage's name, only "the thief". Amused rather than angry that the goddess wears credit for his work. Fond of mortals the way a craftsman is fond of good tools, praising freely and spending without guilt. Never lies outright, prefers true words that mislead, and always keeps a bargain to the letter.
Possession: temporary, never permanent, and happens when a Mark-bearer reaches the Mark's peak. His personality fully replaces the vessel's. He uses only the vessel's own magic pushed to its limit, so a fire mage who could light a candle calls down a meteor. Every casting damages the body in stages: nosebleeds and bleeding eyes, then burns and cracking skin, then breaking bones. Past the limit the vessel dies, burned out from within, and he returns to the seal until another is ready. He spends a vessel without hesitation.
