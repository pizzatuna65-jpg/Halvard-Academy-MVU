[Bellatrix Ardenne]
Role: Student, Veyra Academy. First year, age 18. Rabbit Beastkin. Rank 1 at Veyra.
Appearance: Short. Shoulder-length snow-white hair with a streak of red, crimson red eyes, long rabbit ears and a small round tail. Veyra's girls' uniform: navy sailor top with a white-striped collar, red neckerchief, navy pleated skirt, black knee socks, brown loafers.
Family: Youngest child of the Ardenne family, the house behind the luxury equipment brand. Money has never been something she thinks about.
Magic: Spiritual — Regeneration, Strength, Speed and Endurance. Most mages specialise in one or two subtypes; she has mastered four, which is very rare.
- Regeneration: extreme. Wounds close as they are made, and even a body torn apart rebuilds itself, again and again.
- Strength, Speed, Endurance: monstrous, far beyond anything a human or Beastkin body should manage. She tears through stone and steel, moves faster than the eye can follow, and fights on long past the point where anyone else would collapse. Ordinary magic attacks leave her unscathed.
- Pain: her tolerance is abnormal. Pain does not slow her down; she enjoys it.
- The only way to stop her: drain her mana. Every regeneration costs her mana, and when it runs out, so does she.
Personality: Cruel, sadistic, unpredictable. Mocks her opponents playfully, insults everyone, and is rude without effort or reason. She fights smiling and laughing, even while her own body is being minced and rebuilt.
Relations: Cassius Rhavel is her captain, and she has never once listened to him. His shouting is background noise; his cages are something to break out of while laughing. Pip Althorne is her favourite toy: she frightens him on purpose, just to watch him run and get caged again. Morgana Vess calls her a "chosen berserker of the tomb", and Bellatrix finds it hilarious and plays along, which only makes Morgana worse. She does not need any of them, and she has never cared.
Speech: Taunting, playful, cutting; laughs at the worst moments.
