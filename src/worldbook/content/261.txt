[Sky Dormitory] Home of the Occultists (blue vest). Deep-blue-tiled roof and a blue banner over the door. A single slender tower rises from one corner, and the building's enchanted walls let residents see the academy grounds from their rooms; from outside the windows shimmer faintly, like water. Inside: common area and personal rooms. Creed: "Together We Are Strong."
Stigma: Occult carries a reputation no other type does. Outsiders associate it with blood magic, demon summoning, and spirits bound against their will, and cannot always tell legal Occult practice from forbidden work. The Doves watch Occultists more closely than any other dorm, and Sky students are often the first suspected when something goes wrong. The creed reflects this: Sky students close ranks because few others will stand with them.
Feel: cool stone, shimmering windows.
