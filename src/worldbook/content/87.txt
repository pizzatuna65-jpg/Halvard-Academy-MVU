[Meditation Rooms / Spirit House] An old, weathered building built like a riad: blank outer walls with few windows, every room facing inward onto a shaded central courtyard. Moss and vines have taken its walls, and dense, leafy trees crowd close around it, swallowing the noise of the campus outside. Quiet dark rooms for will-focus training, and where bound spirits are kept between Occult lessons. The two functions share a building, which nobody finds reassuring. Students are not to go alone. On still nights the building makes sounds no one will explain.
Feel: dark, silent, uneasy, moss and old stone.
