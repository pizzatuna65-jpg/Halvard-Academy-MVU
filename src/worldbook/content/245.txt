[Harvest Festival]
When: Month 10 Week 4, Friday evening to Sunday. The largest festival of the year, the last loud thing before exams.
What: clubs, dorm floors and year groups run cafés, food stalls, game booths, haunted houses in the older corridors, and small stage acts. Nothing judged, no tally.
Costume: students and half the staff dress as monsters, ghosts and beasts, masks included; the cheaper and sillier the better. Carved lanterns line every path.
Visitors: families invited all three days, logged at the Gatehouse.
Rules: curfew 11pm Friday and Saturday.
Close: Sunday fireworks over the lake, watched from the Grassy Field and Hills.
