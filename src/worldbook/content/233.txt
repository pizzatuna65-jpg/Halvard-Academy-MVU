[Club Festival]
When: Month 3 Week 2, Friday afternoon and all of Saturday.
What: every club runs a stand in the Main Courtyard: demonstrations, displays, contests, food. Duelling and sport clubs run exhibition matches, Alchemy and Smithing sell their work, Beast Handling brings out tame specimens, quieter clubs use displays and free samples. Club-funded, staffed in shifts.
Voting: every student and staff member gets one gold coin to drop at the best stand; nobody votes for their own club, advisors included. Winner announced at Saturday's close.
Prize: prestige only, plus better leverage for room space and budget all year.
