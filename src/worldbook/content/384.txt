[Norvaine team]
Team: Radomir Sokolov (Attack, Y3, M, fair, proud, Captain); Vesna Zvezdova (Control, Y1, F, pink, sunny, any role); Mirko Petrenko (Defense, Y1, M, red-brown, suspicious); Yarina Kovalenko (Healing, Y2, Elf, F, silver-white, cold).
Selection: Norvaine does not pick its four the way Eldrasil's panel does; it sends whole the team that wins its own national competition. This year's winners lost their ace, Yaroslav Morozov, a week before the World Competition: Vesna, whom nobody had ever heard of, took his place, and their coach was replaced by an army colonel the same day. Yaroslav has not been seen since.
Kit: grey military greatcoats with silver buttons and fur collars, the starglass device of Norvaine on the shoulder.
Coach: Colonel Kazimir Zorin of the Norvaine army (own entry).
Plays: drilled. The team moves as one unit on hand signals and never splits; the Colonel builds every plan around Vesna, whatever role the fight is missing she becomes it, and the other three follow the plan to the letter. Norvaine's mages go to the demon front after their academy years, and its four fight like soldiers because soon they will be.
Radomir Sokolov: third year, age 20, Human. A big fair northerner, close-cropped blond hair, a short beard, a scar through one eyebrow. Elemental (Ice): spears and hammers of ice. Steady and proud, a born sergeant; he won the Norvaine Competition with Yaroslav at his side and cannot forgive a stranger for taking his place. He follows the Colonel's orders to the letter and speaks to Vesna only when he must.
Mirko Petrenko: first year, age 18, Human. Lanky and freckled, red-brown hair, ears that go red when anyone speaks to him. Elemental (Earth): stone shields and walls of packed earth. Earnest and Yaroslav's oldest friend; he has been looking for him since the day he vanished. He has never seen Vesna at any academy in Norvaine, and he watches her as if waiting for her to slip.
Yarina Kovalenko: second year, age 19, Elf. Tall and calm, silver-white hair, pale grey eyes. Spiritual (Healing): a cold blue light that closes wounds and numbs the pain. Kind to anyone but Vesna; she heals her when ordered, without a word, and envies her more than she will admit: a girl from nowhere does without effort what Yarina has trained for all her life.
