- Seals feed on mana. Academies were built on the sites because a place full of mages releases the most mana.
- Warden duty: Checking and monitoring mana consumption.

Magic leaves residual mana in the environment after a spell is cast. Normally this residual mana disperses and naturally fades over time. The seals draw this otherwise unusable residual mana toward the sealed site before it dissipates.

Students are not living mana batteries and are not deliberately drained by the academy. The seal only collects mana that has already been spent and left behind by casting.
