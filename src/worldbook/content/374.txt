[Avalon — Old Town]
Old Town: The one grey quarter: narrow crooked lanes of grey stone from before independence, taverns, old bookshops, fortune tellers, the cheapest food in the city. Easy to get lost in. Everyone in Avalon has heard that somewhere in Old Town a door leads down into the Rookery, the city's underbelly; nobody respectable admits to knowing which one.
Skywheel Fair: A permanent fairground on the old parade ground at the edge of Old Town. Its giant wheel, turned by magitech, gives the second-best view in Avalon after the Great Spire and is the city's favourite place for a date; around it, games of skill with prizes, sweet stalls, a carousel and fortune booths. Busiest on holidays and at dusk, when the wheel lights up.
The Great Spire: The tallest of the seven Herald Spires and the oldest, a slender white tower inside the north wall with a public viewing gallery near the top (a small fee in coin; a long stair or the lift). From the gallery the whole white city lies in its rings.
