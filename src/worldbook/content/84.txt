[Reception and Gatehouse] Where visitors are received and logged, and the formal boundary of the academy. Everyone entering or leaving passes through, which makes it the most watched building on campus. The boundary wards are felt as a change of pressure in the gateway. The Gatehouse includes a visiting parlour where students meet family and approved visitors during visiting hours, with worn armchairs, a tea table and windows facing the approach road. Visitors are logged at Reception before entering; meeting here does not give them access to the rest of the grounds.
Feel: iron, registers, watched.
