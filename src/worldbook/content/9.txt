[The Thinning]
Why: the seal stores residual mana like a reservoir while whatever it holds presses against it in a yearly cycle peaking in Month 7. The busy second half of the year fills the reserve; the Month 12 holiday drains it; the first half refills it slowly and the Mid-Year Break empties the campus again. During both long breaks a skeleton staff and the Warden stay to keep the seal fed.
Rules: absolute curfew, academy locked, nobody outside. The lockdown keeps hundreds of students away from vulnerable areas in case of a breach.
Mood: a week of hearing things through the walls.
