Magic Theory Class
Purpose: Teach students why magic works, not just how — the underlying structure of mana, why seals consume it, and why the four types behave differently. Grounds every other class.
Activity: Lectures with diagrams of mana flow. Written analysis of failed spells. Students invent and defend original spell theories. Occasional joint sessions where all four dorms compare how they'd solve the same problem.
Covers: residual mana and the seal, the four magic types and their subtypes (Elemental magic, Mystic magic, Spiritual magic, Occult magic), Spirit Pact and Spirit Tiers.
