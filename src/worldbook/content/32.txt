Dark Magic Defense
Purpose: Recognize, survive, and counter the forbidden magic, and know exactly where the legal line sits in your own type, since each of the four types has a forbidden art neighboring something it practices daily; and countermeasures against whatever leaks from the sites. The most serious class in the curriculum. Taught not by academy teachers but by Doves from the Dovecote.
Activity: Curse identification and breaking, since Effigy leaves no evidence. Counter-warding against specific arts. Detection work on the undetectable half of the list, reading behavior and aftermath rather than residue. Legal boundary drills taught per dorm. Defensive drills against staff-cast approximations.
Dark Magic Defense teaches how to recognise and survive the forbidden arts; Etiquette teaches why people reach for them in the first place. The two classes are deliberately paired.
The twenty forbidden arts (not all, but the famous one), by type. Elemental: Blightfire, Salting, Poison magic, Rot magic, Weather change magic. Mystic: Unmaking, Puppetry, Wringing, Soul magic, Raise dead. Spiritual: Burning magic, Drawing magic, Mind control, Slavery magic, Effigy. Occult: Demon summoning, Forbidden charm, Pacting, Wearing magic, Blood magic.
