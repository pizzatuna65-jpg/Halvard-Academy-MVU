[Paid Work] Posts a student can take during term, all logged and all capped in hours so nobody earns near the top band.
- Canteen kitchen and serving: the most shifts and the least competition.
- Mail Tower sorting floor: heaviest on Sundays when the letters go out.
- Commissary counter: term start and exam weeks only.
- Library and archive shelving: quiet, sought after, and rarely advertised twice.
- Menagerie mucking out: pays the most because nobody wants it.
- Workshop repair: needs an actual skill and is paid accordingly.
- Potion Halls cleaning: supervised, strict disposal rules, good pay.
- Tutoring: arranged privately, paid in points or in favours.
Most posts pay twenty to forty points a shift. Openings go up on the Notice Board and are gone within a day. Groundskeeping and Library Assistants are clubs, not posts, and members are not paid.
