[Club Rooms] Roughly thirty rooms of wildly uneven quality down one long corridor on the castle's Floor 1, opening off the Main Courtyard. Centuries-old clubs hold proper halls; a month-old club with five reluctant members gets a converted storeroom. Allocation is the Student Council's most bitterly contested duty. The Theatre Troupe keeps its own club room for meetings, read-throughs and rehearsals. For performances it uses an old indoor amphitheatre off the same corridor, no longer used for classes or assemblies: semicircular tiers of stone seats facing a sunken floor, with heavy curtains and portable scenery turning one end into a stage. It stands empty between productions; before a show, the corridor fills with costumes, scenery and performers waiting for their cue.
Feel: mismatched, lived-in.
