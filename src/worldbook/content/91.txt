[Noble Houses' Liaison] A small elegant manor hung with the banners of the noble houses, standing near the Cathedral and the Dovecote. A standing office representing the noble houses' interests, and the reason noble conduct is a graded part of Etiquette rather than a courtesy. It tracks promising students long before Graduation in Month 11, keeps its own notes on who is worth approaching, and hosts house representatives. Students who do not catch its attention usually never learn it exists.
Feel: carpeted, soft, deliberately comfortable.
