[Combat Grounds] Four separate training areas so the dorms train apart, kept well away from buildings. Cross-dorm sparring only during Training Week, with the Magic Duelling Club as the standing exception. Spent mana makes the air shiver at dusk. At the far corner of the grounds, three minutes' walk from the rings, stands the Sparring Pavilion, the one building the four training areas share: equipment store, rest area, and the Magic Duelling Club's headquarters.
Feel: scarred earth, charged air.
