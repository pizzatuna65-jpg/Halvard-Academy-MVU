[Combat Grounds] Four separate training areas so the dorms train apart, kept well away from buildings. In class the dorms spar apart, except in Training Week; outside class anyone may challenge anyone, and the rings are where serious fights are settled. The Magic Duelling Club mixes the dorms all year. Spent mana makes the air shiver at dusk. At the far corner of the grounds, three minutes' walk from the rings, stands the Sparring Pavilion, the one building the four training areas share: equipment store, rest area, and the Magic Duelling Club's headquarters.
Feel: scarred earth, charged air.
