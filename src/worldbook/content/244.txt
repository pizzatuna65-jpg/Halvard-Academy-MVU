[Remembrance Day]
When: Month 10 Week 1, Tuesday. A Halvard day, not a kingdom one, marking the breach night two years ago.
Rules: nothing loud (no clubs, duelling, music, games, sport); classes run quietly; the Bell Tower keeps a slow pattern. Bouquets line the halls until evening.
Honoured: the students killed that night and the Warden who died resealing the Crack. The Acting Warden attends and is not asked to speak.
Evening: lantern release on the Grassy Field and Hills, one lantern per person.
Mood: memory for third-years, inherited for everyone else; silent until the last lantern is gone.
