[The Eternal Sakura] A giant sakura at the heart of the city, wider than a house and older than the throne, in full bloom all year round. In winter its pink petals fall onto the snow beneath it, and the whole city comes to stand under it at least once. Nobody knows why it never stops flowering; Yozakurans say it will bloom as long as Yozakura has an Alpha.
Feel: Pink petals falling on white snow, and the whole crowd gone quiet.
