[Avalon — the Kingsmeadow] The crown's meadowland beyond the Great Gate, thirty minutes' walk from the Skyport road: grass, a brook, an old orchard and a stone ring, warded at its edges by the competition panel. Round one of the Kingdom Competition is fought here, from dawn to sunset; on other days it is closed crown land.
Judges' Pavilion: A white pavilion with the royal blue and gold on its poles where the Kingsmeadow road meets the grass: the competition panel's table, the four academies' banners, a healer's tent beside it. Round one starts here at dawn, and here at sunset the judges take one Kingsbloom from each team.
Long Meadow: Waist-high grass rolling away from the pavilion in long waves, a few lone oaks, larks going up wherever someone walks. Everything moving out here can be seen from far off.
Willow Brook: A clear stream winding under old willows, stepping stones at the shallows and a footbridge of three planks; the banks are soft and keep footprints.
Old Orchard: Rows of old apple and pear trees from a royal estate long gone, gone half wild, windfalls in the grass and a collapsed press house at the end of the rows.
Bramble Hollow: A sunken hollow choked with bramble and blackthorn, narrow tracks through it that badgers made and nobody else can walk upright; the best place in the Kingsmeadow to wait unseen.
Standing Stones: A ring of eleven grey standing stones on a low rise, older than the kingdom; the panel's wards hum faintly between them, and the grass inside the ring is short and dry.
Reed Marsh: Reed beds taller than a person around pools of black water, duckboards laid by the panel along one line only; off them, it is wet to the knee and loud with every step.
Beacon Hill: The highest ground in the Kingsmeadow, crowned by an old beacon basket on a stone post; from the top the whole field shows, and so does whoever stands on it.
