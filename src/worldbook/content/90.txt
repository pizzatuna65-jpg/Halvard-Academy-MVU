[The Cathedral] A house of worship for Asmoday, Goddess of Creation, on academy grounds but answerable to its own authority. Services open to all; its clergy are not academy employees and teach that Asmoday created magic and gifted it to mortals. Every service opens with the Shaping of the World; the clergy teach that Asmoday fractured magic after the First Bargain. That a goddess of creation holds ground at an institution built around sealing things away is a tension nobody discusses openly. It also runs orphanages across the kingdom, the Asmoday's Mercy houses. Halvard: run by a steward who keeps the registry of every oath sworn on academy grounds.
Feel: stained-glass colour, incense, heavy stillness.
