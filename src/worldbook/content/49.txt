[Lecture Halls] Tiered rooms holding all four dorms at once, for the shared track: Magic Theory, History, Etiquette, Creature Studies. Each year level has its own floor: first-years on Floor 2, second-years on Floor 3, third-years on Floor 4. The only classrooms where students sit beside other types. Each class leaves its traces: chalk diagrams, old maps, polished desks, mud and straw after Creature Studies.
Feel: tiered stone, crowded, metallic air.
