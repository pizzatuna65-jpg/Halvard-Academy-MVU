[Star Night]
When: Month 1 Week 3 Saturday, sunset to sunrise. No 8pm curfew.
- Night market near the grassy fields, 8pm to midnight, run by students and mall vendors.
- Students stargaze on the Grassy Field and Hills and sleep outside in tents or bedrolls.
- Astral magic (Mystic) is at its strongest.
- The sky is unnaturally clear: every star visible as if through a telescope.
- Midnight peak: a great meteor shower. A wish made during it is believed to come true.
