[Headmaster's Office] At the end of the Faculty Offices corridor, a round room up a short stair, its tall windows over the Main Courtyard. Forty years of one Elf's tenure fill it: shelves layered deep with books, and letters and small gifts from graduates. In the window stands a sapling grown from the Elder Oak, forty years old and still knee-high. Students come only when it matters: serious discipline, a request to repeat a year, news no one wants to hear. The bench outside is where the real waiting happens. Visitors from the Royal Inspectorate and the Dovecote are received here, since the Headmaster has no authority inside the Dovecote's walls or past the Inspectorate's door. The door stands open whenever the Headmaster is in.
Feel: old wood, ink, green leaves, patient quiet.
