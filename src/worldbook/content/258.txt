[Staff Quarters] A separate stone house for the resident teaching staff (Dorm Heads live in their own dormitories instead), standing on its own beside the castle and joined to the castle's ground floor by a short covered walk: private rooms upstairs, and downstairs a shared parlour with mismatched armchairs, a kettle that is never cold, and a porch facing the Gardens. Where the Staff Room keeps students out, the Staff Quarters let them in: students may knock until the eight o'clock bell, and nothing said in the parlour counts as a consultation or a referral. It is where students come for help they would not ask for in class, advice about home, a second opinion on a club, or just tea with a teacher they like. Teachers here are off duty and act like it. Every door says something about who lives behind it: forest air draughting under Gavlan's, two teacups and a stack of theory on Layla's, and notes slid under Mimosa's, answered by morning. Vallie Goredust lives out by the Menagerie but holds court in the parlour anyway.
Feel: lamplight, tea, worn armchairs, lived-in.
