[Announcement Pillars] Slender stone pillars carved with sound runes, standing across the whole academy. They carry the live voice or music from the Announcement Room and nothing else: they cannot be spoken into, record nothing, and are a separate working from the mana grid. Their runes glow faintly while a broadcast is live. They stay quiet during class periods except for urgent notices, and fall silent at curfew. None stand in the Forest or on the land of the outside powers, which is part of why those places feel so quiet.
