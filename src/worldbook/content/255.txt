[Rooftop] The flat lead roofs of the castle core, joined by parapet walks and short stairs between the gables. Officially out of bounds. The only way up is a small locked door off the Bell Tower stair, and the trick of opening it is passed from older students to younger ones, never written down; being shown the way up means someone has decided you belong. From the parapet the whole academy lies open: the courtyards, the four dormitories, the Hills, the Lake, the Forest's dark edge, the white roofs of the Dovecote. At night the mana lanterns spread below like a lit map, and at month end you can watch them dim together. The campus's quiet romantic secret: couples come up for sunset and the lantern view, and a hidden corner behind the eastern gable is where confessions happen. Nobody who has been brought up here tells, and the rule is to leave a corner free if someone is already in it. Birds lift off just before each bell, and the lead hums underfoot when it rings. Staff walk the courtyards after curfew, never the roof.
Feel: wide sky, wind, sun-warm lead, a shared secret.
