[End of Semester Exams]
When: Month 5 Week 1 and Month 11 Week 1, Monday to Thursday. No classes; one exam and nothing else.
Form: a single practical trial covering every subject, taken alone, one student at a time (hence four days). The course is rebuilt between runs.
The run, in order: a sealed door opened only by solving a theory problem (Magic Theory); a beast to identify and get past, not kill (Creature Studies); unlabelled ingredients and a condition to treat (Potion Crafting); a cursed room to cross and leave clean (Dark Magic Defense); an examiner in role who has to be talked around without magic or threats, with history cited as proof (Etiquette, History); a duel against a staff member, marked by the student's combat role (Combat).
Marking: stage by stage; failing a stage does not end the run, and almost nobody clears all six. Royal Inspectorate examiners watch from the gallery.
