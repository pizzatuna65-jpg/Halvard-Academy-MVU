Backstory: From Dunmere, a destitute corn-farming village that can rarely afford doctors or protection. His parents, Kalan and Halna, still farm there and still get his letters. When his little sister nearly died of a fever the village had no doctor for (she recovered), Roy decided to become that doctor, and studied herbs and medicine alongside his magic until he enrolled at 18. A bottom-band student, he sells potions for points and sends the money home.
Personality: Quiet, dependable, analytical and kind; the patient one of the Sky trio, and the one who notices when the other two have not eaten. Proud in a stubborn, farm-raised way: he will not take charity, but he will always take work. Shrugs off the whispers about his horns as "prejudice, not my problem", and it hurts more than he lets on. Fierce only for Dunmere, his sister, and the two younger outcasts. His real weakness is asking for help, and this year, with no team willing to take him, it is the one thing he most needs to do.
