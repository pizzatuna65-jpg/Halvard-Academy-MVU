[Caelmar team]
Team: Rocco Malvezzi (Attack, Y3, M, shaved head, hard, Captain); Lucrezia Altamura (Control, Y1, F, ash-grey, gleeful, the team's ace); Domenico Conti (Defense, Y2, M, heavyset, patient); Nina Scarpa (Support, Y2, F, cropped dark hair, wary).
Selection: in Caelmar the academies' competitions are fought by the low-born and watched, sponsored and bet on by the high-born; the national four comes up from the lower layers. Lucrezia is the first highborn anyone can remember on the field.
Kit: black work jackets with brass buttons and copper rune piping. Lucrezia wears her own gothic dress instead, which belongs to no academy in Caelmar; somehow nobody on the team has ever raised it.
Coach: Aldo Ferrata, a top-layer guild master and the team's patron, who owns it the way the high-born own racehorses; watches from a cushioned chair and has never been below the clouds.
Plays: chaos. Caelmar takes the field with a plan and keeps to it until Lucrezia gets bored; after that it fights to hers, which nobody, her team included, can predict.
Rocco Malvezzi: third year, age 20, Human. Broad and hard, shaved head, scarred knuckles, from the smelting layers; fought his way up through every bracket Caelmar runs. Elemental (Fire), as hot and blunt as a forge blast. Proud and short-tempered; he captains the team on paper, and on the field he goes where Lucrezia's strings put him, hating her for it with every low-born's hatred of the people above the clouds.
Domenico Conti: second year, age 19, Human. Heavyset and quiet, big burn-scarred hands, from a workshop family in the dark lower layers. Mystic (Telekinesis): catches what is thrown at the team and throws it back. Patient and good-natured; Lucrezia strings him into position mid-fight without asking, and he has stopped complaining, because below the clouds complaining to a highborn has a price.
Nina Scarpa: second year, age 19, Human. Small and quick, soot-dark hair cropped short, a runner's build; grew up carrying messages between the layers. Occult (binding spirits): small bound spirits that drag at enemies and steady allies. Wary and sharp; the only one who has worked out that Lucrezia is bored, and that a bored Lucrezia is the most dangerous thing on any field.
