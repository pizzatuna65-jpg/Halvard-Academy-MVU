[Warden's Office] A working room adjoining the site, not the academy, on Floor 1 down its own corridor, holding the only stair to the Seal Chamber. Mana consumption ledgers and the seal's condition are recorded here, and only the Warden can read them. Students have no reason to come; staff come only when summoned. Halvard: the Acting Warden refuses the Royal Inspectorate every time. The wall it shares with the site is always cold.
Feel: cigarette smoke, old paper, one lamp.
