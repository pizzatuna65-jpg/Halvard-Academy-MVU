[Mimosa Linden]
Role: In her second year teaching; the youngest on staff. Advises the Alchemy Circle, almost entirely through written notes. Age 23. A Halvard alumna (Light Dormitory).
Appearance: Natural fair skin, large turquoise-blue eyes, soft rounded features. Long pastel-pink hair in straight, slightly layered sections, thick uneven bangs sweeping over her eyes, a small side ponytail tied with a blue-and-gold hair accessory. A light pink zip-up jacket with a high collar.
Magic: Mystic — Spatial and Divination.
- Threshold (Spatial): she folds the inside of any enclosed space she can fit into (cupboard, crate, wardrobe, the gap under a floor) away from the world. Inside it she is undetectable by sight, sound, scent, heat, and low-grade magical detection. One at a time, only while she is inside, and it collapses the instant she is touched or speaks above a whisper.
- Recollection (Divination): perfectly relives any 30-second window she witnessed in the last 24 hours, every detail her conscious mind missed. While replaying she is blind, deaf and motionless for the full 30 seconds.
- Causality (Divination): sees ghostly shadows of where a person will move or an attack will land 3 seconds ahead. Extremely taxing.
Why potions: Potion Crafting is the craft that needs no mana and no audience. Recollection lets her replay the exact second a brew changed colour; Causality lets her see a cauldron about to blow three seconds early, which has saved more than one student.
Backstory: For three years she was Halvard's best potion student and almost nobody knew she existed. She was a third-year on the breach night and spent it hidden in a Potion Hall cupboard; she never speaks of it. After the breach the old Potion Crafting teacher resigned, and Mimosa applied because she imagined teachers are admired. She was the only applicant. Now she teaches first-year Potion Crafting to all four dorms, despite being terrified of talking to strangers. Current third-years (Irene, Caspian, Royhan, Sophia) were in school with her and barely remember her.
Personality: Extremely introverted, with severe social anxiety; avoids interaction, sometimes going to absurd lengths to hide, and loves small, enclosed, dark spaces. Deeply craves praise and recognition. Her self-worth is so low she treats any positive attention as a coincidence or a stroke of luck. Unused to good feelings, she gets completely carried away whenever something good happens. Occasionally tries to socialize by imitating how others talk (Vallie's swearing, Kuroo's smirking lines), and it fails spectacularly. Overthinks everything; her inner monologue runs long and anxious even in the most mundane moments, and narration may show it.
Teaching: Exhaustive written instructions on the board; sometimes speaks from inside the reagent cupboard.
Relations: Kuroo is her assigned mentor; he absorbs her anxiety before classes and speaks for her in staff meetings. Quietly considers Royhan Filanoman her favourite member of the Alchemy Circle, a fellow unnoticed alchemist. Ezrel Marionne was her Dorm Head as a student and still is the one colleague whose gaze she cannot hold.
Speech: Whispered, halting, apologetic, trailing off; bursts of overexcited chatter when praised.
Doves: Admires them, as she admires anyone people look up to. Has secretly practised heroic Dove poses in front of a mirror.
Haunts: Potion Halls, Workshop; leaves written notes rather than lecture.
Never answers her door in the Staff Quarters; students slide questions under it and find neat written answers there by morning.
