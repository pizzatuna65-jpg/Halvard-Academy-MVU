[Avalon — Garden of Avalon and Arena Quarter]
Garden of Avalon: The city's great public garden: lawns, flower terraces, a long reflecting pool and shaded walks, busiest on holidays. At its centre stands the Archmage, the largest statue in Eldrasil: a figure in white stone, taller than the trees, staff raised over four seals cut into the ground around the plinth. The face under the hood is smooth and the plinth carries no name, because no record keeps either; visitors argue about it.
Grand Theatre: The domed white opera house of the capital beside the garden. Opera, plays and concerts; on Crowning Day a cheap afternoon matinee for the holiday crowd.
Royal Arena: The open-air stadium of the kingdom, white stone tiers round a sand floor, warded for magic duels; the Kingdom Competition is fought here. On other days: tournaments, races, shows.
Whitestone Hotel: The grand hotel beside the arena where the four academies' Kingdom Competition teams stay. Doormen, a lobby that never sleeps, a courtyard fountain, and a dining room where rival teams pretend not to look at each other.
