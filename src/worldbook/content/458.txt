[Old Town] The one grey quarter: narrow crooked lanes of grey stone from before independence, taverns, old bookshops, fortune tellers, the cheapest food in the city. Easy to get lost in. Everyone in Avalon has heard that somewhere in Old Town a door leads down into the Rookery, the city's underbelly; nobody respectable admits to knowing which one.
Feel: Grey stone, crooked lanes, and doors nobody talks about.
