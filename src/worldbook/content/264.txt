[Announcement Room] A small booth-like room off the Club Rooms corridor on Floor 1, home of the Academy Newspaper club. A speaking rune set into the desk carries whatever is said into it, live, to the Announcement Pillars around the grounds; nothing is recorded. Students on duty open the day with a good-morning after the seven o'clock bell and give the noon, afternoon and evening greetings, the last just before the eight o'clock bell. In between they read out what staff, the Student Council and event organisers send down, plus whatever the student on duty decides is important, the most argued-about power any club holds. Outside class periods the Music Club plays live from the side room, and the whole campus hears it. Staff can take over the rune for anything urgent; during The Thinning lockdown it is how the academy speaks to everyone at once.
Feel: cramped, warm lamplight, sheet music and newsprint.
