Combat Class
Purpose: Practical casting under pressure. The direct feeder into all four competition tiers.
Activity: Drills, sparring, and duels. Each dorm trains differently — Elemental on destructive range, Mystic on battlefield control, Spiritual on endurance and frontline pressure, Occult on disruption and binding. Cross-dorm sparring in class only during Training Week; this is a class rule, not a ban on casting elsewhere.
Drills, sparring and marking are adjusted to each student's combat role.
Covers: the five combat roles, the Combat Grounds, and preparation for the Dorm and Academy Competitions.
