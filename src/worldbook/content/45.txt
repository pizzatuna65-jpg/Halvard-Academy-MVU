[Ring Dining Hall] Formal hall, tables in a ring around an open floor so all four dorms face each other across empty space. Musicians in the gallery, an illusion sky that turns a whole day's weather overhead, climate runes holding it windless and warm. Used only for the Entrance Feast and other full-academy meals. Empty most of the year and unsettling to walk alone.
Feel: grand, too-perfect warmth, echoing footsteps.
