[Airship: Promenade Deck] The glassed gallery that wraps the airship's bow and runs down both sides: brass rails, padded benches under the windows, and nothing between the passengers and the sky but the glass. The bow end is the best view on the ship and the first place every student runs to at boarding; by the second hour it is the quietest. The glass is cold to the touch at height and the deck hums faintly underfoot. Yozakuran stewards walk it with tea on the long flights. The teachers stroll here too, which makes it the safest place on board to be seen talking to someone and the worst place to say anything private. Past the bow, a door marked in Yozakuran and Common leads to the bridge: crew only.
