[The First Bargain]
Story: when magic was still whole, a mortal bargained with something that shone brighter than any star and asked for all of magic. The bargain was kept to the letter. With all of magic in his hands he tore a Gate from his own land into the demon world, crowned himself the Demon King, and opened more Gates across the continent to loose his demons on every other nation, and the world nearly broke. Magic was then fractured into four; nearly every mage since is born to one type.
Told: everywhere; Dark Magic Defense opens its Pacting lesson with it.
Lesson: never make a pact with anything but a spirit.
Status: the mortal's name is forgotten; he is remembered only as the Demon King. The Cathedral says Asmoday fractured magic to save her world.
<narrator_only>What shone was Lucifer. Freed, he could give any magic to anyone again. He remembers the mortal fondly: the Demon King was his first bargain, and his favourite.</narrator_only>
