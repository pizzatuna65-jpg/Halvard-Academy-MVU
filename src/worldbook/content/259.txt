[Fire Dormitory] Home of the Elementalists (red vest). Red-tiled roof and a red banner over the door. A great hearth burns in its common room day and night, and the broad chimney stack rising above the front of the building is always warm, even in winter. Inside: a common area and personal rooms. Creed: "Through the Elements, Victory Is Ours."
Feel: hearth warmth, woodsmoke, stone.
