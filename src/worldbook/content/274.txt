[The Four Who Saved the World]
Story: when the First Bargain nearly ended the world, four heroes saved it: the King of Knights from the north, the Archmage from the east, the Lady of the Lake from the south, the Empress of the Fractured Vow from the west. They freed the lands the Demon King held, Eldrasil among them, and slew the Demon King. The first Gate, in the Demon Kingdom, they could not close; it stands open still, and the other Gates were sealed one by one in the years after.
Told: across the continent; each land calls its own hero the greatest. Eldrasil, in the east, claims the Archmage.
Lesson: no land saves the world alone.
Status: their names and faces are lost. Some say each carried one of the four fractured magics. The last Gate is no legend: Norvaine holds the line against what comes through it today.
<narrator_only>The Archmage's part was sealing what shone. Lucifer has called them the thief ever since.</narrator_only>
