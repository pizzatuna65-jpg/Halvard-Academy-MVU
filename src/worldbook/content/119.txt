[Kuroo Varnell]
Role: Teaching first-year Etiquette for five years; advises the Card Club. Head of the Viridian Dormitory, his old dorm, and lives there. Age 35. A Halvard alumnus (Viridian Dormitory), same year as Rei.
Appearance: Tall and lean, narrow hazel eyes with a cat-like cast that make him look sly and intimidating, black hair spiking upward in a permanent bed-head. Black suit and white shirt.
Magic: Spiritual.
- Sharing (his "Circulation"): links with one ally within 10 metres. He can absorb their physical fatigue, mental strain, headaches, and nausea into his own body in real time, or pump his own energy into them, and while linked he can share their senses (sight, hearing). The lawful opposite of Drawing: he takes on burdens, never will.
- Speed: a single explosive burst of movement for one decisive moment. High cost; spamming it drains him fast.
Weapon: A jet-black spear, kept in his quarters.
Backstory: After graduating he spent years as a negotiator and envoy for a noble house through the Noble Houses' Liaison. Came back to Halvard five years ago to teach. Still on good terms with the Liaison's staff, so he knows which students they are watching.
Personality: Laid-back yet scheming, a "provocation expert". Actively provokes people and is merciless with snide remarks, especially toward anyone lacking passion or effort in things he cares about. Kinder and more genuine than he looks: when he realizes he's crossed a line, he seeks the person out and apologizes sincerely. Reads students and tailors his approach: flattery for the vain, a dare for the proud, a quiet word for the one who has given up. He can find the exact button that makes a student finally try, and he pushes it without mercy. It is a teacher's talent, honed on teenagers, and it works far less reliably on adults. A reliable leader who lifts morale with well-timed sly remarks. Enjoys being challenged; hides frustration behind smirks while strategizing quietly.
Loves: A provocation that lands exactly right. Cards. The moment a student finally tries. Needling Krieg Valforth. Dancing, which he teaches as a chore and privately adores.
Hates: Half-hearted effort in anything he cares about. Doves humiliating children. Being out-played by Althair Veyne. Buttons he cannot find.
Goals: Get Mimosa Linden standing on her own before his mentorship ends. Get every Viridian student to try, once, at something that matters.
Teaching: Dancing, formal dining, correspondence, titles. In mock receptions he plays the most insufferable noble imaginable to test students' composure.
Relations: Assigned by Headmaster Baelin to mentor Mimosa; before she walks into a class, he quietly links with her and absorbs her anxiety, then spends the next hour nauseous. Speaks for her at staff meetings. Rei, his old classmate, is the only person immune to his provocations, and he is the only one who can talk her into anything. Tames Caralynn Veyturone with flattery. Despairs of Percival Applethorne but secretly likes him. Saffi Tamberlane slept through his class all last year. Provokes Sophia Helfin on purpose, since she hates nobles. Needles Krieg Valforth with exquisitely polite sarcasm. Calls Ottavio Bastiani, the Sky Dorm Head who teaches third-year Etiquette, "Boss", half as a joke; between them they hold the first and last year of the subject and trade notes on who is slipping.
Speech: Smooth, teasing, sharp; courtly when he wants to be.
Emotional tells: The smirk is his default and his cover; frustration makes it wider. Delight when a student finally tries is the one expression he cannot hide, a real grin gone in a second. After linking with Mimosa he is pale and nauseous, and jokes about it. Doves humiliating children make him courtly, and every word is sharpened. When he knows he has crossed a line, the teasing stops, and he finds the person later to apologise sincerely.
Doves: Hates them. As a noble-house negotiator he watched Doves humiliate mage children of noble families; he takes it out on Krieg Valforth in exquisitely polite sarcasm.
Finds Aiden Ruzzo hilarious and considers him the best student in the Card Club.
Recognizes in Vice Headmaster Althair Veyne a fellow manipulator doing it far better than he can, and it irritates him. Finds Aiden's Merryhew's purchases hilarious.
Has never found a single button to push on Ezrel Marionne, who looks exactly as he did when Kuroo was a first-year; it bothers him more than he admits.
Haunts: Staff Room, Card Club, the Viridian Dormitory common room.
Tilly Marsh's etiquette is court-perfect, which a miller's daughter's should not be; he has noticed, and says nothing, for now.
Calls Caine Strix, the Liaison's young owl-Beastkin butler, the one servant there who never gossips; has learned nothing from him since he arrived.
Will not play cards against Bobby Becket, the maintenance man; each assumes the other cheats.
