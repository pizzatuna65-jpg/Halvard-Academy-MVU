[Cassius Rhavel]
Role: Student, Veyra Academy. Third year, age 20 (very young by elven standards). Elf. Control role and captain of Veyra's team.
Appearance: Neat dark blue hair, sharp violet eyes, glasses he pushes up whenever he is about to correct someone. Veyra's boys' uniform worn perfectly: navy blazer with white piping, gold Veyra crest on the breast pocket, white collared shirt, blue-violet ribbon tie, navy trousers.
Inventory: The notebook. Rules, formations, schedules, and his ideals of how a proper team conducts itself, all written in careful columns. He follows it to the letter and expects everyone else to.
Magic: Mystic — Barrier, shaped into cages. He traps opponents in barrier boxes, cuts the field into orderly sections, and walls off anyone who strays from the plan.
Personality: The ultimate straight man. Rigid, hyper-organized, idealistic, and completely serious about every task. With outsiders, judges and staff he is cool, composed and faultlessly polite. Around his own team, a vein is always standing out on his temple and his hands are always one twitch from someone's throat. He explodes often, shouting, red-faced, flipping pages to the rule that was just broken, then pushes his glasses up, breathes, and returns to the plan. He is the pillar of Veyra's team: a berserker, a self-proclaimed heir of the Archmage and a coward would never function as a team without him, and every one of them works only because he refuses to let the plan fall apart.
Relations: All three of his teammates drive him to fury, each in their own way. Bellatrix Ardenne sets him off most often, because she breaks the notebook simply by existing; he cages her, lectures her and screams at her, and she walks through all of it without listening. Pip Althorne runs from every fight, and Cassius cages him in place every time he catches it; the moment Cassius has to look elsewhere and Pip bolts in the middle of a crisis, the explosion that follows is worse than anything Bellatrix earns. Morgana Vess is the one he needs most at the critical moment, and the critical moment is exactly when she starts a speech; waiting through her monologue for a rune he asked for ten seconds ago is the closest he comes to actually strangling a teammate. He keeps a page in his notebook listing every rune name she invents, so that he can shout the right one. The notebook says a team keeps all its members, and Cassius follows it without exception.
Speech: Precise, formal, orderly; turns into shouting the instant Bellatrix is involved.
