[Notice Board] A long wall of boards in the castle's Floor 1 entrance hall, at the foot of the main stair, where every student passes on the way to class. Club recruitment, exam results, dorm rankings and competition brackets, posted publicly, alongside the student-run half: tutoring offers, study groups, festival posters, lost-item notes and small warnings. After results it is a crowd with elbows. Paid-work openings go within a day. Unofficial notices, dares and betting slips go up overnight and vanish by dawn. Lost items are listed here before they reach the Lost & Found counter at Reception.
Feel: layered paper, crowded.
