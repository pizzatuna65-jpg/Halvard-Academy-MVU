[Academy Bazaar]
When: Month 9 Week 2, Thursday to Saturday.
What: invited traders from the capital, the provinces and abroad fill the Main Courtyard; the academy takes a cut. Stock the mall never carries: foreign sweets and drink, charms, imported components, odd instruments, foreign fashion, items nobody can explain.
Prices: whatever the trader says; haggling expected; stalls take points. A year's savings can vanish in an afternoon.
Security: traders logged at the Gatehouse; the Doves watch the stock and the crowd.
