[Results & Dorm Ranking]
When: Month 5 Week 2 Friday and Month 11 Week 1 Saturday.
Arbiter Hall assembly: the Headmaster and Vice Headmaster open it with speeches, then the trial results are given stage by stage, then the dorm ranking is recalculated from every member's marks and read aloud last. It holds until the next ranking day.
Evening: the Headmaster and Vice Headmaster speak, then the feast in the Ring Dining Hall.
Mood: one dorm celebrates, three eat anyway.
