[Hanakaze — The Warrens]
The Warrens: Hanakaze's underbelly, a warren of old flood tunnels, sake cellars and smugglers' passages beneath the Hundred Alleys, older than the castle on the hill. Its people call themselves ronin, the masterless, a joke at the Alpha's expense. A black market (stolen goods, unregistered blades from smiths who will not sign them, forged Tournament Office records, contraband components), gambling dens that take bets on every challenge in the city, the Cellar Ring where fights are held that are anything but fair (no rules, no clerks, bets in cash), and brokers who sell information to anyone. Lantern-lit, crowded and polite in its own way: ronin do not rob guests who bow properly, and they never forget a face that brings the Alpha's Watch. Students are forbidden to go there. Buying or practising a forbidden art there is still arrest, as everywhere; a competitor caught there can be struck from the competition.
<narrator_only>The way in: behind the oldest noodle stall in the Hundred Alleys; the cook lifts the curtain at the back for anyone who orders "the bottom of the pot". A few alley regulars will point the way for coin; nobody else will. The Alpha's Watch raids the Warrens now and then (the engine decides when, in <now>), and the ronin usually hear it coming.</narrator_only>
