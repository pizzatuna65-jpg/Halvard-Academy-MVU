HANAKAZE INDEX — the capital of Yozakura, host of the World Competition this year. Each place has its own entry when it comes up.
A city of wooden streets, red gates and snow. Every street is lined with sakura, bare now and heavy with snow, and at the city's heart stands the Eternal Sakura, in full bloom all year, its petals falling onto the snow. Mount Hakuho rises white on the horizon. Everyone bows, and everyone competes.
Skyport: Hanakaze Skyport, Red Tower Arcade. Castle hill: the Hall of Victors (banquets), the Alpha's Residence (closed). Sakura Park: the Eternal Sakura, Snow Festival Park, the Golden Pavilion. Victors' Avenue: the Challenge Crossing, Thunder Gate Temple. Old quarter: the Hundred Alleys, the Glass Canal, Forge Street, the Mask Theatre. Hell Valley Springs. Shrine hill: the Fox Gates, the Cliff Temple. By the park: the Crowning Ring (the Grand Final) and the Thousand Lantern Inn (every team lodges here).
Beyond the city, by team carriage on match days only: the Bamboo Sea (Phase 1), Old Castle Shiroyama (Phase 2).
