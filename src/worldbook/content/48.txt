[Bell Tower] Marks class periods, meals, and curfew. Its pattern changes during The Thinning. The seven o'clock bell wakes the mana lanterns; the eight o'clock bell begins curfew. After it the lanterns drop to their night burn, each dormitory seals to its own students, and staff walk the courtyards for the first hour. Birds leave the roof just before it sounds. A bell-keeper looks after the bells and their ropes, and takes on a student for a paid shift now and then.
Home of the Thirteenth Stroke story.
Feel: booming, felt through the floor.
