[Broken Statue] A white marble statue standing in a stand of old trees between the Fire Dormitory and the Dovecote, off the path students take when they would rather not walk past the Dovecote's front. The figure has lost its head and one arm; the arm lies half-sunk in the moss at its feet, and moss and lichen have crept over the rest until the white shows only in patches. The inscription on its plinth has worn smooth. Who it depicted is unknown, and nobody on staff claims to know; students have theories and none of them agree. Groundskeeping leaves it exactly as it is. Light comes through the leaves in pieces, and the place is quieter than it should be for somewhere so close to the courtyard.
Feel: green shade, cold marble, moss, stillness.
<narrator_only>A hidden room lies beneath the statue. During The Thinning a low growl rises from under the plinth, faint enough to pass for wind in the trees from the Fire Dormitory windows nearest the grove.</narrator_only>
