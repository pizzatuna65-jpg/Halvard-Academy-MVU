Potion Crafting
Purpose: Give students a method that works without spending mana, and produce healers. Each dorm's approach differs by type.
Activity: Brewing to recipe, then independent formulation. Reagent identification and storage. Harvest-linked practicals. Failed batches disposed of under supervision.
Covers: the Potion Halls, reagents from the Gardens, healing draughts, and where the line to Poison magic sits.
