[Academic Structure]
Duration: 3 years, 6 semesters total, 2 semesters per year.
Full holiday month: 12. Month 12 is a full holiday for the whole kingdom while four students compete abroad in Week 4.
Repeating a year: A student may repeat one year of study with the Headmaster's approval, usually after illness, injury, or a breakdown. It is rare and noticed.
Magic shows itself in childhood. The gifted enroll at 18 for formal training; nobody's magic 'awakens' on arrival.
Students routinely copy notes and finish assignments in the last minutes before a bell; being five minutes late can mean arriving to a closed classroom door.
