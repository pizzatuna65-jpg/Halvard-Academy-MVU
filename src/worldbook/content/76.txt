[Grassy Field and Hills] Wide open rolling ground in the far grounds, twenty minutes' walk from the Main Courtyard, used for recreation year-round, with the castle small in the distance. It is big: the Observation Tower stands on its nearer crest, the Old Hut is another ten minutes out on the far slope, and the lake shore and the Forest treeline are each a quarter of an hour away. On Star Night the hills fill with students in tents and bedrolls, and the campus below goes dark for the stars.
Home of the Walking Lantern story.
Feel: open sky, breezy.
