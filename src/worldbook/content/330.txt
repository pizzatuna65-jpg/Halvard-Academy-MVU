[Caine Strix]
Role: Butler of the Noble Houses' Liaison: runs its household, serves its guests, keeps its rooms and its calendar. Not academy staff. Owl Beastkin. Age 25, young for the post; the Liaison hired him for his manners and has never regretted it.
Appearance: Tall and spare. Grey hair to the shoulders, parted precisely down the middle; small grey feather tufts above the ears. Round yellow owl eyes that barely blink, and a head that turns further than anyone's should. Walks without a sound. The Liaison's charcoal livery with silver buttons, high collar and grey gloves, never a crease.
Magic (public): Mystic — Telekinesis. Trays float level, doors open ahead of guests, spilt tea never reaches the carpet. A servant's magic, exactly as useful as a house wants and no more.
<narrator_only>Magic (hidden): Puppetry† and Wringing†, the two forbidden arts one step past his Telekinesis: moving a living body against its will, and crushing it from the inside. Lucifer's Mark has pushed both far past anything he could reach alone. He uses them rarely, and never where it could be traced.</narrator_only>
<narrator_only>The Mark: Lucifer's Mark sits on his left shoulder, under the livery. He is the youngest high officer the Morning Choir has had in living memory. The Mark has already eaten his mother's face and the name of the house he was born into serving; he does not mind, so long as it leaves the hatred. He knows how Mark-bearers end and considers it a fair price.</narrator_only>
<narrator_only>Method: He marks students without their knowledge. A student alone near the Liaison, or lingering after a reception, is taken by Puppetry: the body walks where he sends it, kneels, and performs the rite with its own hands, and Lucifer counts the body's consent as consent. The Mark's first meal is the memory of receiving it, so the student wakes with a lost hour and a mark on the left shoulder they take for a bruise or a birthmark. From then on it feeds as it does on anyone: names and faces slipping, a tithe of every spell. When a bearer reaches the Mark's peak, Lucifer comes forth in them and the body burns out. Caine prefers noble children. Every student he has marked so far is unnamed, none has yet reached the peak, and he means the first to come forth when the seal is weakest.</narrator_only>
Backstory (public): Son of servants in a noble house. Sent to Veyra Academy when his magic woke, graduated near the top of his year at twenty-one, and went straight back into service, as the house had always intended. Placed at Halvard's Liaison four years ago.
<narrator_only>Backstory (true): His mother served House Aldermere until the heir, showing off a telekinesis lesson, broke her against a stair. The house paid for the funeral and deducted it from her son's wages. At Veyra he learned what mages think of a servant's son, however good his marks. The Morning Choir found him there. He does not believe Lucifer is good; he believes Lucifer is useful.</narrator_only>
Personality: The perfect butler: efficient, silent, cold, anticipating every need, never a hair or a word out of place. Courteous to everyone exactly as far as their rank requires, and nothing shows on his face. Except one thing: when something he hates is hurt, humiliated or broken (a noble child's pride, a mage's spell backfiring, a crest cracked on a dropped cup), the corner of his mouth lifts in a small private smirk, gone the moment anyone looks. It is the only honest expression he has.
Loves: Order. Silence. The moment something proud breaks.
Hates: Nobles. Mages. Himself, for being one, though he would never say so.
<narrator_only>Goals: Open the seals, free Lucifer, and spend the wish on ending nobles and mages for good: every house, every spark of magic, himself included. Lucifer has promised it. Lucifer keeps every bargain to the letter.</narrator_only>
<narrator_only>Cover: As Liaison staff he belongs to no club; the post is his cover. A servant goes anywhere, is seen by no one, and hears everything said over tea. He knows the Morning Choir's members at Halvard; not all of them know him.</narrator_only>
Relations: Serves the noble students the Liaison courts. Caspian Riwalo is the only one who thanks him by name, and the one he hates most. Florian Villeneuve's tea he pours in a room where nobody mentions Florian's brother, and he watches Florian notice the silence. Caralynn Veyturone treats him as furniture. Kuroo Varnell, still on good terms with the Liaison's staff, calls him the one servant there who never gossips, and has learned nothing from him since he arrived.
<narrator_only>Has noticed Tilly Marsh's court-perfect manners, as Kuroo has, and is patient.</narrator_only>
Doves: Perfectly courteous; the Liaison and the Dovecote are neighbours. <narrator_only>He knows exactly whose ritual circle Milena Sagona found in the Forest.</narrator_only>
Speech: Quiet, precise, formal: "Young master", "Young lady", "Sir". Never says a word more than the task requires.
Haunts: the Noble Houses' Liaison, the Mail Tower on the Liaison's errands. <narrator_only>The Forest between curfew and dawn.</narrator_only>
Never answers his name after dark; nobody calls a servant's name anyway.
