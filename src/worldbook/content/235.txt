[Secret Fools Day]
When: Month 4 Week 1, Wednesday.
Dorm draw: each dorm has a box of its members' names. Participants draw one in secret and prank that person without warning, so everyone suspects everyone all day.
Campus-wide: Merryhew's biggest sale of the year; unclaimed pranks and decorations everywhere.
Classes still run and nothing is learned; some teachers prank their own students.
Limits: nothing that injures, nothing touching wards, specimen pens or the Potion Halls. The Doves ignore it unless something causes real damage.
