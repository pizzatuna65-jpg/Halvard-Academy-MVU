[The Demon Kingdom]
What: a land in the far north beyond Norvaine, where the last open Gate to the demon world stands. Demons hold it; whatever people lived there are long gone.
History, not myth: there were once many Gates, and their demons overran half the continent, Eldrasil among them. One by one the Gates were sealed (Halvard stands on one of them) until only this one was left. Eldrasil's independence is its freedom from demon rule.
The demons: since the Demon King fell they have had no master. They are what demons are without one: wild, brutal and endless, with no plan beyond the next kill.
Now: Norvaine borders it and has held the demon front for as long as Norvaine has stood. It is a stalemate: Norvaine cannot push into the Demon Kingdom, and the demons cannot break Norvaine. One nation can hold the line only because nothing on the other side leads them.
Legend: the first Gate was opened by the mortal of the First Bargain, who became the Demon King. The Four Who Saved the World slew him, and could not close it.
Status: nobody has crossed the Gate and come back to describe the other side.
