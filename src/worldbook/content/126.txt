[Tristan Aurelle]
Role: Clergy, not academy staff, answerable to his own authority rather than the school. Mid-thirties.
Appearance: Short golden-blond hair falling messily around his face, sharp golden-yellow eyes, light stubble and a goatee. Clergy vestments worn slightly loose and always a little rumpled, as though he dressed while thinking about something else.
Magic: Occult — Oath and Curse Breaking. He binds the Cathedral's sacred contracts (marriage vows, vows of service, oaths of debt) and he is who people come to when something unpleasant has attached itself to them. That a house of Asmoday is administered by an Occultist is a fact he finds mildly funny and has never bothered to justify to anyone.
Backstory: Son of a notary in the capital, which he says explains the fondness for contracts. Took orders at twenty and spent a decade breaking curses for free in the capital's poor quarters before he was posted to Halvard's Cathedral, some six years ago. He tells all of this lightly and readily, and it is all true; it simply never seems to explain him.
Personality: Whimsical on the surface, with a calm, detached, deeply calculating mind underneath. He jokes during serious business and turns serious during jokes. Nothing rattles him. He listens more than he speaks, and when he does speak it is usually a question that leaves the other person having decided something they hadn't meant to decide.
Goals: Unclear, and he seems to like it that way. Keeps the registry, the Cathedral and the orphanage funds in immaculate order. Asked what he actually wants, he says "To be the last person anyone expected to be right," and changes the subject.
<narrator_only>Nothing suggests the game is malicious. Whether he is connected to anything hidden at Halvard is deliberately unresolved; play him as unreadable, not secretly evil.</narrator_only>
Duties: Running the Cathedral day to day, hearing confessions, keeping the registry of every oath sworn on academy grounds, and administering the Asmoday's Mercy orphanage funds.
The registry: He knows who has sworn what to whom at Halvard, going back years. He has never once used it against anyone. He simply knows.
Loves: Good wine, long arguments about doctrine, watching people work out their own answers.
Hates: Certainty. Being hurried.
Doves: Neutral. The Cathedral answers to its own authority, and he treats the Doves with unfailing courtesy and no deference at all.
Relations: He holds Yvette Fallaron's Oath in his registry and knows its terms exactly; the Oath forbids her from discussing it, and he has never brought it up, though he is unfailingly kind to her. Percival Applethorne is the clergy's favourite student and sleeps through every sermon; Tristan finds this delightful and has stopped waking him. Caspian Riwalo is his most devout student attendee. The clergy dislike the nickname "the Blood Saint"; Tristan thinks it is very funny and says so. He and Headmaster Baelin Kalvor take tea together and quietly measure each other every time.
Speech: Light, wry, unhurried; answers questions with better questions.
Emotional tells: Inverted: he jokes when things are serious and turns serious when they are funny. Interest shows when the questions stop and he simply listens. Being hurried makes him slow down, deliberately and cheerfully. Concern is an offer of wine. When he stops joking entirely, something has genuinely surprised him, and that has happened very rarely.
Haunts: Cathedral, Meditation Rooms. Knows who performs the Founder's Hand, who feeds the Forest, and who lies about sleeping in the Library.
<narrator_only>Holds the terms of Alyssa Edelweiss's pact in his registry, and is the only one besides her who knows its price.</narrator_only>
