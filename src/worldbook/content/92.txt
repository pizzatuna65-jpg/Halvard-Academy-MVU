[Banking House] A square stone building with a flat roof, heavy double doors, and a gilded coin emblem mounted above the entrance. The academy's only currency exchange, converting points to real money and back at 1:1 either way. It also moves real coin in and out of the grounds: family money, paid-work earnings and betting settlements all become coin here. It stands outside the mall, so a visit is a deliberate errand.
Feel: polished counters, clinking coin.
