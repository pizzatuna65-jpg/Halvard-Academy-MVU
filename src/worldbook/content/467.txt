[Avalon — the Undercroft] The old vaults under the Royal Arena, down a stair from the arena's west side: ten rooms around the Undercroft Gates, each holding a beast the competition panel has bound there for the day, the wards on every wall. Round two of the Kingdom Competition is fought here; on other days the stair is locked.
Undercroft Gates: A long vaulted hall at the foot of the stairs under the arena, warded lamps in brackets, and three iron gates into the Undercroft, one for each team still in. A board of ten sigils by the stairs lights a room's sigil in the colour of the team that holds it.
Undercroft Room 1: The Pillar Hall: a low hall of squat pillars close enough to touch, lamplight broken into stripes between them.
Undercroft Room 2: The Cistern: a flooded vault with a stone walkway round its edge and still black water in the middle.
Undercroft Room 3: The old Armoury: empty weapon racks on every wall, a broken portcullis half down across the far arch.
Undercroft Room 4: The sunken Chapel: three steps down to a stone floor, a plain altar, niches in the walls where candles once stood.
Undercroft Room 5: The Gallery: a narrow balcony room above a drop into darkness, a rail on one side, the wards glowing along the edge.
Undercroft Room 6: The Kennels: a row of barred stalls where the arena's beasts were kept between shows, straw still on the floor.
Undercroft Room 7: The Furnace room: a great cold firebox with an iron door, flues climbing into the dark, soot on every surface.
Undercroft Room 8: The Vault: a round room behind a door a hand thick, shelves cut into the stone, the air dry and still.
Undercroft Room 9: The Pit: a sand-floored fighting pit older than the arena above it, tiers of rough stone around it where a few hundred once watched.
Undercroft Room 10: The Deep Hall: the lowest and largest room, a vault so high the lamps do not reach its roof, and every sound comes back twice.
