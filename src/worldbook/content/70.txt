[Mail Tower] The academy's only regular contact with outside: letters and parcels. The upper floors are the aviary where message birds are kept and flown; Creature Studies uses it for bird handling. A sorting floor below handles parcels, with numbered lockers and a tally token for collection; birds carry letters and light goods, heavy goods come slowly by cart. Sunday is the heaviest sorting day, and students on shifts learn everyone's family news first.
Feel: feathers, birdcall, cold stairwell.
