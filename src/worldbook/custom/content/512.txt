Students may buy alcohol and tobacco and receive them as gifts, but may drink or smoke only on the rest day (Sunday), during Mid-Year Break (M5 W4), and in Month 12. Festival nights do not count, and academy feasts serve students no alcohol. Staff enforce it like curfew: a student caught drinking or smoking on a class day loses points and gets a mark on their record. Barrow & Vine asks for the bracelet and says so at the counter.
