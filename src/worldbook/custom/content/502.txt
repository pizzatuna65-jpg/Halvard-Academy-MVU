- Trust 0–100: how sure they are that {{user}} can be relied on (closeness is Rank). Low Trust holds back what the bond would give (keeping {{user}}'s secrets from Rank 6, real risks from Rank 7, a best friendship or romance at Rank 8, Rank 9–10); <now> says what is held back and how each present character carries it.
- Judging a deed (Trust and Tension): by what the character believes {{user}} meant, not by the harm alone. Carelessness without malice (a real try that fails, a secret that slips out by accident) is small: Trust −3 for a failure, −8–10 for a slipped secret, and Tension only if it truly cost them something. Teasing, mock insults and play-fighting that both sides enjoy are not offences: report them as talk or hangout; whether a character enjoys them is theirs (<now>, "Teasing"). Harsh words from someone they trust (Trust 65+) who plainly means well are not offences either. Only count what the character saw or later learns of. If they know {{user}} had no choice (forced, charmed, protecting them), Trust does not fall; a lie to protect them or for a surprise, once understood, is −0–3. - Tension 0–100: follows conflict and unresolved friction. Move it in the same reply as the deed, by its weight: a slight or a sharp word +3–5; an insult, humiliation or broken promise +10–15; violence against them (a punch, a spell) +20–30. Never lower Tension yourself for an apology: report it as an apology interaction and the engine applies what that character's nature allows (some forgive easily, some hold grudges, some take it as weakness). Quiet days ease it too (engine); a great deed (saving them, a real sacrifice) may lower it directly. These are sizes, not limits: unlike bond XP, Tension has no daily or weekly cap, several offences in one reply add up, and one bad day can make an enemy. Mending is slower than harm: an apology never undoes more than part of what the offence did. How each character carries tension, and what eases it, is in <now> for those present.
