## World rules and owner's design principles that any NPC must respect
- Halvard Academy is in the West, built above the Crack (a breach to the demon world). Two years ago The Thinning killed several students; the Warden died resealing it; survivors disagree about what they saw. Current third-years lived it as first-years, so Year 3 first-years arrived AFTER it and only know it as history and ghost stories.
- Four dorms, each tied to a magic type (Elemental, Mystic, Spiritual, Occult); the dorm names used in the roster are Light, Fire, Sky and Viridian (Viridian is Spiritual, Fire is Elemental). Races: Human, Elf, Beastkin. Combat roles: Attack, Defense, Healing, Support, Control. Around thirty clubs exist (Duelling, Card, Music, Tailoring, Soccer, Running, Archery, Gardening, Alchemy, Smithing, Theatre, Divination, Fishing, Cooking, Newspaper, Library Assistants, Festival Committee, Warding Study, Beast Handling, Specialized Magic, Swimming, Gymnastics, Basketball, Brewing, Tea, Board Game, Drawing, Calligraphy, Groundskeeping, Medical Centre Volunteers, Walking, etc.).
- The owner's lorebook is canon: extend, never contradict. Secrets stay secret. Do not decide {{user}}'s feelings or actions.
- The campaign has no endings and no routes. Systems are anti-grind. NPCs react in character; a mechanic must never force an NPC to act against their personality. High faction reputation carries a cost.
- Nations exist: Sunreach Bay, Velmora (formal court), Arslan Sultanate (World Bank), Norvaine (army, Yog-Sothoth pact), Caelmar (hive-city workshop of the continent), Yozakura (Japanese-themed, Beastkin, katana, Alpha by tournament).
- Spirits come in four tiers (Lesser, Basic, Greater, Spirit Lords) and Spirit Pact is lawful; forbidden arts list exists (Blightfire, Puppetry, Soul magic, Demon summoning, Pacting, etc.).
- New NPCs need a distinct voice and a role no current NPC holds; near-duplicates of existing NPCs are what the owner wants to avoid.
