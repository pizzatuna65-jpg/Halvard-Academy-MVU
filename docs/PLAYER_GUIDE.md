# Eldrasil: Halvard Academy — Player Guide (card v1.2.2)

You are a first-year at Halvard, one of four magic academies in the Kingdom of Eldrasil. A year of classes, dorm rivalries, clubs, competitions and festivals, with something old and sealed underneath the castle. The card tracks the world for you: the clock and calendar, seasons and weather, your body and mana, money and belongings, relationships, secrets, and what the campus remembers.

## 1. What you need

- **SillyTavern 1.13.5 or newer**, using a **Chat Completion** API.
- **Tavern Helper** (JS-Slash-Runner) **4.8.4 or newer**, with this card's scripts allowed to run.
- **Prompt Template** extension (ST-Prompt-Template, EJS). The calendar, the "now" line and a few date-gated lore entries need it.
- Internet on first load: MVU, the portraits, the map and the connections diagram load from jsDelivr.

## 2. Installing

1. Import `Eldrasil_Halvard.png` as a character.
2. When SillyTavern asks whether to **import the embedded lorebook, say yes.** The card needs its own worldbook bound to it, including a small control entry that tells MVU how to run this card.
3. When Tavern Helper asks whether to allow the card's scripts, allow them (MVU, Schema, Engine, Eldrasil UI).
4. **Do not also enable the standalone Eldrasil lorebooks** (v37/v38/v39) in the same chat. The card already carries the merged, corrected version, and running both duplicates the lore.
5. Start a new chat. In the browser console you should see MVU report that the variable structure was registered.

## 3. Your first session

The greeting carries the **bracelet**, a status bar shown above the text of the latest message. On a fresh chat it offers **Create your student**, which opens the Student Builder. You can also use the **Builder** button under the chat box.

The Builder walks through seven steps: a sample to start from, identity, power, techniques, pacts, hidden magic, and a review. A few choices matter later:

- **Identity** has pronouns (a list, or your own), a **personality**, and an optional **birthday** in the academy calendar. The birthday goes on your calendar and the day plays out as your birthday. **Combat roles** are not chosen here: the five role teachers assign yours at your first Practical Magic class (the old Combat class), from how you fight. With two roles you attend the class you want most; now and then a teacher cannot come and the class becomes a free period.
- **Mana capacity** is a slider (50 to 1,000; type a number for more). It is capacity, not a rank: nobody can read it off you, so a big pool can live a quiet life. Overpowered is allowed; the world reacts to what you show, never by quietly weakening you.
- **Specialties**: the subtype lists are suggestions. Add your own subtype and say whether it is a forbidden art. A technique with a subtype of your own asks the same question.
- **Techniques** start with your specialty for their type as the subtype (change it freely). **Apply technique** folds a finished one into a single line; tap it to edit. They have fixed costs: per use, sustained (upkeep per in-world minute), or hybrid. The engine charges them; the narrator never guesses.
- **Pacts**: first choose what the pact is made with. Only a **spirit** pact is lawful; a pact with a demon, a monster (by bestiary grade), an animal or a person is the forbidden art of Pacting. Choose whether your partner is with you only while summoned or lives as the pact says. Then give your partner its **abilities**, like a creature's moves: each has a name, what it does and cannot do, and a strength (Light, Standard, Heavy, Ultimate) that sets its cost for the partner's tier; an ability can be a single move or something lasting with upkeep. A partner usually has 2 abilities at the lowest tier, 3, 4, and 6 at the highest (more is allowed, with a warning), and it can use nothing else. Press **Apply pact** and the Techniques page shows its Summon technique (which only pays for the partner being there), each ability as its own technique ("Ember: Flame Lash"), and, for the two highest tiers, Channel. An ability is only charged while the partner is with you (summoned, or living by the pact's terms).
- **Dominant type** is what the Arbiter Stone reads at the Entrance Event. It picks your dorm (Elemental → Fire, Mystic → Light, Spiritual → Viridian, Occult → Sky). The Stone cannot see subtypes, or whether your magic is forbidden.
- **Hidden magic** is optional: your **true magic, filled in like a technique** (it is charged like one), a cover story and how you conceal it. Who finds out is decided in the story. Using it where it can be noticed raises **Dove attention**; after a quiet week it falls by one.

Register, then write your first action. The bracelet hides Builder once your student is registered. To change your profile, affinity or techniques later, open **Student file** and choose **Edit student file** in its top-right corner. The Builder's entry in the chat is hidden, so the bracelet stays on the last story message and shows your new file there.

## 4. Playing

Write actions in the chat as usual. Each reply ends with a folded "📜 State update" block that you can open to see exactly what changed. The bracelet above the latest reply shows the clock, period, location, points, vitals, and chips for whatever matters right now. Chips can be tapped to open the matching panel.

**Panels** (bracelet buttons, or the buttons under the chat box):

- **Student file**: profile with your student's **picture** (Upload picture on the Overview; it is saved in SillyTavern's user images, so it shows on every device) and **reputation**, body (injuries, weather conditions and training), studies, magic, hidden magic (once relevant), **Gifts & perks** (once a bond has given you one), the engine log, and **Settings** (colour theme, features and their token cost, bonds, training and reputation).
- **People**: everyone you have met with their bond rank, dossiers that fill in as bonds deepen, and a connections diagram: everyone you have met around you, with the lines between them appearing as you learn them (someone's views on others open at bond Rank 5–6; family and teachers are common knowledge). Drag a face to rearrange, scroll or pinch to zoom.
- **Map**: the campus with clickable pins. Places you have not found stay hidden until you visit them. Each place card shows the walk from where you are now, access, clubs, regulars and connections; use ‹ › to step through the places at one pin. A regular is listed once you know them well enough to know where they hang out.
- **Inventory**: your **Bag**, your **wallet** (points, coin, recent transactions), planner and calendar, journal, notice board, letters, mystery board. Tabs appear once they become relevant. Tap a calendar day to see what is on it and write yourself a note ("ask Gareth to spar"); promises, appointments, project due dates, notices and your birthday land on their day by themselves. Notes are yours; the narrator does not read them.
- **Activities**: clubs, competition, shop, projects, trips. The **Shop** tab appears only at a shop (the Commissary, the Mall and the shops inside it); a "Shop here" chip on the bracelet opens it. Its lists are recommendations, not the whole stock. **Battle** opens from the "In battle" chip.

**Buttons never move the story on their own.** "Go here", "Buy", "Reply" and similar buttons write a suggested action into your chat box. Edit it if you like, then send it. This keeps every change in the chat, so swipes and deletions stay consistent.

## 5. How the world works

- **Time.** The clock follows what actually happens: a brief exchange may fit within the same displayed minute, while a sustained conversation or real activity takes longer. Map routes show ordinary walking estimates; a suitable speed technique can make the trip faster and still costs its listed mana. Classes follow the weekly timetable. Every event day has a plan with times (the Entrance Event: arrivals until 09:00, Headmaster and Vice Headmaster speeches before Stone sorting, Council vest handover and dorm seating until 10:00, the assigned two-newcomer/one-senior tour and club booths 10:00–18:00, the Entrance Feast at 19:00); tap the day in Notebook → Calendar to read it. Club booths stay up every afternoon after classes (16:00–18:00) through the first week, and club registration closes on Friday at 18:00. Curfew is 20:00 (later on some festival days: Training Week 21:00, Crowning Day and the Traveling Circus 22:00, the Harvest Festival's Friday and Saturday 23:00); on a trip or an off-grounds outing the teachers set the rules instead. The Thinning locks the academy down for a week in Month 7. Breaks empty the campus. Month 11 ends with the World Competition abroad (Week 4, Wed–Sat) and Graduation (Week 4, Sunday). The next morning, the first day of the Month 12 holiday, the third-years leave: they stop showing up as regulars and club members, though you keep their bonds and dossiers, and the story can keep one on (repeating a year, staying as staff). Each new school year everyone moves up a year, you included (the story can hold you back), everyone's age moves on with it, last year's competition record is cleared (your Journal keeps it), and a new class of first-years arrives; you are not sorted again at their Entrance Event: you are the senior guide for two roster first-years who tour the campus with you. On your birthday you turn a year older. From your second year the Journal groups its lines by year and month; newcomers written for a later year stay out of the story until their year begins. In your second year six new first-years arrive: Linus, Maple, Nerys, Hadrian, Wren and Tsubaki. Seven more arrive in your third year: Aurelian, William, Bruno, Bianca, Rigel, Topaz and Faye. All have portraits.
- **Magic on campus.** Safe everyday casting, including movement magic, is allowed. Doves intervene for restricted acts, harm or established suspicion. The **Magic Duelling Club** practises spell duels inside warded rings.
- **Reputation and reminders.** The narrator reports what actually happened (a staff job, a prize, a witnessed offence); the engine assigns the listed deed's reputation and XP. Buying listed stock without a matching payment, an injury reaching its expected recovery time, or being outside the dorm after curfew prompts a conditional reminder. These reminders never take points, heal an injury or impose a penalty by themselves.
- **Body.** HP measures injury, not an RPG life bar. Every loss comes with a named injury, and one blow can take at most 40 HP unless it is truly lethal. Stamina is exertion. Sleep and rest recover you, and nothing heals off-screen. A night skipped in one go counts as sleep unless you stay up on purpose.
- **Seasons change places.** Many places read differently through the year: sledding on the Hills in winter, the Old Hut snowed in, the pool steaming in the cold, the Canteen's seasonal menu. Each place's card on the Map shows what it is like this season.
- **The map** changes with the calendar: on some event nights (Star Night first) it shows the campus as it looks then, and more event and season maps can follow. Pins and places stay the same.
- **Mana.** Costs come from your technique list. Sustained effects drain mana per in-world minute until you end them, and end on their own if you run dry; a summoned partner costs its upkeep for as long as it is with you. If the narrator forgets to start or end an effect, the engine keeps the two halves in step and reminds it of an effect left running for hours.
- **Exams and grades.** Four exam weeks a year (Months 3, 5, 9, 11). Every subject gets a mark from A to F out of three things: preparation (study time in the Library, Study Rooms, Archive, study sessions with friends, assignments done on time), the proficiency the subject draws on, and how many of its classes you attended. Theory subjects (Magic Theory, History, Etiquette) lean on preparation, practicals on proficiency; how the exam day goes adds or takes a little. Attend every class of a subject and you get at least a C; sitting an exam Tired or Exhausted lowers it. **Student file → Studies** shows the mark you can expect now and the sum behind it. Midterm marks arrive on the Friday of exam week, final marks on Results day. An A pays for tutoring (two sessions a week) and helps risky attempts in that subject's field; an A or B makes that subject's teacher warm to you faster; an F brings a remedial session the next Saturday, and attending it turns the F into a D. Your marks set your dorm rank and your Academy standing.
- **Money.** Points live on the bracelet. A monthly payout depends on your dorm rank, which is recalculated from your marks in Months 5 and 11. Coin is real money and the only kind that works off campus.
- **Bonds.** Time together, gifts and genuine help fill a bond XP bar, within daily and weekly limits. Once the bar is full and the cooldown and Trust/Tension requirements pass, the next genuine encounter deepens the bond by one rank. There is no required rank-up scene. **Student file → Settings → Bonds** controls the pace and the earliest official romance rank (8 by default). At Rank 8 the lived relationship becomes a friendship, romance or rivalry as canon allows. NPCs reveal more in their dossiers as the relationship deepens; secrets still require events in the story. Rank 2 adds +1 XP to the first accepted hangout you invited that week. Rank 4 allows shared mana training, stamina training or study according to the NPC-specific categories shown in People; active shared work receives a modest bonus. Rank 6 lets an NPC introduce a new roster NPC whose bond begins at Rank 1; narrator-created Extras do not get bonds. Rank 7 permits an accompanied dorm visit or an unscheduled visit to staff at work when practical. At Rank 9 a present witness may publicly vouch for you once a month, reducing one related reputation loss by up to 5 XP.
- **Bond rewards.** At Rank 5 the person may hand you their unique gift in the story; it appears in **Student file → Gifts & perks** only after that handover. Rank 10 brings their unique benefit. One-use benefits show their remaining uses. There are no endings or routes. Some bonds pause at Rank 8 until you uncover what the person hides. Krieg grows by his own weekly rule while the Doves think well of you. Rival academy teams and narrator-created Extras have no bond system.
- **Tension.** Conflict raises a bond's Tension (a sharp word a little, an insult more, violence a lot); quiet days bring it down, and so can an apology, depending on who you apologise to: the gentle forgive quickly, the proud want it said in public, fighters want a fight instead, staff accept it formally, and the dangerous hold grudges (Sophia even takes an apology as cowardice). From 40 time together counts for half, from 70 rank progression pauses and your reputation suffers, from 90 the bond stops growing, and at 100 it drops a rank. How people show it depends on who they are: the gentle ones withdraw, the popular ones turn the room against you, fighters challenge you (and a fair fight clears the air), staff use the rules, and a few dangerous people use everything they have. A handful are their own case. The dossier shows the tension band and what it is doing to the bond.
- **Proficiency, Practice and crafting.** Six proficiencies (Knowledge, Arcana, Craft, Prowess, Society, Nature), Rank 0 to 5, grow from classes, exams, daily life and **Activities → Practice** at places you have found (a weekly limit keeps it steady). Each rank helps Fortune rolls in its field. Craft recipes (potions, runes, cooking, needlework, drawing, carving, calligraphy, smithing) show their result before you make them; a Project of a craft kind finished at Craft Rank 4 becomes a masterwork. Rank 5 opens a mastery perk, listed under each proficiency in **Studies**.
- **Reputation.** Three standings from −5 to +5: **Academy** (staff and the institution), **Student** (the student body) and **Doves** (the Dovecote). Everyday good conduct counts a little each week (only so much a week), exams, competitions and crises count more, and trouble always costs. What you do for one side can cost you with another: reporting a classmate pleases the staff or the Doves and the students never forget it. Bonds count too: a bond reaching Rank 5 or 10 raises the matching standing, and a public falling-out costs it (a bond's Tension reaching 70 costs a little, reaching 100 costs more): staff move Academy, students move Student, Milena moves Doves. Each level changes how people treat you (Student file → Overview says how), and a high or low standing makes some people warm to you faster or slower. Academy +5 hangs your portrait in the Portrait Hall and adds a monthly bonus; Academy −5 is probation. Doves reputation is not the Doves' suspicion of hidden magic; that stays separate.
- **Training.** Drill to grow your **mana pool** or your **stamina** (tell the story you are training). Each session adds a little of your starting value; there is a weekly limit and a lifetime limit of twice your starting value, which a steady student reaches in about a school year. Training with a partner who gave you a training gift (Saffi or Percival for stamina, Sophia or Gareth for mana) counts half as much again, and two partners count double; it saves sessions, never raises a limit. Student file → Body shows how far you have come. Your maximum mana and stamina change only through training or the Student Builder. **Student file → Settings → Training** changes the gain per session, the weekly limit and the lifetime limit (each can be "No limit"); **Settings → Reputation** changes the pace, the weekly limit for everyday deeds, the bond milestones and the tension penalty. A dot marks the standard value.
- **Campus happenings.** Some days bring a small happening: a paid-work posting, a Dove sweep, a loose creature, a duel challenge. It shows as a chip and in the planner. Go if you want; the narrator may weave it in or ignore it. It is seeded, so a swipe shows the same happening for that day. Change the frequency (Off / Rare / Normal / Often) in **Student file → Settings → Features**. Outdoor happenings stay away on days of heavy weather, and some only turn up in their weather (a snow-clearing crew, a storm clean-up, doubled Dove patrols in dense fog).
- **NPC initiative.** From bond Rank 3, a person may try to reach you on some days in a way that fits their character. The engine picks at most one person a day and two days a week, and waits at least seven days before picking the same person again. This is an opportunity in the narrator's prompt, not an automatic arrival or a required scene. High Tension changes its tone; trips, lockdown and a person's absence can prevent it. Turn it off in **Student file → Settings → Features** if you prefer to initiate every meeting yourself.
- **Classes.** During a class period the students are in class, so the library at 09:00 on a Monday is nearly empty; the map shows "(in class until 10:00)" next to students. Go to your own class and the narrator knows which class it is, who teaches it (if the lore names the teacher) and who your classmates are. Saturday afternoon everyone is at their club. You may join the Student Council separately from a club: it meets Saturday 10:00–12:00 in the Chamber instead of Study Hall; a required F remedial takes priority.
- **Seasons and weather.** Real northern seasons: winter in Months 12, 1 and 2, spring 3–5, summer 6–8, autumn 9–11. The sky is rolled for the morning, afternoon and night, follows on from the previous block, and has wind and the occasional rare event (a lightning storm, hail, a blizzard, dense fog, a heatwave). Temperatures are in °C. The bracelet shows a weather chip; hover it for the full line.
- **The forecast.** Every morning at 07:00 the Divination Society posts tomorrow's skies on the **Notice Board** (Inventory → Notice Board). It is right about three times in four. The bracelet does not show it: you have to check the board.
- **Weather effects** (setting Full, the default): stay out in the rain without cover and you get **Soaked** (rest and sleep restore half the stamina); long in the cold, or soaked in the cold, you get **Chilled**; an hour in the summer sun can leave you **Overheated** (both cost stamina outdoors). An hour indoors (half an hour for heat) clears them, and so do a hot bath, a hot drink, dry clothes or a cold drink in the story. A soaking may turn into a **Head cold** the next day (stamina capped at 80% for two to four days, or until the Medical Centre treats it), more likely during the winter cold that goes round campus. Rain on the roof makes for good sleep, a storm or a hot night for poor sleep. Bonds grow a little faster when you are stuck indoors together in the rain, or out in fine mild weather. Fog and heavy weather hide hidden magic from the Doves a bit better. Outdoor people move indoors in the rain, and outdoor clubs practise in the Gymnasium (the Fishing Club goes out anyway). Weather never costs HP by itself. Outdoors, strong wind changes mana costs by your magic subtype: it feeds some, scatters others and leaves some untouched. Custom magic has an **In the wind** choice in the Builder; a barrier that blocks wind shelters your casting. Gale exposure and wind chill make cold weather harsher.
- **Mood and sleep.** People have Good, Normal or Bad days influenced by their character, weather, the hour, events and health. From Rank 4 their mood appears on the cast strip. With weather effects set to Full, mood affects conversations, apologies and conflict. Tired training gives half the usual gain; Exhausted or Collapsed stops training, and injuries can limit it too. Staying awake between midnight and 06:00 lowers your stamina ceiling; six consecutive hours of sleep clears the debt. The Vigil postpones it for 48 hours, then recovery takes two full nights. Flavor keeps narration without these numeric weather penalties; Off parks them.
- **Gifts and birthdays.** Gifts have five reactions: loved, liked, neutral, disliked and hated. Your dossier records the latest items you have tried. Birthdays appear in dossiers and the calendar once you know the person well enough, or learn the date in the story. The first birthday gift earns double XP outside the weekly gift limit; disliked or hated gifts cause twice the usual tension that day. Remembering the birthday builds Trust; forgetting it has no penalty. Close friends can arrange your evening birthday party. Seven additional shops share the Mall map pin. Students may consume alcohol and tobacco only on Sundays, during Mid-Year Break or in Month 12.
- **Gear.** Weather gear works from your Bag by name: an **umbrella** keeps you dry unless it is windy; an **oilskin rain cloak** always does; a **wool scarf and mittens** or a **lined winter cloak** keep the cold off; a **straw sun hat** helps in summer heat. The shop sells them in their season; out-of-season items are greyed out.
- **The Bag.** The first tab of **Inventory**. Things you buy, receive or find and keep go into the Bag, with a plan if you give one ("a Star Cookie for tonight"). The buttons Use, Give and Drop only draft the action. Sunfizz comes in returnable bottles: return empties at the Commissary or the Mall store for 2 points each.
- **Gossip.** Rumours spread: first only the witnesses know, then the source's dorm and club, then most of campus, then it is old news; some fizzle out after a couple of days, some fly round the campus at once. The Journal shows how far each one has got; rumours that died out stay there, faded.

## 6. Settings, themes, features and token cost

**Updating the card.** The card installs its own lorebook when it is missing, and when SillyTavern still holds an older version it asks whether to update it (that replaces the lorebook; start a new chat afterwards). You can also do it by hand: character panel → More… → **Import Card Lore**. Tavern Helper scripts must be allowed for this character. If you forget, the first reply of a new chat warns you ("Old lorebook"). If the lorebook is not attached at all, the bracelet says so instead of syncing, with a button that runs Import Card Lore for you.

**People the narrator invents.** A shopkeeper or a senior the story makes up gets a short record the second time they appear, so they keep the same look, manner and voice. The record holds 20; the ones you have not seen for longest make room. In the bracelet's row of people present, tap an invented character to **keep** them (a pin shows before the name) so they never leave the record; tap again to let them go.

The bracelet shows what comes **next** today: the next class, the next item of an event's plan ("Next 09:00 · Sorting at the Arbiter Stone in the Arbiter Hall") or curfew.

**Student file → Settings → Appearance** changes the colours of the panels and the bracelet: Pewter (the default), Midnight (darker), Rosewood (warm dark), Parchment (light) and Frost (light, cool). The choice is kept in this browser and is not written to the chat.


**Student file → Settings → Features** lists every module you can switch off: planner, story notices, letters, mystery board, competition, projects, trips, battle tracking, the Bag, campus happenings (Off / Rare / Normal / Often), NPC initiative, weather (Off / Flavor / Full), class-aware presence, story hooks, gossip, the Fortune roll, attendance, the weekly recap, small scenes on ordinary days, study and exams, **ambience** (Off / Texture / Full: how crowded and lit a place is, a sensory detail when you arrive, small moments around you and, very rarely, one of Halvard's ghost stories), and **report reminders** (when a reply seems to have left something out of its update, such as an injury without a name, a spell that was not charged or a conversation that was not counted, the narrator is reminded once; a reminder never changes anything by itself). Each row shows roughly how many tokens its rules and its current state add to every request, and the footer adds everything up, with what the switched-off features save. The preset and the chat history come on top.

Switching a feature off removes it from the narrator's state and rules and hides its tabs and chips. Nothing is deleted: its state is set aside and comes back when you switch it on again. Weather **Flavor** keeps the sky and temperature but drops the effects; **Off** removes weather entirely. Turning off the four new features (weather, class presence, hooks, gossip) and the Bag brings the prompt back to about the size of v1.0.3.

**Dialogue colours** (1.37.0): every named character speaks in a colour, and two people in the same scene never share one, so you can tell who is talking at a glance. A character keeps their colour from scene to scene; if they walk into a scene where someone already has it, they switch to a free one and keep that. Strangers and passers-by share one grey until the story gives them a record (Extras), and then they get their own. Your own lines have a soft colour too. The colours follow your SillyTavern theme (light or dark). Switch it off in Features if you prefer plain text.

The **story hooks** are the narrator's private notes on promises and planted details it owes you a payoff for. They never appear in your panels; that is on purpose.

## 7. Memory

To keep long campaigns affordable, the narrator reads only the **latest ~24 messages**; older ones reach it as `[…]`. What it remembers of the rest is the game state: the Journal (latest 30 turning points), the latest 10 known facts per bond, milestones, campus news and rumours, commitments, letters and clues. Older journal lines and facts leave the narrator's view but stay visible to you in the Notebook and dossiers, faded or listed first. That archive is large but not endless: the latest 300 journal lines and 40 older facts per bond. Secrets you have uncovered are kept for good, so a dossier never locks again.

To send more chat, open the Regex extension and raise the **Min Depth** of "Eldrasil — State-as-memory: trim far chat (prompt)", or disable it to send everything.

**Memory extensions.** VectFox works with this card as chat memory. Do not vectorize the card's own lorebook for semantic World Info, and use either VectFox ghosting or the card's trim regex, not both. Settings and ready-made cleaning patterns: `docs/ECOSYSTEM.md` (VectFox section) and `docs/vectfox_cleaning_patterns.json`. A compatible Gemini edit of the Realistic Frankenstein 2.2 preset and how the three fit together: `docs/ECOSYSTEM.md`.

**Preset toggles that change how you play (Eldrasil preset, 1.5.0).** "🎮 Player Input Authority" is ON: you write what your student thinks, says and tries, and the world decides how others react and whether it works ("she agrees" in your message is a wish, not a fact). Turn it OFF if you want to direct the story yourself. "🌐 Output language" is OFF; turn it ON and change the language name in it to read the story in another language.

## 8. MVU mode

The card asks MVU to update variables **together with the AI's reply**, one request per turn. You can switch to MVU's "extra model analysis" mode in the MVU panel under the current card's config; the card's entries are tagged for both modes. In the tool-call response format, names mentioned only in story prose will not unlock early in dossiers, because the update then arrives without the prose.

## 9. Troubleshooting

- **No bracelet under replies.** Tavern Helper is disabled, or the card's scripts were not allowed. Check Tavern Helper → character scripts.
- **The reply shows a raw `<UpdateVariable>` block, or nothing changes.** MVU did not load (first load needs internet), or the model broke the patch format. Swipe once. Persistent errors appear in the browser console as `[Eldrasil engine]` or MVU messages.
- **`<%` code visible in prompts, or the calendar is wrong.** The Prompt Template (EJS) extension is missing or off.
- **Portraits or map missing.** jsDelivr is blocked or slow on your network. The card still plays; only images are affected.
- **Duplicate lore, contradictions.** A standalone Eldrasil lorebook is enabled alongside the card. Disable it.
- **The MVU panel shows no card override.** The embedded lorebook was not imported or bound. Re-import the card and accept the lorebook prompt, or bind the "Eldrasil — Halvard Academy" world to the character.
- **Time went backwards, a rank reverted, damage was capped.** That is the engine enforcing the rules; the Student file → Log tab says what it did and why.
