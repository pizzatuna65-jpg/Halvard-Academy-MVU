Don't list obvious defaults as open `[?]` decisions (e.g. "holiday = Sunday + Mid-Year Break + Month 12" for the student
alcohol rule). Pick the sensible default, write it into the draft as decided, and mention it in at most one line. Keep `[?]` for real
design or canon choices where the owner's answer changes what gets built.

**Why:** On 2026-09-27, while the owner was reviewing the gift/birthday draft (FP6), they called this "being pedantic about obvious
stuff". Too many trivial questions made them re-read and ask what I meant.

Same session, the owner said it again: they asked "is Saturday a day off in Halvard?" and I answered with a table and caveats.
A simple question gets a one or two sentence answer.

2026-09-28, a third time: after I ended a batch draft with nine `[?]` questions, the owner answered "most of your questions are
just yes / follow your suggestion; ask only important ones, don't be pedantic". Default now: build with my suggestion and list it
as decided; a draft should end with at most 1–3 questions that genuinely change what gets built (canon, balance that is hard to undo).

2026-10-01, a fourth time and sharper ("why are you so insistent? … don't waste my time"): I re-asked three open questions
after the owner answered only other points. **Silence = approval**: anything the owner does not comment on is accepted as drafted.
Never repeat an unanswered question; in per-NPC drafting rounds, ask nothing unless it truly blocks, and move straight on.

2026-10-01, a fifth time (cohort 3 drafting): I carried the same "#2 → Beastkin?" question through three rounds. The owner answered
"read the memory" and had CLAUDE.md step 0 added: read every memory file before starting. An unanswered suggestion is adopted as drafted.

2026-10-21, a sixth time ("kamu gk baca memori?"): after "proceed" on the event-frame draft I kept writing "Founder Day's prize needs
the owner's fact" in HANDOFF and asked again when listing the remaining releases. "Proceed" with an open `[?]` adopts my recommendation;
when it needs content (a fact, a text), I write that content into the draft myself as decided and note the owner may replace it.
Never carry an open question into HANDOFF, a status line or a later answer.

**How to apply:** When drafting (`planning/DRAFT_*.md`) or asking in chat, filter questions first: if a default follows from the lore
or the engine, state it and move on.
