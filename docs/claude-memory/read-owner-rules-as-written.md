When the owner gives a design rule, quote and test it as written before calling anything a gap. Do not restate it with words they
did not use, and never present a problem my own paraphrase created as a hole in their design.

**Why:** 2026-10-21, Kingdom Competition design. The owner wrote "tim yg berhasil menguasai 4 room menang" (10 rooms, 3 teams, 1 out).
I restated it as "the first team to 4 rooms wins", listed "only one finalist" as a gap and patched it. Their rule needed no patch:
10 rooms let at most two teams hold 4 at once. The owner asked: "jadi kamu bikin celah sendiri lalu tambal sendiri?"

**How to apply:** before a gap table, check each gap against the owner's exact sentence and its numbers (their numbers are usually
deliberate: 3 flowers for 4 teams, 10 rooms for two holders of 4). A real gap is something the rule does not cover; small edge cases
I decide quietly in the draft, per [[no-obvious-open-questions]].
