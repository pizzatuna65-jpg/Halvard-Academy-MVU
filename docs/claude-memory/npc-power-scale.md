Owner, 2026-10-22: my World Competition aces (Akane, Kamran, Fia) were "too conservative / grounded" for a competition of the best
mages in the world. The owner's own standouts (Bellatrix, Lucius, Elion, Vesna, Lucrezia, Cyprien) all share: one sharp contrast in
character, a power that is frightening in scale with clear rules, one hard, exploitable weakness (not a soft one like "mood" or
"runs out of parts"), a tie to deep lore (Spirit Lords, nation secrets, institutional cruelty) and an unforgettable visual.

**Why:** I designed upward from team role and national flavour (Caelmar = workshop, so the ace is a tinkerer) and over-balanced;
the result was likeable support characters, not stars players fear.

**How to apply:** when designing or completing a standout NPC, start from the most unexpected person for that place and scale the
power to "nightmare against mages", then give it one precise counter. Keep likeable-but-modest designs for rough members.
