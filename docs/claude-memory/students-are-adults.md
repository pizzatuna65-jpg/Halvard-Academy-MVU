Halvard students are 18 when they enter, so every student is an adult (owner, 2026-10-03). Tsubaki is 17 only in Year 1, while she
is a Royal Mage and not yet a student. She arrives as a first-year in Year 2.

**Why:** the owner removed a narrator line from the Sunreach index. On the commit I attached an unverified warning that "many
Halvard students are minors". It implied the card enabled sexual content with minors. The warning cited Tsubaki wrongly, and an
earlier draft had invented an "under age" rule. The owner was offended; the sudden tonal shift ruined their mood.

**How to apply:**
- Treat students as adults.
- Never make an age-based or content-safety claim about the card without first checking the canon (lore ages, `npcs.json`).
- When an owner edit removes a line I wrote, commit it without commentary unless it breaks functionality or an established decision.
- If a real concern ever remains after checking, say it in one neutral sentence as a question: no alarm, no lists, no implied accusation.
